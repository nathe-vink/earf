# Hemisphere and Core: the facts the site lacks (2026-11-20)

One line each, for the product pages. Where a figure is computed rather than measured, it says so. Sources: the CAD builds
(`spinoffs/barkhorn/companions/out-dome-pendant/`, `out-orb-table/`), their parts lists (`bom.md`), `PARTICULARS.md`,
and the photometry of the mix (`renders/2026-11-10/barkhorn/lamp-site/final/photometry.txt`). Some lines in
PARTICULARS.md predate the current design (Hemisphere's COB, Core's 120 × 30 black base); where they differ, the
current CAD and parts list win, and that is noted below.

## Hemisphere

| | |
|---|---|
| Height of the shade | 100 mm (3.9 in) from the rim to the top of the shade; 126 mm (5.0 in) from the rim to the top of the cap. |
| Weight | About 1.6 kg (3.5 lb) hanging: the shade and its fitting. The canopy, with its driver, about 0.4 kg (0.9 lb). Computed from the CAD's volumes; the driver's weight is estimated. |
| Watts | About 7 W at the wall: about 5.9 W at the LEDs (24 LEDs in two strings, about 33 V and 180 mA), plus the driver's loss (estimated). Computed, not measured. |
| Dimmer type | Phase-cut, forward (TRIAC) or reverse (ELV), at 120 V, or 0-10 V; 1 to 100 %. The driver in the canopy, ERP Power ESS010W-0180-42. Not yet bench-tested with the ring board. |
| Cord supplied | 3 m (about 10 ft) of black textile-braided cord, cut to the drop at install. |
| Longest drop | Not decided. The 3 m cord is the upper bound, less what the canopy and cap take up; with the steel suspension cable option (2 m as listed), 2 m. |
| Lux on a table | To come: being measured at the standard 600 mm drop under a 2.44 m ceiling (the table 0.914 m below the rim). The photometry of the mix gives about 340 lux on a table 0.5 m below the rim (336 to 347 across the tints). |
| "About two thirds of its light comes back down" | It does not hold in every tint. The share going down is 56 % (Fog) to 76 % (Haze): Ember 70, Dust 65, Acid 64, Poison 73, Ice 74, Haze 76, Fog 56, Smoke 75. Suggested line: "Most of its light comes back down: about two thirds to three quarters, a little over half in Fog." |

## Core

| | |
|---|---|
| Dock and charger included | Yes. The box holds the lamp, its dock, a 2 m braided USB-C cable and a 20 W USB-C wall adapter; the cells ship in the box beside the lamp. |
| Cell size | Three LiFePO4 18650 cells (Power Sonic PSL-FP-IFR18650EC: 3.2 V, 1.5 Ah, 4.8 Wh each; 14.4 Wh in all), button-top, user-replaceable behind a hatch under the base. The button-top version is still to confirm with the maker. |
| Where the touch ring is | At the top of the base, just under the ball, 30 to 37 mm above the base's foot. Since the base became the lamp's own color (2026-11-11), the ring is under the tinted skin, so you touch the base's rounded top edge. It is no longer a visible black ring. |
| IP44 | A design target, not tested. Its basis: O-rings at the window, the chassis and the hatch, and a sealed USB-C receptacle. |
| Height (for the drawing) | 187 mm (7.4 in), on its dock or off it: the base's skirt reaches the table and the dock sits inside it. The "180 tall" in PARTICULARS.md is the older 120 × 30 base. Ball 160 mm (6.3 in); base 150 mm (5.9 in) across at the table. |
| Weight | About 1.4 kg (3.1 lb) for the lamp with its cells; the dock about 0.12 kg. Computed from the CAD's volumes; PARTICULARS.md's 1.46 kg was the older base. |
