# Mantle deck loading: 1× or 0.1× (2026-11-24)

`mantle-loading-sheet.jpg` has three rows:

1. the eight approved Tropos fronts;
2. the Mantle deck at 1× of the speakers' cabinets' dye loading;
3. the deck at 0.1× of that loading.

In both deck rows the top and underside are polished, the outer edge is satin, and there is nothing white. Each frame is 400 × 500 at 64 samples, lit with the physical model on the house ground. The 1× frames are in `../tests-2026-11-24-1x/`; the 0.1× frames, with their audit and cast files, are here.

The deck's top face and front edge band, C* / hue:

| Tint | 1× top | 1× edge | 0.1× top | 0.1× edge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ember | 7 / 39 | 3 / 86 | 12 / 27 | 45 / 34 |
| Poison | 5 / 124 | 3 / 106 | 12 / 143 | 41 / 141 |
| Dust | 19 / 47 | 50 / 37 | 10 / 78 | 48 / 55 |
| Acid | 20 / 101 | 26 / 89 | 11 / 103 | 48 / 101 |
| Ice | 12 / 270 | 42 / 300 | 3 / 220 | 30 / 266 |
| Haze | 12 / 300 | 7 / 294 | 8 / 324 | 54 / 311 |
| Fog | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| Smoke | 2 | 3 | 3 | 3 |

At 1× the deck reads as deep tinted glass: the dark tints go nearly black at the edge, while Dust, Acid and Ice keep colour. At 0.1× every edge glows (C* 30–54), the top is paler, and Smoke turns from dark to light grey glass.

Audits on the 0.1× frames: no emitting material. HIDDENCAST and CASTCHECK read 0.0% for all seven house lamps, with no linking.
