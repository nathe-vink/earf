# Round 1: six proofs, two blind critics (fresh subagents; images under neutral names, in different orders)

Proofs: `../proofs/r1-<direction>-<palette>.png` (960 x 540, 32 samples). Keys: `r1-critic-A.key.txt`, `r1-critic-B.key.txt`.

| proof | critic A | critic B | mean |
|---|---|---|---|
| b-p1 golden hour, all Poison | 6 | 6 | 6.0 |
| b-p2 golden hour, Ice hi-fi + Dust lamps | 6 | 6 | 6.0 |
| a-p1 morning, all Poison | 5 | 5 | 5.0 |

| c-p1 blue hour, all Poison | 4 | 5 | 4.5 |
| c-p2 blue hour, Ice + Dust | 4 | 4 | 4.0 |

Both critics: the orange (Dust) lamps beside blue (Ice) hi-fi read as "a two-colour accessory set", "borrowed from another
set"; all Poison reads "as one family more clearly than any other". Morning: flat, "empty showroom". Evening: muddy, the
speakers sink into the wall, Mantle's edge glow "reads like a gaming rig" at this exposure.

Common faults: a dead-square frontal camera at standing height (a catalogue line-up); the window's shadow band across the
upper wall where the header goes; the rug a flat card; a sparse room; the products small in frame.

Best fixes, verbatim in substance:
- A: lower the camera to seated eye height, swing it 15-20 degrees off axis so the sun comes through the acrylic toward
  the lens; the window-frame shadow on the floor and lower wall, not the upper wall; lamps in the speakers' tint.
- B: lower (1.0-1.1 m), closer, a longer lens, a gentle three-quarter view; the hi-fi about half the frame height; the
  empty floor cropped; the upper third of the wall kept clear.

Decision: direction B (golden hour), palette P1 (all four in one tint, Poison). Round 2 re-lays the room.
