# horn-plan-b: Plan B beside the formed horn (2026-10-31)

`make/plan-b.md`'s recommended Plan B: the horn laminated from 14 layers of the horn lot's 20 mm sheet plus the boss,
each layer CNC-cut 10 mm outside the air path, bonded, and the air path machined through. It is drawn in
`horn_options.py`. Here it is rendered beside the formed horn in studio31's horn macro rig, in Ember, at proof size
(800 x 800 each, 64 samples). The camera, the light and the cabinet are the same in both.

- `compare-formed-vs-laminated.png`: the formed horn (left) and the laminated horn (right).
- `proofs/`: each frame, with its shot and report.

What it shows: the laminated horn's 20 mm layers read through the deep tint as contour rings, from the mouth down to
the throat. The thick walls make it deeper and heavier-looking than the formed shell. It stands on the same pod, cap,
posts and cabinet. Its outside is left stepped; machined smooth, the rings would still show through, but softer.
