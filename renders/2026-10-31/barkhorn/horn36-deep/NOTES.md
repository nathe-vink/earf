# horn36-deep: the formed horn in its heavier lot, against the site (2026-10-31)

The owner, 2026-10-31, shown `renders/2026-10-30/barkhorn/horn36-site-check/compare-site-vs-horn36.jpg`: "Deep, like
the site." The site's pictures stay as they are, and the drawn horn is made to match them. In each colour the horn's

cabinet's dye, Ember about 10.

| the site (resin horn) | #C85C5E | 0.288 |

By eye the flare now reads as the site's horn: deep and nearly opaque red. One difference stays, and it is the shape,
not the dye. The site's horn has a dark wedge round the throat, its solid resin neck. Round 36 removed that neck on
purpose, so the formed horn's dark neck is the slim throat piece and the pod, and it reads smaller. The mean colour is
a touch redder (226 against 201 in red) and the luminance about 5 % lower: within what a real chip will move.
