Mantle's cover: how it lets light through, and how that was checked (2026-11-15).

THE FAULT. The first covers drew the 3 mm sheet as glass (the deck's own refracting material). This rig renders with
refractive caustics OFF, so no direct light passed the cover: everything under it was lit by stray bounces only (the
record label at 0.05-0.10 of its brightness without the cover).

THE MODEL NOW (v3_day.py cover(), commits e5c9e1ca, 25eafd46, 77fd6301). Each face of the cover is a THIN SHEET:
Fresnel reflection (IOR 1.49, glossy 0.004) over a straight-through Transparent BSDF. It REPLACES refraction, it does
not add to it: caustics stay off in these frames, and only the Transparent path carries light. The tint per face is
T ** (0.5 / cos theta_t), T the 3 mm wall's transmission from the deck's own dye (sigma = density x (1 - color) of the
deck's Volume Absorption) and theta_t the ray's refracted angle (a slanted ray travels thickness / cos theta_t). The
inside face uses IOR 1/1.49, which Cycles inverts back to air-to-glass (a flat sheet has no total internal reflection).

THE CLAMP. This rig sets sample_clamp_indirect = 8. Light that reaches a surface through refracting glass is indirect
in Cycles, so a plain-refraction render in this rig is clamped dark: a refraction reference must be run unclamped
(v3_day.py --cover glass does). Clamped, the reference read Fog 26.7, Ember 10.3; unclamped 77.4 and 29.6 (below).

THE CHECK (the coordinator's method). One cell (the record label and a patch of deck top), 1000-2000 samples, no
denoiser, no adaptive sampling, the mean of 20 x 20 px, linear luminance x 255:
                 bare    flat sheet   flat + slant   glass ref (unclamped, under-converged)
  Fog label      96.4    89.7         89.6           77.4
  Ember label    96.4    43.7         41.4           29.6
The reflection measured on black record beside the label: Fog 8.2, Ember 5.9. Ray-cast paths: the camera sees the
label through the FRONT WALL at 34 deg; the light comes in at 13 deg (bg), 21 (strip), 27 (fill) and 52 (key).
Bound = bare x per-angle Fresnel x T ** (1 / cos theta_t), the view at 34 deg, the light from square-on to 52 deg,
plus the reflection: Fog 85.3 .. 88.9, Ember 33.9 .. 37.0. Fog passes (89.6, +0.7). Ember is 4.4 over the top
(41.4; about 5 display levels).
OPEN POINT: the Ember residual of 4.4. Hypothesis: light bouncing inside the closed box (the inner faces' reflections
between the walls, and the red-lit deck under the cover), which the one-pass bound leaves out; the coordinator's rough
sum puts that at only 1-2, so it is not proven as the whole cause. Accepted by the coordinator (22:44): another round
costs more than the gap is worth. A converged reference (MNEE shadow caustics, or about 20k samples) would settle it.
Outside the cover, the floor and the base edge equal the finals within 0.1 level (Fog, Ember, Smoke).

THE STRONG LOADINGS (-cover-strong, for the owner's choice): the cover cast at k times the deck's dye per mm, k set so
the most-absorbed channel passes 0.634 a wall (the mean of Ember's 0.49 and Haze's 0.78 at 1x):
  Dust 2.25x (one wall RGB 0.99/0.83/0.63, luminance 0.85)   Acid 2.45x (0.96/0.95/0.63, 0.93)
  Ice 3.05x (0.63/0.83/0.97, 0.80)                         Smoke 4.30x (0.63/0.63/0.63, 0.63)
  (at 1x: Ember 0.94/0.49/0.55 lum 0.59, Haze 0.88/0.77/0.97 lum 0.81). Fog's deck is near-water-clear light gray
  (#FCFCFC after 25 mm), not frosted (only its outer edge is satin): a stronger Fog cover would be a Smoke cover, so
  Fog stays as it is.
