The satin edge (v3-signed-day-edges-2x.png, the front edge at 2x, Ember above, Ice below): at site size the satin outer
edge reads as a flat, featureless matte band, dark gray with a faint tint, frosted and opaque-looking, in both tints. It
does not read as damaged, but it does not read as glass either: a satin (bead-blasted or machined) edge scatters, so lit
from the front it shows its own matte surface, not the tint through it. Lit through from behind it would glow as frosted
acrylic does. This is the design as signed ('the outer edge satin'), not the light: a polished edge would read as glass.
