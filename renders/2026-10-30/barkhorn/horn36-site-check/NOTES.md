**Verdict: re-render the site's speaker pictures.** The horn is the change you see first. The site's horn is a deep,
nearly opaque red, its neck a solid dark mass round the driver. The formed horn, even in its heavier lot, reads as a
clear pink shell: the flare is see-through, the neck is slim, and the driver's pod and the cap show through it. The
silhouette is the same, but the colour weight on top of the speaker is not. The cabinet, the woofer and the ports look
the same; the bosses, the seal and the IR receiver do not show at this size.

The owner asked for the horn "as deep as the cabinet". It reads at the cabinet's depth, which is lighter than the
resin horn the site shows. The owner may want to see this pair before the site set is rendered again.
