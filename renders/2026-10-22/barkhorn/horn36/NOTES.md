# horn36: the formed horn, for the owner to sign the look

Round 36's proof (spinoffs/barkhorn/README.md, Round 36): the Cloud's horn thermoformed from 10 mm tinted cast sheet,
its wall 8.5 at the throat end to 4.9 in the mouth's corners, rendered from the model's real wall.

The owner's call of 2026-10-22, "Horn tint: as deep as the cabinet (a heavier dye lot for the horn's sheet)": every horn
part in a dye lot of its own, about three times the cabinet's dye (the model's `horn-lot` material, product.HORN_LOT).
These frames are rendered with it. The first versions, in the cabinet's own lot (a pale horn), are in this folder's
history (commit 7e5828d).

- hero-haze-horn36.png: studio31's hero-light-haze rig, camera and light unchanged, framed 3:2 at 2400 x 1600 (the
  resin horn's is renders/2026-10-18/barkhorn/studio31/hero-light-haze.png, 2560 x 1440, the same camera).
- macro-horn-haze-horn36.png: studio31's macro-horn rig (shot on Acid there), in Haze, 1600 x 1600.

Proofed at half size first (64 samples); finals at the shots' 128. No critic round yet: the owner signs the look first,
and the rest of the set is not rendered again until then.
