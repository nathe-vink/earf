# Horn A close-up: satin against polished, with the HF lead modelled (2026-11-03)

Ice, A's studio, light and polish. The camera is at 70 mm, front three-quarter and a little above, 1000 x 800 at 64 samples. The two frames differ only in the waveguide's outside: roughness 0.25 (satin) against 0.05 (polished, the owner's choice at 15:14).

The HF lead is now modelled as the design runs it (`product.py`: "up the cable post (7 bore) into the pod"):
- `lead-hf-pod` (5 mm, two-core) goes from rear post 2 up through the horn's pad and the pod's floor, along the pod's back to the driver's terminals;
- the Cloud's `lead-hf` carries it inside the cabinet, from the amplifier up to the gland under that post.

Outside the horn, the cable runs only inside the bronze post, out of sight.

**What the polished horn shows:** the pod round the driver (the dark round shape) and the two rear bronze posts going down to the cabinet's top, seen through the flare. Those posts are the "two lines" the critics saw. The lead itself stays inside the pod and the post, so it doesn't show. The satin horn shows nothing behind its face.
