# E, the side light: does it draw the site's V? (2026-11-03)

E is described in `spinoffs/barkhorn/variants/variants.py` (`SIDELIGHT`, option "Side light"):
- a COB strip at the foot of each side wall, inside the ports' bay (whose side walls carry no batts), tilted 45° up and outward, about 33 W/m;
- the brace runs moved onto the brace's rear member, at 30 W/m;
- a white film on the bays' bare floor.

The set is D night's, with the gate-3 fixes: a broad slate fill, a darker floor at roughness 0.06, and exposure −0.67 stop. The proxies are 360 x 450 at 24 samples. `sheet.png` shows, left to right: D night (the earlier set, for scale), E with the wall 14 cm behind, and E with it at 0.75 m.

**Result: E does not draw the V.**
- The wings beside the cabinet rise only to about 0.13-0.21 of the front panel's brightness, against the model's estimate of 0.5-1x. On the site's Ice slide they are about 0.45.
- Light does leave through the lower side walls, but it grazes the wall behind, and most of it lands on the floor beside the cabinet.
- A bright streak shows at the lower right, where the side lens is seen through the side wall: the kind of hot line the gate already failed.
- The dark spine above is there, but only because nothing lights the wall at all.
- More power raises the front and the wings together, so the ratio doesn't change. Its upper bound is a side panel with nothing in front of it.
