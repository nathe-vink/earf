Hello,

I'm starting a small hi-fi and lighting line called EARF, and I'd like a quote for the acrylic of a record player
called Mantle. Almost everything is cast acrylic, **tinted through in one colour** and polished:
- a 25 mm plinth floats on four short posts over a 10 mm base;
- the base's edge glows from an LED strip standing in a routed slot, masked from above by a printed black band;
- a five-panel lid sits over it all.

The polished edges are what people see, so they need to be clear and even, with no saw or router marks. The base's
edge carries the light, so its polish decides how evenly it glows.

**The parts, per record player:**

| Part | Thickness / stock | Size (mm) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plinth | 25 mm cast sheet | 450 × 360 | Routed pockets: bearing, motor pod, arm hole, posts. Edges diamond-polished. Brass inserts. |
| Base | 10 mm cast sheet | 470 × 380 | A routed slot in three straight runs (0.98 m) for an LED strip standing on edge. Edges polished to carry the light. A black band printed over the slot (two coats, UV ink), or black strips set in a rebate if you'd rather. M6 inserts for the feet. |
| Lid | 6 mm cast sheet | top 448 × 358; front and back 448 × 94; sides 346 × 94 | Bonded into a box, the joints clear and bubble-free. Edges polished. |
| Posts | cast tube, 40 OD | 4 off, 44 tall | Turned, polished. |
| Platter | cast acrylic, 310 diameter × 25 | 1 | Turned true and flat. A bore for the brass hub. Polished. |
| Pod rings | 20 mm and 12 mm cast sheet | 4 off, 88 diameter | Turned or routed, polished. Stacked around the motor. |
| Pod mat and hinge blocks | 6 mm and 20 mm | small | Routed. |
| Mark | laser etch | EARF, 44 wide, on the plinth | The etch frosted, about 0.1 deep, colour-filled. |

**Quantities:** sets for 1, 10 and 50 record players, each order in one tint.

**Please tell us:**
1. Unit price per set at 1, 10 and 50, and per part if it helps.
2. Setup or tooling (bonding jigs for the lid, routing fixtures), separately.
3. Minimum order, and the minimum per tint.
4. Lead time for first articles and each quantity.
5. Sheet and tube in our tint, at 25, 20, 12, 10 and 6 mm and 310 diameter: can you source it, or should we? Is it
   cast throughout? Dye-lot control so the plinth, base, lid and platter all match. Any custom-tint minimum.
6. Tint matching: chips from the lot at 25 and 10 mm for us to approve, since the tint reads deeper through the
   plinth.
7. Edge finish: diamond, flame or buff, and which you'd use on the base's lit edge.
8. The lid's bonding: cement (we've specified Acrifix 2R 0190 or Weld-On 40), how you keep the joints clear, and
   annealing.
9. The platter: flatness and runout you can hold on a 310 × 25 cast disc.
10. The printed band over the base's slot: do you print, or do you work with a printer?

We're not ready to order yet. This is to plan a first run. Drawings, DXF and STEP are attached.
