# The eight tints

Each part in a tint is cast acrylic coloured through (no paint, no film). The colours below are the **cast colours**:
what the acrylic looks like by transmitted light through about 5 mm, as matched in renders against the speakers'
swatches (`crystal.CAST`; README, round 32f). They are a starting point for the caster, not a finished spec.

| Tint | Cast colour (hex) | Reads lit as |
|---|---|---|
| Ember | #CD8553 | a deep red |
| Dust | #DDBA7B | a warm orange |
| Acid | #E2D481 | a warm yellow |
| Poison | #A4C87C | a green |
| Ice | #5AC0E7 | a sky blue |
| Haze | #A080D7 | a violet |
| Fog | #F7F8F7 | near clear, cool |
| Smoke | #787E87 | a neutral grey |

**How we match.** We will send printed swatches and, once a caster quotes, pay for chips:
- 10 mm thick, 50 × 50, polished both faces;
- one per tint, from the lot the parts will be cut or formed from.

We judge the chips over a 2700 K source against the speakers' swatches and approve them in writing before any parts
are made. Every part in one order should come from one approved lot. If a lot changes, we re-approve on chips.

**Thickness matters.** Lit, a deeper tint pulls redder:
- the shades are 9 mm of tint;
- Core's ball is one 9 mm layer, deepened from the 12 mm the casts were first matched at (README, round 35);
- Mantle's plinth is 25 mm and its base 10 mm.

So a chip at the part's real thickness is the one that counts.
