Hello,

I'm starting a small lighting line called EARF, and I'd like a quote for the turned and milled aluminium parts of two
lamps, all **6061-T6, Type II black anodise, sealed, satin**. One part is polished before anodising.

The black is the only colour on these lamps besides the tinted acrylic, so it needs to be an even, deep black that
matches from part to part and from batch to batch. The heat sinks rely on it for emissivity.
