Hello,

I'm starting a small lighting line called EARF, and I'd like a quote for the shades of a pendant lamp called
Hemisphere. Each shade is a dome, 200 mm across and 100 mm deep. It is made of three nested layers formed from cast
acrylic sheet:
- a tinted outer shell;
- a white opal liner;
- a thin tinted inner skin.

A turned tinted ring closes the three layers at the mouth. The shade is clamped at its crown by an aluminium collar,
which another shop makes. We'd send the collar to you if you want to check the fit.

What matters most is the surface. The outside of the shell should keep the cast sheet's gloss: it never touches the
mould, or it is formed into a female mould. The opal must stay evenly white, with no thin spots at the crown. The
tint must match our approved chips.

**The parts, per shade:**

| Part | Material | Formed size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shell | 10 mm cast acrylic, tinted through | 210 × 210 × 98, wall 9 at the crown | outside glossy, polished edge |
| Liner | 3 mm volume-diffusing opal cast acrylic (about 70 % transmission) | 190 × 190 × 82 | evenly white, sits 1 mm inside the shell |
| Skin | 3 mm cast acrylic, tinted through | 182 × 182 × 81 | its seen face (inside) glossy |
| Rim | cast acrylic tube or ring, tinted through | 192 OD, 14 tall | turned and polished, bonded to the three layers |
| Crown hole | through all three | 96 diameter | for the collar; we can cut it if you prefer |

The three layers keep 1 mm air gaps. They are closed by gaskets at the crown and the rim at the mouth, and the rim
carries a small vent we supply.

**Moulds.** We expect three turned aluminium moulds (shell, liner, skin) that we would own. Please quote:
- making them yourselves, or having them made;
- or tell us what you'd rather form over.

If you'd free-blow the shell or form it some other way that keeps the outside glossy, tell us that too.

**Quantities:** shades for 1, 10 and 50 lamps, each in one tint. A tint is chosen per order: the first lot is most
likely Ember (red) and Smoke (grey).

**Please tell us:**
1. Unit price per shade (shell, liner, skin and rim, bonded) at 1, 10 and 50, and per layer if you'd rather.
2. Tooling: the three moulds, and any fixtures for trimming and bonding. Who owns them, and how long they last.
3. Minimum order, if any, and the minimum per tint.
4. Lead time for the moulds, for first articles, and for each quantity.
5. Sheet: who buys the tinted cast sheet? Can you source it in our tints, or should we supply it? Any minimum on a
   custom tint, and dye-lot control (one lot per order)?
6. Tint matching: will you form test chips or a test shade from the sheet lot before the run, for us to approve?
7. Finish: the gloss of each face as formed, what you polish, and how you trim and finish the mouth.
8. Bonding the rim to the three layers: your cement (we've specified a polymerising cement, Acrifix or Weld-On 40
   kind) and how you keep the opal from wetting through.
9. Tolerances you can hold on wall thickness at the crown and on the 1 mm gaps.
10. Packing and shipping to the Bay Area.

We're not ready to order yet. This is to plan a first run. Drawings and STEP files are attached. Happy to talk it
through or visit.
