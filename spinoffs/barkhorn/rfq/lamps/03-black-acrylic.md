Hello,

I'm starting a small lighting line called EARF, and I'd like a quote for three turned parts in **black cast acrylic,
polished to a deep gloss**, plus one small clear part.

The black parts are seen up close on a table or a ceiling, so the finish is the thing. We want an even, mirror-like
polish with no swirl marks or flame-polish waviness on the faces you can see.

**The parts:**

| Part | For | Material | Envelope | What's in it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base | Core | black cast acrylic rod, 130 diameter or more | 120 diameter × 30 | eased edges. A spherical cup on top for the ball. From below: a 112 counterbore, a 50 middle pocket, three bays for 18650 cells, three pockets for steel ballast, a cable notch. M3 threaded inserts for the hatch and chassis. The faces you see are polished. |
| Window | Core | clear cast acrylic rod | 29.6 diameter × about 5 | a plug with an O-ring groove, polished both faces |
| Canopy | Hemisphere | black cast acrylic rod or tube | 150 diameter × 50, hollow, 3 wall | a ceiling canopy over a steel strap; outside and rim polished |

**Quantities:** sets for 1, 10 and 50 of each lamp (bases and windows for Core, canopies for Hemisphere).

**Please tell us:**
1. Unit price per part at 1, 10 and 50.
2. Setup or tooling charges, such as fixtures or soft jaws, separately.
3. Minimum order.
4. Lead time for first articles and each quantity.
5. Material: can you source black cast acrylic rod at 130 diameter, and 150 for the canopy? Is it truly cast, not
   extruded? (Extruded crazes near the threaded inserts.) Is there any colour or gloss difference between lots?
6. Finish: how you polish turned faces and pockets (diamond, buff, vapour or flame), and what we'd see.
7. The threaded inserts (heat-set or bonded) and how you keep them from stress-cracking the acrylic.
8. Tolerances on the cup and the 112 counterbore, where an aluminium hatch with an O-ring seats.
9. Annealing after machining.
10. Packing so the polish arrives unmarked.
