Re: FusionAmp FA122 in a finished loudspeaker: certification, mounting and the preset

Hello,

We are building a two-way horn loudspeaker, Tropos, around your FA122. Each speaker has its own FA122, in a sealed,
earthed aluminium compartment behind the plate, and a master and slave on your link. Before we take the finished
product to a test lab, we have a few questions.

1. **Certification.** Do you have a CB test certificate and report for the FA122 to IEC 62368-1 (with the US national
   differences), or a UL or other NRTL recognition? Could you share them, or the report's number, for our lab?
2. **Mounting.** We count ten mounting holes on the plate, five each side. Could you confirm the hole pattern and
   sizes against your dimensional drawing? Our plate mounts on brass inserts in a cast acrylic back, with machine
   screws in nylon shoulder washers, so it can slide as it warms.
3. **The preset.** We plan to load the same verified filter into all three preset slots and to set the filter
   password at the factory. Is there anything we should know about doing that, or about restoring it in service?
4. **Gain.** We attenuate the HF channel 6 dB in the DSP rather than with a passive pad (a 16 ohm compression driver,
   108 dB at 4 V, behind a series capacitor). Is the FA122's output noise figure published, so we can confirm the
   driver's hiss at 1 m?
5. **The link.** We would like to put a 75 Ω S/PDIF isolation transformer in the master-to-slave link, to break a
   ground loop. Does the link tolerate one?
