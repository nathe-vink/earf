# Candidate vendors (research only, 2026-10-30)

From public web pages only. Nobody has been contacted, and no form or account was used. Anything seen only in a search
snippet or a directory listing (not on the vendor's own page) is marked **[snippet]**. None of the pages read gives a
custom-colour minimum. Ask each one before relying on anything here.

## 01 Shades: drape forming, moulds, domes

- **Rapid Accu-Form**, Benicia CA (Bay Area). Thermo-, pressure- and vacuum-forming in acrylic.
  - Makes its own aluminium and wood moulds in house, on 3- and 5-axis CNC and a lathe.
  - Sheet 0.015" to 0.500", so 10 mm fits.
  - Takes "small run quantities". Drape forming isn't named on the page.
  - https://rapidaccuform.com/
- **Crystal Craft**, La Verne CA. Thermoforming and acrylic fabrication for lighting.
  - Domes, globes and hemispheres; moulds made in house.
  - "Museum-grade bonding" and polishing; "no job too small".
  - https://www.crystalcraft.com/
- **Plastic Domes & Spheres** (a Laird Plastics division), Santa Fe Springs CA. Custom domes and hemispheres up to 8 ft.
  - Pressure, drape and thermoforming; blow-moulds one-piece spheres.
  - Minimum $250 for custom work.
  - https://www.plastic-domes-spheres.com/
- **Acrylic Art**, Emeryville CA. Thermoforming, CNC routers and lasers.
  - https://www.acrylicart.com/
- **Mr. Plastics**, San Leandro CA. In-house thermoforming, machining and CNC; sells sheet.
  - https://www.mr-plastics.com/
- Out of region: **Reynolds Polymer**, Grand Junction CO, does acrylic forming **[snippet]**.
  - https://www.reynoldspolymer.com/capabilities/forming/

**Gap:** no Bay Area page offers drape forming over a customer's own aluminium moulds, or free-blowing, by name.
Rapid Accu-Form (moulds in house) and Plastic Domes & Spheres (blow-moulding) are the nearest.
