# Held back from the site

The owner, 2026-10-25: "everything about the sustainability of the brand feels lame and cheap until we can deliver on
it". So the home page's Mother EARF section ("Everything comes home.") and its link in the footer came off earf.world
with r42-site-hemisphere. Its words are kept here, as they stood, for the day each line is true. "We mean to" is still a
claim: under the FTC's Green Guides (and the EU's rules on green claims) a promise about recycled content or a take-back
has to be backed when it is made, so none of this goes back up until it can be shown.

## The section as it stood (web.py, main at 00a02545)

Eyebrow: Mother EARF. Heading: Everything comes home.

> Our things borrow their names from the Earth, core to atmosphere. Mother EARF is how we pay it back.

Beside the multicolour globe ("The mark, made in the same acrylic."):

1. **Acrylic that has lived before.** Cast acrylic can be broken back down to the liquid it was cast from and cast
   again. Recycled sheet isn't yet made in our colours at 20 mm; when it is, we mean to use it.
2. **Made to be mended.** Drivers and amplifiers any good technician knows. Screws, not glue, wherever a part may one
   day need to come out. Core's battery changes with a screwdriver.
3. **Taken back at the end.** When you're done with a piece, we mean to take it back: what still works carries on, and
   the acrylic goes back to be cast again.

## What each line needs before it goes back

| Line | True when |
|---|---|
| Acrylic that has lived before | A caster supplies recycled (or depolymerised and recast) acrylic in the eight colours at the thicknesses we use, with its share on paper, and the pieces are made from it |
| Made to be mended | The first pair is built and serviced as drawn: a service guide, spare drivers, amplifier and Core's battery on sale, and the screws where the drawings say |
| Taken back at the end | A take-back runs: terms, shipping, and a recycler or caster who takes the acrylic, named |
| The naming line | Free to use any time; it was held only because it introduced the section |
| The lining's "Recycled polyester batts" (Atmosphere's particulars) | The batts are bought, with their recycled share on the supplier's sheet; then the share goes in the line ("70 % recycled polyester") |

The globe itself stays a brand object (the header's mark, the bar's centrepiece); only the promise is held.
