# Tropos: transit and the crate (2026-11-01, the engineering audit's item 6)

**The load.** A speaker is 70.2 kg with the formed horn and 75.2 kg as Strata (`stability/com.json`). The audit said
62 kg; the parts list gives these. The horn lifts off (11.4 kg with its driver and posts, Strata) and travels separately
in the same crate, so no load passes through the posts or the pads in transit.

**The crate, one speaker each:**
- **The base:** a plywood pallet base with three skids (a forklift or a pallet jack from either side). The cabinet stands
  on its own four feet, removed, through 75 mm PE foam pads (about 30 kg/m3) under its bottom's edges.
  - The pads are sized to about 5 kPa static stress: about 0.14 m2 under 70 kg.
  - Cushion curves put that at about 45 to 60 G for a 760 mm drop (est.; confirmed by the drop test).
- **The cabinet:** 75 mm PE foam blocks on every face, at the corners and edges, never on a polished face's middle. A
  soft cotton bag over the cabinet first, so the foam does not mark the acrylic.
- **The horn:** in its own carton with a foam cradle under its pads and boss, in the crate's top bay. The cap stays on
  and the driver stays mounted.
- **The panels:** ISPM 15 exempt (plywood), screwed, not nailed, so it can be reused for returns.
- **Inside:** about 710 x 570 x 1050 mm for the cabinet, plus the horn's bay. About 105 kg packed (est.).

**The tests:**
- the packaged speaker to ISTA 3B (less-than-truckload), at the heights and sequence the standard sets for its weight;
- or ISTA 2B if a lab prefers the simpler partial simulation;
- a rotational edge drop, a vibration profile and a concentrated impact on the crate's faces.

The pass criteria:
- no crack, craze or bond-line failure (polarized light afterward, FAI V4);
- every insert tight;
- the posts' sockets undamaged;
- the response within FAI E4.

**First:** one test crate with the first article, before the second speaker is packed.
