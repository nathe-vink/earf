# Tropos on the FA123 instead of the FA122: desk check (2026-11-13)

The owner's yes to the change, if it fits. The plan: channels 1 and 2 bridged (BTL) to the woofer, channel 3 on the horn
driver. This is desk work only; no CAD has changed.

**Sources:**
- Hypex's Fusion manual R4 (hypex.nl): the dimension table, bridging, installation, thermal protection, specifications.
- Hypex's NC100HF datasheet R8: the FA123's third channel.
- Hypex's NC122MP pages: BTL power, idle losses, noise. The datasheet PDF itself returned 404; its figures came through
  search.
- The woofer model: `acoustics/fa123_woofer.py`, output in `fa123_woofer.txt`. It takes FaitalPRO's figures from
  `components.json` and the box at 135.6 L net, 38 Hz, QL 7, half space at 1 m.

## a. Fit

| | FA122 now | FA123 |
|---|---|---|
| plate (Hypex, A x B x C) | 315 x 120 x 55 | **360** x 120 x 55 |
| cutout (D x E) | 291 x 96 | **336** x 96 |
| weight | 815 g | 955 g |
| the compartment inside (x, y, z) | 125 x 75 x 320 | 125 x 75 x **365** |
| its centre on the back | z 520 | **z 497.5** |
| the box overall in z, with the flange | 343.5 to 696.5 | **298.5** to 696.5 |

- **The top stays where it is:** it keeps its clearance to the upper brace (z 700). The box grows downward, into the
  back's lower batt, which is set back by the lining's 20 mm amplifier clearance as now.
- **Nothing else lies in that band.** The tie bosses sit at the lower brace (z 182 to 218), and the back's screws are
  at its edges. The IR lead's rise and the engraved rating follow the box, since both are set from it in `product.py`.
- **Clearance:**
  - Hypex asks for 3 mm to any conductive part. The cutout leaves 14.5 mm to the box's walls at each end, as now, and
    20 mm behind the module (75 deep against 55).
  - The 5052 box is earthed.
  - The compartment is metal, so it meets Hypex's V-1 and V-0 rule.
- **Mounting:**
  - Hypex leaves 12 mm round the cutout for the screws, as on the FA122.
  - The FA122's ten bonded inserts do not match: the plate is 45 mm longer, so the pattern changes.
  - The pattern is in Hypex's 2D drawing, which is not public. It is already asked for in the Hypex letter for the
    FA122; it needs asking for the FA123 too.
- **The box volume:** 0.4 L gross more inside a 135.6 L box. The tuning moves less than 0.2 %, so the ports are
  unchanged.

## b. The woofer, bridged

- **The amplifier:**
  - The FA123's main module is the FA122's own NC122MP; its third channel is an NC100HF.
  - Hypex rates the NC122MP in BTL at 200 W into 4 ohm and 250 W into 8 ohm.
  - Hypex's minimum BTL load is 4 ohm. It recommends 8 ohm or higher.
  - The 15PR400-8's lowest impedance is 5.55 ohm in the model (5.9 on FaitalPRO's sheet). That is inside Hypex's
    minimum but under its recommendation, so it needs Hypex's word.

**Excursion and level** (peak excursion, mm; dB at 1 m, half space; the preset's 28 Hz LR4 high-pass):

| Hz | FA122, 24.5 V: x / dB | FA123 bridged, 44.7 V: x / dB | FA123 held to Xmax: dB |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | 4.2 / 93.2 | **7.6** / 98.5 | 96.1 |
| 31.5 | 3.2 / 103.6 | **5.9** / 108.9 | 108.7 |
| 40 | 1.6 / 110.9 | 2.9 / 116.1 | 116.1 |
| 50 | 3.9 / 114.3 | **7.2** / 119.5 | 117.6 |
| 63 | 4.4 / 115.9 | **8.1** / 121.2 | 118.3 |
| 80 | 3.7 / 116.9 | **6.7** / 122.1 | 120.7 |
| 100 | 2.8 / 117.4 | 5.1 / 122.6 | 122.6 |

**What this shows:**
- **No damage:** the full bridged swing peaks at 8.1 mm, against an Xdamage of 15.5. The high-pass cannot help much,
  because the peak lies above the tuning (50 to 80 Hz): an LR4 at 40 Hz only brings it to 7.2 mm.
- **Clean:** the Fusion has one limiter, the soft clip, set by the load and the maximum power. Set to **125 W into 8
  ohm (31.6 V)**, it holds the cone to Xmax everywhere.
  - That is +2.2 dB over the FA122.
  - The woofer's maximum goes from about 117 dB to about **119 to 120 dB at 1 m** (100 Hz), and from 116 to 118 dB at
    63 Hz.
  - Left at the full 250 W, it would be +5 dB, with the cone past Xmax at 50 to 80 Hz: audible distortion on bass at
    full level.
- **Thermal:** 125 W is about a third of the driver's AES 400 W. Even 250 W is within it.
- **Settings:** keep the 28 Hz LR4 high-pass. Set the soft clip on, at 8 ohm and 125 W. Keep the port notch and the
  rest of the preset.

## c. The horn driver on channel 3 (NC100HF)

- **Power:** in an FA123, Hypex notes the NC100HF's swing is limited by the NC122MP's supply. So the swing into the
  DE1085TN-16 is about the same as the FA122's channel 2 now: about 22 V, 31 W, about 123 dB at 1 m, within its 140 W.
- **Continuous rating:** Hypex rates the NC100HF at 20 W continuous at 25 C. It has no over-temperature protection of
  its own; Hypex designs it for the band above about 500 Hz. Above the 1 kHz crossover, programme power on this
  channel is a few watts.
- **Padding:** as now, no L-pad. The 6 dB comes off in the DSP and the 33 uF capacitor stays in series. The NC100HF's
  gain differs from the NC122MP's, so the preset's level is re-measured (FAI).
- **Noise:** 40 uV maximum, unweighted, the same figure as the NC122MP. The hiss stays at about 8 to 10 dB SPL at 1 m.

## d. Heat in the compartment

**Idle:**
- FA122: the NC122MP, about 7.5 W.
- FA123: plus the NC100HF's 3.5 W, about **11 W**, which is 47 % more.

**At 1/8 of the rated power** (the 62368-1 normal-operation case):
- The woofer's 125 W limit gives 15.6 W out, about 1.4 W lost at 92 %.
- So about **13 W in all**, against about 9 W now.

**Against Hypex's guidance:**
- The guidance is met as now: a separate sealed compartment, the plate as the heat sink to the room, nothing over the
  ventilation, an ambient of 35 C at most.
- Its thermal limiter (-6 dB) and shutdown protect the amplifier.

**Still open:**
- The rise inside is not computed here.
- The 62368-1 temperature-rise pre-check (`compliance.md`) is where it is measured, at full output, with the box sealed.
