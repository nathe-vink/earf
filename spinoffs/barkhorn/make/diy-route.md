# Tropos's horn, made ourselves: the do-it-yourself route (2026-10-31)

This is the plan B for the laminated horn (`laminated-horn.md`) if no shop can make it in time: EARF cuts, bonds and
anneals it, and only the air path's 5-axis machining goes out. The figures come from research R8 (Ace Makerspace,
Röhm's and ACRYLITE's bonding guides); "est." marks an estimate.

## Where and with what

| what | where | cost | when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cutting the 44 layers** | **Ace Makerspace, Oakland**: a 100 W CO2 laser (1300 x 900 mm bed), $0.50 a minute of firing time; a 4 x 8 ft CNC router for the cabinet's panels if wanted | membership $200 a month (or $2,000 a year), individual only, small-business use allowed; classes: Shop Basics $18.50 (**5 Nov**, one seat left, or 3 Dec), laser $65 (**17 Nov**), router $95 (5 Dec) | laser access from **17 Nov** |
| the laser time | about 80 m of cut for the pair. At 2 to 4 mm/s on 1/2 in (est.), that is about 6 to 11 hours of firing, perhaps two passes | about **$170 to $340** | two or three long sessions |
| **Second laser** if Ace's is busy | Maker Nexus, Sunnyvale: two 100 W lasers, a 24 x 40 in and a 700 x 500 mm bed. The largest layer, 630 x 366 mm, fits the first | $160 a month, no tool fees | class dates to confirm |
| **Bonding and annealing** | **EARF's own space**, not a shared one: the cement's vapour is heavier than air and needs floor-level extraction | kit **about $1,500 to $3,000** (est., R8 §D.1): Weld-On 40 (a gallon kit $198), a degassing pot with a bleed valve ($160), a scale, PE spacers, a stepped core, weights, a polariscope, a respirator, extraction | about a week to assemble |
| the annealing oven | a forced-air oven that holds one horn: **Despatch DOL-69A, $6,500**, or a used convection oven with good air circulation and control, about $1,000 to $3,000 (est.) | $1,000 to $6,500 | used: depends on the find |
| **The air path** | out, on 5 axes (Atlas Contour, SF, or the R7 list), from `rfq/tropos/e-laminated-horn/step/air-path.step` | about $750 a horn (est., `laminated-horn.md`) | 1 to 2 weeks |

**Total cash** for a pair's horns, beyond the sheet, est.:
- membership for two months, $400;
- classes, about $180;
- laser time, about $250;
- the kit, about $2,000;
- a used oven, about $2,000;
- two air paths, $1,500.

That is **about $6,300**, or about $10,800 with a new oven. A shop's job is about $3,600 to $5,700 for the pair
(`laminated-horn.md`). Doing it ourselves is not cheaper for one pair. It is a way to get the horns if no shop will, and
the kit and oven stay for every later pair.

## Against the schedule

| week of | what | the owner's own time (est.) |
|---|---|---|
| 2 Nov | Join Ace; book Shop Basics for 5 Nov and the laser class for 17 Nov. Order the sheet in 48 x 36 in blanks, and the bonding kit. | 1 hour |
| 5 Nov | Shop Basics at Ace (about 3 hours). | 3 hours |
| 9 Nov | The kit arrives. **Coupon trials** with off-cuts: 2074 and 2423 two-layer coupons, clear cement and tinted, annealed at 70 and 80 °C (3 to 4 days). | 2 days |
| 16 Nov | Laser class on 17 Nov, then the online check. The sheet arrives about 16 to 23 Nov. | 4 hours |
| 23 Nov | **Cut the 44 layers** at Ace: two or three sessions of about 4 hours. Check stress in the polariscope. Number them. | 2 days |
| 30 Nov | Pre-anneal, then bond the first horn's sub-stacks within 24 hours: 3 to 4 hours a joint, about 7 joints a sub-stack, the sub-stacks in parallel. | 4 to 5 days |
| 7 Dec | Anneal the sub-stacks (about 2 days). Join them (1 to 2 days). Final anneal (2 to 7 days, by the rule the oven allows). | 1 to 2 days of hands-on time, the rest oven time |
| 14 to 21 Dec | Horn 1 out for its air path (1 to 2 weeks). Bond horn 2's sub-stacks while it is away. | 4 to 5 days |
| 28 Dec to 8 Jan | Horn 1 back, polished, annealed again. Horn 2's anneals and air path. | — |
| about 15 Jan | **Both horns**, if everything runs first time and the 5-axis shop turns each in a week. | — |

**What it means:**
- The do-it-yourself route misses 19 January as likely as it makes it: about 3 to 5 weeks a horn, and one oven holds one horn. It
  is a fallback, not the plan.
- It needs **about three weeks of the owner's own time** between 2 November and mid-December, almost all of it bonding.
- It is best started anyway in its cheap first steps. These cost about $450 and make any shop's job safer:
  - the Ace membership and classes, which give a laser for the lamps and companions too;
  - the coupon trials, which every route needs.
- The commitment comes with the kit and the oven (the weeks of 9 and 30 November). Decide on it once the shops' quotes
  are in, about 16 November.

**The owner would personally:**
- take the two classes;
- cut the layers;
- do or supervise every joint, in a respirator, with floor extraction;
- run the oven;
- take each horn to the 5-axis shop and back.

Nobody else at EARF is trained to bond yet. The coupon trials are the training.
