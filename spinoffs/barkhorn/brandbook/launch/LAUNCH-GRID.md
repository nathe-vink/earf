picture, one line, the wordmark small in a corner (the Brand Book's social rules); the caption below the post says
what is true and useful, never "link in bio", no hashtag lists, no emoji. Pictures marked **stand-in** are renders
holding the place of photographs to take at the pop-up; every other picture is a render and is labeled so in the
alt text until the real pieces are photographed. Mockup: `out/launch-grid.png` (drawn by `../scenes/launch-grid.html`).
