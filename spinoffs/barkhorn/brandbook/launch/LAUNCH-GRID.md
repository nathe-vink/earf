# The launch grid: nine posts after the debut

Three weeks, three posts a week, from the morning after the debut. Days count from the debut, D; no calendar dates
until the owner sets the debut. Each post is one picture, one line, the wordmark small in a corner (the Brand Book's
social rules); the caption below the post says what is true and useful, never "link in bio", no hashtag lists, no emoji.

**Pictures (2026-11-09):** only renders that passed the critics, each labeled "Render" in its alt text until the real
pieces are photographed: the seven tall fronts (`brandbook/book/img/honest-front-*.jpg`), the Ice habitat (`honest-ice-habitat.jpg`). Seven colors,
Ember to Fog; Smoke is held for the owner. Where no passed picture exists yet (Core, Mantle, the listening sessions),
the post is marked "to come". The mockup (`out/launch-grid.png`) still shows the earlier pictures and is
redrawn when the waiting posts have theirs.
