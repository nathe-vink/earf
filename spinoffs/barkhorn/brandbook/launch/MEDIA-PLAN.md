# Media plan: twenty outlets, January to May 2027

Brand lane, r47 (2026-10-31). Preparation only: nothing has been sent and nobody contacted. Sixteen outlets and their
pitching routes come from the events and press research (`research/2026-10-31/R5-events-press.md` on `r46-owner-notes`,
§4.6, fetched 31 October 2026; its tags kept: **[C]** confirmed on the outlet's own page, **[U]** unverified because the
site refused automated access). Four more are outlets the investor deck already cites as sources (SF Chronicle, 7x7,
Sunset, Gear Patrol); their routes are to be found by hand. People are named only where R5 found them as an outlet's
published editors.

## The angles

- **The eight tints.** Hi-fi and light in cast acrylic coloured all the way through, the same eight in everything; a
  room in one colour or three. The design press's angle.
- **The horn.** "The oldest amplifier is a shape": a horn formed from the cabinet's own tinted acrylic. If the owner
  chooses the laminated horn (14 to 22 stacked layers whose edges read as strata, `r33-speaker-horn`), that is the
  picture: a horn you can read like a cliff face. The hi-fi and object press's angle.
- **The listening bar where everything is for sale.** The pop-up, then Planet EARF: a room you can buy, one coin one
  song, Sessions pressed to vinyl. The lifestyle and city press's angle.
- **Mother EARF.** The globe mark made as an object, six slotted discs of tinted acrylic, 1.25 m in the bar and drawn at
  4 m for a plaza. The art and architecture angle; best as a picture inside a larger story.

- for hi-fi: measured figures from the first pair and a written loan policy (to write).

## Notes

- **Embargoes.** No outlet R5 could read publishes a policy; ask each editor before sending embargoed material, and use
  one lift time (R5's recommendation).
- **Exclusives.** Offer one first look, to one outlet, and say so to the others.
- **Honesty.** Every render is captioned as a render; every figure is "design" until measured.
