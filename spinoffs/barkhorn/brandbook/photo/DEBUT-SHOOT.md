# The debut night: photo and film brief

Brand lane, r47 (2026-10-31). **A draft brief for the photographer and the filmmaker.** Nothing is booked.

**Why it matters.** These are the first pictures of EARF that aren't renders. Kickstarter allows no photorealistic
renderings (`../kickstarter/README.md`). The partner packs wait on "a photograph from the debut"
(`../partners/README.md`). The hi-fi and design press get them within 48 hours (`../launch/MEDIA-PLAN.md`). So the
night is a shoot with a party around it, not the other way round, and the empty-room hour before the doors is the most
important hour of the day.

**The look.** The Brand Book's:
- a dark room, one source of light, the tint doing the work;
- no teal-and-orange grade;
- no added light on the products;
- faces lit by the lamps, not by a flash.

## Light in a dark room

| Where | About how bright (est.) | Camera (stills) | Camera (film) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Under a Hemisphere, on the table | about 450–500 lux (the lamps lane's figure for 590 lm at 750 mm) | ISO 800–1600, f/2.8, 1/125 | Base ISO, f/2.8 |
| Faces at a high-top lit by Core | a few tens of lux on a face (est.) | ISO 3200–6400, f/1.4–2, 1/100 | Dual-gain high base (e.g. ISO 3200–4000), f/1.4–2 |
| The room at 20–30% house | low: a dim gallery | ISO 3200–6400, f/2, or the tripod | f/1.4–2, high base ISO |
| **"Lights"**: lamps only | very low, except at the lamps | **Tripod**, ISO 1600–3200, f/2.8–4, 1/4–1 s for the room; handheld f/1.4 for people | f/1.4, high base ISO; keep the lamps from clipping |

- **White balance: fixed at 2700 K for the night**, the lamps' colour. The tints must read as the tints, not as
  something corrected toward grey.
- **A ColorChecker** (or a grey card and a tint chip) shot once at every station before the doors, under the night's
  light. It gives the editor a reference for each tint.
- **The tints are the risk.** Red and blue clip first in a dark room (Ember, Ice, Haze). Expose for the brightest rim,
  and bracket the empty-room wides.
- **No flash.** During the Session, no light of any kind from the crew. For the few words, one small LED panel
  bounced for the face only.
- **Lenses:** fast primes, 24 or 28, 35, 50, 85 mm. A 100 mm macro for the details.
- **RAW** for stills. A log or flat profile for film, at 4K 24 fps.
- **Sound** for film: the Session's audio comes from the recording engineer. The film's own mic records room sound and
  the words only.

### B. The doors (6:30–6:45)

- B1: arrivals in silhouette against the room; a coin taken from the bowl.
- B2: two or three faces turning to the room for the first time (with releases).

### C. Session 00 (7:05–7:50; silent; two fixed positions)

- C1: the wide from the back: the seated room, the artist, **the pair in frame** (it proves where the sound came from).
- C2: the artist close, from the side, by the lamps' light.
- C3: faces listening (only those with releases).
- C4 (film): a locked-off wide for the whole set, synced later to the engineer's audio. **This is the listening film's
  source.**

### D. A few words (7:55)

- D1: the owner speaking, the room behind.

### E. Listening (8:00–8:45)

- E1: faces lit by a Core from below, at the high-tops.
- E2: a coin handed over for a song; the record going on.
- E3: people at Mantle; a hand on the lid.
- E4: the shade swap at the door, with a guest's hand (with a release).

### F. "Lights" (8:45–8:55)

- F1: **the room by the lamps alone**: a tripod wide, 1/4–1 s, people soft, the lamps sharp.
- F2: the pair lit from below in the dark, with silhouettes in front.
- F3 (film): one slow push-in across the room, 20 seconds, for the teaser's last frame.

## Releases

- **Notice on the invitation and at the door:** "We'll be photographing and filming tonight, and Session 00 is
  recorded. If you'd rather not be in the pictures, tell us at the door and we'll give you a [black] coin."
  - Guests holding the black coin are left out of every selected frame.
  - Note for counsel: whether a notice is enough consent for a seated, identifiable audience.
- **Model releases** (signed, paper or a release app) for **every face used in Kickstarter, ads, partner packs or the
  site**. Those are commercial uses; editorial use of the night is a different matter, **[for counsel]**. Aim for 10–15
  signed releases on the night, asked politely after each picture.
- **The artist** approves pictures of themself, as `../sessions/RIGHTS.md` provides.
- **The room.** Commercial photography and filming are allowed in the hire contract. Ask FMCAC whether a filming permit
  applies on federal property inside a leased venue **[to ask]**.
- **The photographer's licence** (in the booking): EARF gets a perpetual, non-exclusive licence for editorial and
  commercial use (site, Kickstarter, press, partner packs, social, print), with credit where it's practical. No
  exclusivity is needed.

**Captions** follow the honesty rule:
- what the object is;
- "prototype";
- the tint as cast. A stock colour is named as stock (`PRODUCT-SHOOT.md`): "Hemisphere in stock red, nearest to
  Ember. Prototype, January 2027."
