## The eight-tint problem: shoot what exists, and say what it is

The first prototypes are cut from **stock sheet**:
- **2423 Red** stands in for Ember;
- **2064 Gray** stands in for Smoke.

The lamps lane picked them as the nearest stock colours (`stock-colours.md`), because a mill's custom tint takes weeks
and a pallet. The other six tints don't exist as products yet.

**The rules:**
1. **Never recolour a photograph** toward the mark's hex, and never composite a tint that wasn't cast. A photograph of
   a stock-red Hemisphere stays stock red.
2. **Caption the colour as cast.** Where it differs from the tint, name both:
   - "Hemisphere in stock red (2423), nearest to Ember. Prototype, February 2027."
   - "Core in Smoke. Prototype." (if the cast matches).
3. **Show the eight only as samples**: the Tint Library or the chips, photographed lit from behind, captioned "Samples of
   the sheet".
4. **A ColorChecker frame for every set-up**, so the colour is corrected to the truth, not to taste.
5. **Kickstarter offers only what is cast.** The campaign's "any of the eight tints" holds only if the run is cast to
   match (`../kickstarter/README.md`, open question 3). Until then the photographs show the stock colours and the
   samples, labelled.

## The look

The Brand Book's look, made with real light:
- **Night:** a black set, one hard key, the object's own light doing the work. Hemisphere and Core lit by themselves;
  Tropos lit from below, as on the site; Mantle's base glowing.
- **Paper:** the brand's paper (#ECE8E1, a warm off-white seamless) under one soft source, for clean catalogue and
  press frames, and the tints' coloured shadows on the paper.
- **Acrylic needs edges, not fronts:**
  - large strip lights behind and to the side define a transparent edge;
  - black flags stop the set from reflecting;
  - a polariser controls glare;
  - the object's own light gets a long exposure, blended with a flash frame only if both are real and the caption
    stays true.
- **Clean as for a jeweller:** cotton gloves, a microfibre, an anti-static brush, a blower. Dust shows on every acrylic
  face at 100%. Never solvent or glass spray: they craze acrylic.
