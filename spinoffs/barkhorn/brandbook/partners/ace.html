A proposal for Ace Hotel Group · draft · confidential

A listening corner for one Ace lobby.

EARF makes hi-fi and light in San Francisco, in cast acrylic tinted all the way through, in eight colours from Ember to Smoke. Tropos is a powered horn loudspeaker; Mantle, a record player whose plinth floats over a base lit at its edge; Hemisphere, a pendant; Core, a cordless table lamp. The first pair of Tropos played for the first time at our debut in San Francisco on 19 January 2027, in FOG Design+Art week.

What we propose

A season-long EARF listening corner in one Ace lobby: a numbered pair of Tropos, a Mantle record player and Hemisphere pendants, in a tint chosen for the room, with listening sessions through the season. It starts as a residency and becomes a purchase if it works for Ace. Beside it, an Ace × EARF edition of Core, our cordless lamp, for the rooms or the shop.

Why Ace Hotel Group
  • Ace has treated sound as part of the building: Devon Turnbull built the lobby system at Ace Hotel New York.
  • Tivoli made a co-branded radio for Ace Sydney’s rooms; Ace set the brief and the silkscreen.
  • Elsewhere, hotels now commission listening rooms: OJAS’s Listening Room at Patina Osaka; the Equipment Room at Hotel Magdalena, Austin, with Klipsch.
  • A corner is smaller than a room: one season, one tint, no construction.
Ace Hotel Group brings
  • The lobby, and the guests in it
  • The programme: who plays, and when
  • Its shop, for the Core edition
  • Photography and its own channels
EARF brings
  • The system: Tropos, Mantle, Hemisphere, installed and tuned
  • The Sessions: one album, start to finish, on set nights
  • Core in an Ace tint, numbered
  • Service through the season

How it could work.

An outline to discuss, not an offer. The ranges are those typical in published sources for licensed and co-branded design (furniture, lighting, consumer products); none of it is agreed, and every line is for counsel.

TermIn outline
ResidencyA season (about three months): EARF installs and services; Ace hosts and programmes. A residency fee or a loan [to agree]. for counsel
PurchaseAt the end, Ace may buy the corner: 50% on order, the balance on installation, as commissions usually run. for counsel
Core editionAce buys a numbered run (for example 100) at wholesale, about 40–50% of retail. for counsel
Co-brand“Ace × EARF” on the Core edition, under a licence of 1–3 years; each approves the other’s use of its name. for counsel
IPEARF keeps the design; a credit line such as “The Listening Corner by EARF”. for counsel
CareInsurance and liability for loaned equipment in a public lobby; a service contract if bought. for counsel
PricesA suggested retail price for the shop only: no resale price set (California). for counsel
