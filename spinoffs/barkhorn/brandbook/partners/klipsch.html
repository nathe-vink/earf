A proposal for Klipsch · draft · confidential

A numbered horn edition, built in Hope.

EARF makes hi-fi and light in San Francisco, in cast acrylic tinted all the way through, in eight colours from Ember to Smoke. Tropos is a powered horn loudspeaker; Mantle, a record player whose plinth floats over a base lit at its edge; Hemisphere, a pendant; Core, a cordless table lamp. The first pair of Tropos played for the first time at our debut in San Francisco on 19 January 2027, in FOG Design+Art week.

What we propose

A Klipsch × EARF edition of a Heritage model, built by Klipsch in Hope, with an EARF cast-acrylic horn surround, baffle or stands in one of our tints: about 100 numbered pairs. EARF sells it direct in the United States; Klipsch dealers sell it everywhere else, as with the kO-R2.

Why Klipsch
  • The kO-R1 (2024): 100 pairs at $8,498, built in Hope with Devon Turnbull of OJAS, and sold out.
  • The kO-R2 (2026): 600 numbered pairs at $11,998, “handcrafted in Hope”, “sold direct from Ojas” in the US, through authorised retailers in Europe, launched at Milan Design Week with USM and Karimoku.
  • Klipsch’s sound in other people’s objects and rooms: La-Z-Boy’s AudioLuxe furniture, and the Equipment Room at Hotel Magdalena, Austin (October 2026).
  • EARF brings what OJAS brought: a designer’s object, a colour, a room to launch it in. The tint is new.
Klipsch brings
  • The Heritage model and its engineering
  • The build in Hope: quality, warranty, certification
  • Its dealers outside the US
  • Its press, and a show stand
EARF brings
  • The acrylic parts’ design, drawn and specified
  • The eight tints, and approval of each cast
  • The US direct channel and the launch: a listening room, a Session record
  • The design press (Wallpaper*, Dezeen, Core77 on our list)

Terms in outline · the timeline

How it could work.

An outline to discuss, not an offer. The ranges are those typical in published sources for licensed and co-branded design (furniture, lighting, consumer products); none of it is agreed, and every line is for counsel.

TermIn outline
StructureKlipsch builds. EARF buys finished pairs at a transfer price and sells them direct in the US, keeping the retail margin; Klipsch’s dealers sell elsewhere. for counsel
RoyaltyOn Klipsch’s own sales: 3–7% of net wholesale, 5% common for consumer products. Net defined, with listed deductions only. for counsel
FeeA development fee for the design work, recoupable against royalty. Advances in this field are small or none. for counsel
EditionAbout 100 numbered pairs, capped; the tint and the numbering EARF’s to approve. for counsel
ControlEARF approves colour, material and finish, and the use of its name; credit on the product. for counsel
Term1–3 years, renewable; a sell-off of 30–180 days; rights revert if not launched in 12–18 months. for counsel
PricesA suggested retail price only: a licensor can’t set resale prices in California. for counsel
ReportsQuarterly statements, about three months in arrears; audit rights. for counsel
