A proposal for Pro-Ject Audio Systems · draft · confidential

A tinted-acrylic edition, made under licence.

EARF makes hi-fi and light in San Francisco, in cast acrylic tinted all the way through, in eight colours from Ember to Smoke. Tropos is a powered horn loudspeaker; Mantle, a record player whose plinth floats over a base lit at its edge; Hemisphere, a pendant; Core, a cordless table lamp. The first pair of Tropos played for the first time at our debut in San Francisco on 19 January 2027, in FOG Design+Art week.

What we propose

A Pro-Ject turntable built in Pro-Ject’s own factory with an EARF cast-acrylic plinth and platter, in a few of our tints, numbered: about 50 to 100 in each. Pro-Ject sells it through its dealers; EARF sells it direct and launches it with a listening event in San Francisco. Mantle, our own record player, stays separate.

Why Pro-Ject Audio Systems
  • Pro-Ject already makes editions “under license”: its Harley-Davidson turntable is “Manufactured by Pro-Ject … under license from Harley-Davidson”.
  • Its Artist Collection runs with partners from Metallica and The Rolling Stones to Third Man Records.
  • It sells an acrylic deck already, the 6 PerspeX, and builds its turntables in its own Czech factory.
  • Its home is about 35 km north of Vienna, where HIGH END meets on 6–9 May.
Pro-Ject Audio Systems brings
  • The deck: motor, bearing, arm, and their engineering
  • The factory, quality control, certification
  • Its dealers, worldwide
  • Its stand at HIGH END
EARF brings
  • The plinth and platter, designed in cast acrylic
  • The tints, and approval of each cast
  • The US launch: a listening event, a Session record
  • Direct sales, and the design press

How it could work.

An outline to discuss, not an offer. The ranges are those typical in published sources for licensed and co-branded design (furniture, lighting, consumer products); none of it is agreed, and every line is for counsel.

TermIn outline
StructureA licence: Pro-Ject manufactures; EARF licenses the design and the tint. EARF buys units at a transfer price for its direct sales. for counsel
Royalty3–7% of net wholesale on Pro-Ject’s sales, 5% common; net defined. for counsel
FeeA development fee, recoupable; a minimum guarantee from the second year. for counsel
Edition50–100 numbered per tint, in a few tints; the edition capped. for counsel
ControlEARF approves colour, material and finish; the tint system stays EARF’s own, and exclusivity covers this edition only. for counsel
Term1–3 years; sell-off 30–180 days; reversion if not launched in 12–18 months, or if production stops. for counsel
PricesA suggested retail price only; channel terms, not resale prices (California). for counsel
ReportsQuarterly statements; audit rights. for counsel
