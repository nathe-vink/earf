Brand lane, r47 (2026-10-31). **Drafts only; none has been sent, and no form has been filled in.** The owner sends
them, ideally in the week of 2 November, so the queue is known before the artist is booked.

**Rainbo Records is closed.** It stopped pressing around the turn of 2020 after a rent rise, and United Record Pressing
bought its equipment and name ([Hypebot](https://www.hypebot.com/vinyl-manufacturer-rainbo-records-has-closed-its-doors/)).

[Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2024/04/25/fidelity-record-pressing-hosting-open-house-at-new-oxnard-facility/)),
but it publishes no colour options; it is listed at the end as a fifth request. Gotta Groove says it doesn't press 180 g
in colour, so it's left out.

No plant publishes its current queue. Plan on **four months from lacquers or files to records** (URP's own figure).
That makes the master's date the critical one (`README.md`, Timeline).

**The colour to ask for.** A *translucent* tint matched to EARF's mark colours:
- **Ember:** #D2382F. The Pantone named in the requests ([7621 C]) is a guess: confirm against a swatch book before sending. Ask the plant for its nearest translucent red, and for a match.
- **Smoke:** #34323A, a dark smoky grey. A translucent "smoke" or "Black Ice" reads as the dark acrylic.

The owner chooses one; Ember is the likelier match from stock (Ruby, T-4, Pirates's red).

## Mastering and cutting (for the plants that want lacquers)

Published rates for lacquer cutting, each side of a 12" (read 31 October 2026):

| Studio | Per side | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Chicago Mastering Service | $235 | from a search excerpt; the page now returns 404 |
| Bonati Mastering | $250 | cuts only projects it also masters |
| Golden Mastering (Ventura, CA) | $285 | reference acetates $90 a side, "may not be available" (a lacquer shortage) |
| Marsh Mastering | $400 | |

That's about $470 to $800 for two sides. DMM from files is included in Precision's price and offered by URP.

**Recommended:** the plant's own cut (DMM or lacquer), from a mix mastered for vinyl. It's one less hand-off, and the
lacquer shortage Golden mentions is a real risk.
