## 1. The format

**The night.** The debut, Tuesday 19 January 2027, in the hired or lent room at or near Fort Mason (venue not yet
named). **Session 00 opens the evening:**
- one album's worth of music, 35 to 50 minutes, played start to finish;
- a seated audience of [30 to 60] invited guests, the doors held from the first note to the last;
- then the room opens, and the party starts.

The house rule from the bar's book holds: no talking during the record.

**The record.** That set, mixed and mastered for vinyl, pressed as **300 numbered LPs, 180 g, in a translucent tint**
(Ember, or Smoke; the owner chooses), in a printed jacket with the room on the front. The jacket's line:
"Recorded live at the EARF debut, San Francisco, 19 January 2027."

**"Through the first Tropos pair": only if it's true.**
- **Literally true** when the artist's sound goes through the pair: an electronic or ambient set, or amplified
  instruments, the pair acting as the room's sound system. Mics in the room record what the pair puts into it, and
  direct feeds go to the multitrack beside them. Then the jacket can say "through the first Tropos pair".
- **Not true** for an acoustic jazz trio, which plays to the room and needs no system. The record is "recorded live at
  the EARF debut", and the pair's part is the playback of the finished record, at the next Session.

So the artist choice decides the claim. The two that make it literally true are first in the shortlist.

**And if the pair isn't ready on the night**, the Session still happens and is still recorded. Only the line on the
jacket changes.

Silent Way (SF) does live remote multitrack too, but its site returned 503 and wasn't retried.

**Recommended:** Wildplum. Its redundant rigs and splitter are exactly what a once-only night needs. Different Fur is the
budget option. Budget about $1,950 to record (Wildplum's 24-track midpoint, est.).

**What the night needs**, from the engineers' published practice and the trade press (SOS, ProSoundWeb, Live Sound
International):
- **Microphones.** A main stereo pair for the room picture (ORTF or spaced omnis), close spots on each source, and a room
  or audience pair placed beside or behind the loudspeakers, using their rear rejection.
- **A multitrack**, 16 to 24 tracks at 24-bit / 96 kHz, and a **second recorder** on the same splitter, on UPS power.
  Redundancy is "vital".
- **A transformer-isolated or active splitter**, so the recorder and the pair each get their own feed, without ground
  loops.
- **The pair in the room.**
  - If the artist plays through Tropos, the pair is in every mic. Keep its level modest, mic close, and record the
    pair's feed on its own track so the mix can manage the bleed.
  - If the artist is acoustic, the pair stays silent during the set.
- **Room noise.**
  - HVAC, refrigerators and ice machines off for the set; check the street; doors held.
  - The audience asked for quiet, as the Session's rule asks anyway.
  - Two minutes of room tone recorded, and applause on its own tracks.
- **A soundcheck** in the room at the same hour, with the HVAC as it will be, and the pair at show level. Check the phase
  between the spots and the main pair.
- **For vinyl.**
  - The mix goes to a mastering engineer who cuts (or to the plant's own DMM). They'll watch sibilance, low-end phase,
    and side length: about 18 to 22 minutes a side for good level.
  - So **the set should run 36 to 44 minutes**, in two halves that each make a side.

## 4. The rights: summary (full outline in `RIGHTS.md`, for counsel)

- **The artist keeps the master.** EARF takes an exclusive licence for the vinyl edition and its download, for [5] years.
- **The artist approves the mix**, and is paid a fee for the night (not recouped).
- **The artist's share:** either 50% of net after costs, or **$6 a record from the first copy**. The second is simpler for an edition this small.
- **Mechanicals** for every song pressed:
  - at the statutory **13.1 cents a song a copy** (2026);
  - covers through **HFA Songfile**, at "$16 processing fee per song license" ($14 each beyond five), not per unit.
  - About $120 to $550 at 300 copies.
- **To clear as well:**
  - the room's permission to record;
  - the engineer's work-for-hire;
  - the audience's notice;
  - any union rules.

## 5. The pressing: summary (details and the requests in `QUOTES.md`)

- **Rainbo is closed** (since about 2020). The requests go to **Pirates Press** (Emeryville: Pantone matching on
  request), **Precision** (the only published all-in price: translucent colour, 300 for $2,396, 500 for $3,067,
  standard weight), **United Record Pressing** (custom translucent colour +$0.99 a record; "allow four months") and
  **Hand Drawn** (Dallas). Fidelity in Oxnard is a fifth.
- **The queue is the risk.** No plant publishes one, and URP says four months. **The master must be at the plant by
  about 15 February** for records by late April, and only a fast plant makes that.
