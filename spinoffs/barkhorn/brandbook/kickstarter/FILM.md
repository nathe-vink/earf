# The campaign film: storyboard and shot list

Brand lane, r47 (2026-10-31). A draft for the owner. **Real prototypes only:**
- no renders, no CGI and no simulated light;
- nothing the prototypes can't do yet (Kickstarter: working prototypes, no photorealistic renderings; `README.md` §1).

Shoot it in the week the lamps are photographed (mid-February 2027; `first-articles.md`).

**Length:** about 2:00 (est.); the first 10 seconds carry the page.
**Sound:** room sound and one track played through Tropos, if the first pair plays by then. Otherwise a record on any
good system, credited. No voice-over until 0:40; then the owner, on camera or off, in their own words (lines below
are prompts, not a script to read).
**Look:** the Brand Book's: dark rooms, one light source, the tint doing the work, no grading toward teal and orange.
**Crew:** the owner and one camera operator (est. $3,000 for two days' shooting and an edit, in `ECONOMICS.md`).
**Kit:** a camera that shoots 4K at 24 fps, a 35 mm and an 85 mm lens, a slider, a tripod, a small LED panel bounced
for faces only (never on the lamps), and a lavalier.

**The film shows (each claim filmed working):**
- cordless;
- the quarter turn;
- dimming;
- the tints;
- the making;
- the tests.

**It does not show:**
- Tropos, unless the first pair plays by then;
- any tint not yet cast;
- any claim not measured on the prototype.
