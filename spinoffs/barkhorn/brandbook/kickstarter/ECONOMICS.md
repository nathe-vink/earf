## The goal (est.)

it, from the pre-seed; the run's share of them is in each unit above, so it returns to the business.

The margin is about **47% of every dollar pledged** at a run of 50 (est.). To carry the fixed costs, the
campaign must raise about **$106,104** in pledges.

**Proposed goal: $110,000** (est.), about 110 backers at the mix's average pledge of $997.
Below it, a run of lamps would be made and the listings left unpaid, so the campaign should not fund. Above it, the
run grows toward 100 of each, the cost of a unit falls (the second margin table) and every lamp adds margin.

- If the labs quote R3's low end ($18,500), the goal is about $75,000.
- If the labs quote R3's high end ($47,000), the goal is about $145,000.
- If the pre-seed pays for the two UL listings (midpoints $14,000 and $10,500), the goal is about $50,000.

R2's customer-funded path aims at $150k to $500k for the lamps; the goal is the floor, not the target.
