## FOG week without a show (21–24 January), either date

- **Book half-hour meetings, not a party.** The six gallery letters (`money/GALLERY-LETTERS.md`) already ask for "half
  an hour in FOG week". Re-date their invitations to the spring debut, and keep the ask.
- **Bring what exists:**
  - Mantle (likely working by then; `first-articles.md`);
  - the cast tint samples;
  - the drawings;
  - a lamp if one is ready.
- **Show the pair to a few, by appointment, if it's playing.** The pair could be in a borrowed room for an afternoon.
  It's the strongest thing to show a dealer, but keep it private, with no pictures, so the spring debut stays the
  first time (est.).
- **Collect names** for the debut and the waiting list. Every gallery met is on the spring invitation.
- **No press.** The FOG-week note (`press/releases/LOCAL-AND-FOG.md`) becomes a quiet meeting request, or isn't sent.

## Recommendation

**Thursday 18 March.**
- **The press:** the debut, the photographs and the release land together, the morning after the night or at the
  start of the media plan's 22–29 March window.
- **Milan and Vienna:** the partner packs go out with real pictures a month before Milan, and Vienna sits in the
  Kickstarter's middle week.
- **The Kickstarter:** it keeps 27 April, five weeks after the debut's press, so the campaign opens on coverage rather
  than ahead of it.
- **Easter and the schedule:** it avoids Easter week, and leaves four weeks between the mid-February shoot and the night.

**The risk:** the Tropos pair is assembled about 5–12 February (horn lane ec252318) and must be measured by early March. The shoot holds; if the pair slips, only its photographs move a week (the fallback above). If the pair slips
past February, 29 April buys a month. But it stacks the debut, a re-dated Kickstarter (11 May – 10 June), Vienna and
the press into one fortnight, and it misses the planned press window.
