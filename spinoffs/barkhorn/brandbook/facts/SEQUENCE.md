## FOG week without a show (21–24 January), either date

- **Book half-hour meetings, not a party.** The six gallery letters (`money/GALLERY-LETTERS.md`) already ask for "half
  an hour in FOG week". Re-date their invitations to the spring debut, and keep the ask.
- **Bring what exists:**
  - Mantle (likely working by then; `first-articles.md`);
  - the cast tint samples;
  - the drawings;
  - a lamp if one is ready.
- **Show the pair to a few, by appointment, if it's playing.** The pair could be in a borrowed room for an afternoon.
  It's the strongest thing to show a dealer, but keep it private, with no pictures, so the spring debut stays the
  first time (est.).
- **Collect names** for the debut and the waiting list. Every gallery met is on the spring invitation.
- **No press.** The FOG-week note (`press/releases/LOCAL-AND-FOG.md`) becomes a quiet meeting request, or isn't sent.

## Recommendation

**Thursday 25 March.**
- **The press:** it lands the debut, the photographs and the release in the media plan's own online window (22–29
  March).
- **Milan and Vienna:** the partner packs go out with real pictures before Milan, and Vienna sits in the Kickstarter's
  middle week.
- **The Kickstarter:** it keeps 27 April, a month after the debut's press, so the campaign opens on coverage rather
  than ahead of it.
- **The schedule:** it still leaves about five weeks between the mid-February shoot and the night.

**The risks:**
- It's the Thursday before Easter, so some guests will be away. If that matters, **Tuesday 23 or Wednesday 24 March**
  keeps every other date.
- The pair must be playing and measured by mid-March.

29 April buys a month of build time. But it stacks the debut, a re-dated Kickstarter, Vienna and the press into one
fortnight, and it misses the planned press window. Choose it only if the Tropos pair slips past February.
