The Founding Hundred · proposed terms

The first hundred pairs of Tropos, reserved before they exist.

EARF, [legal name and form, not yet registered], San Francisco, proposes to take refundable reservations, then deposits, for the first hundred pairs of Tropos, a powered horn loudspeaker, made to order in one of eight colours. Each pair is numbered on its rating plates (FOUNDING 001–100 / 100). We ask counsel to confirm or correct each term below, and to settle the points marked Q.
