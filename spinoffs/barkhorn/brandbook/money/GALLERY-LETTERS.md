# Six gallery letters: an invitation, and a numbered edition

Brand lane, r47 (2026-10-31). **Drafts only, for the coordinator to turn into Gmail drafts; nothing has been sent.**
Each goes to the gallery's published address (seen on its own site, 31 October 2026, `../launch/INVITE-LIST.md`). A
named person replaces "[Name]" only where the gallery publishes one; otherwise keep the gallery's name.

**The edition proposed** (the market and pricing research's gallery guidance, r46-owner-notes 97ca003e,
`R4-market-pricing.md` §2.4, all estimates, not in the letters): Hemisphere, large format, an **edition of 8 + 2 AP**,
one in each tint, suggested gallery price about **$9,000** (est.), EARF's net about $4,500 at 50% consignment; Tropos as a
**unique colourway pair**, about **$80,000** (est.); Mantle, **unique**, about **$24,000** (est.). Galleries typically
sell on consignment at around 50% and rarely advance production money to an unknown maker (R2 §4.7). The letters name
the edition's shape, not its price: price comes in the conversation, once the gallery has seen the pieces.

**Before sending:** a finished Hemisphere and the Tropos pair should exist or have a date, and an edition sheet (size,
colours, prices, lead time) should be ready to follow the first reply (R2 §4.7).
