EARF makes hi-fi and light in cast acrylic, tinted all the way through, in eight colours. On 19 January the first pair of Tropos, our horn loudspeaker, plays for the first time, in an eight-week listening room in San Francisco that opens that night. We are looking for three partners to make the night, and the weeks after it, theirs as much as ours: a hotel, a drinks brand, and a record label or shop.

What a partner gets · what we ask

One night, eight weeks, and the first pictures that aren't renders.

Who will be in the room

Collectors and dealers in town for FOG Design+Art: galleries from its 2027 list (Marta, Patrick Parrish, Hostler Burrows, Gallery FUMI and others). San Francisco's design studios and showrooms. The city's hi-fi shops and listening bars. Record labels and shops. Hotel and hospitality groups. And the design, city and hi-fi press, briefed for a spring launch.

What every partner gets

  • Named on the printed invitation and the email (both drafted)
  • A Session of its own: one album, start to finish, in the listening room, under its name
  • Its product in the room, all eight weeks, where the guests are
  • Named in the spring press release and the fact sheet
  • Use of the night's photographs and the teaser film
  • A first conversation about an edition in one of the eight colours (est.; nothing promised)
A hotel

Near Fort Mason, Union Square or Jackson Square

  • Rooms for visiting guests and press in FOG week
  • A room for the after-hours Session, or the pop-up's AV
  • Later: a listening suite, fitted by EARF
Ask: $5k–15k cash, or rooms and space in kind (est. value $3k–10k)
A drinks brand

Spirits, or non-alcoholic

  • The night's drinks, named, through a licensed caterer
  • A drink for each tint, Ember to Smoke
  • Its bottles on the bar for the catered Fridays
Ask: product and bartending in kind (est. value $2k–8k), or cash, structured with counsel
A record label or shop

Bay Area first

  • The room's records, chosen with them
  • A selector or artist set on the night
  • The first Session, possibly recorded and pressed (EARF Sessions)
Ask: records, an artist set and a Session in kind; or $5k toward the pressing

Drinks and the law. California's tied-house rules bar alcohol makers and wholesalers from furnishing money or things of value to on-sale licensees, with narrow exceptions (B&P §25500). Any spirits partnership is structured with counsel, and drinks on the night are served through a licensed caterer (the pop-up's plan uses Type 58 caterer nights). A non-alcoholic partner avoids the question.
