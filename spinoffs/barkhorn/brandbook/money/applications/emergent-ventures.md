# Emergent Ventures: the questions, and why there is no draft here

**Not drafted, on purpose.** Emergent Ventures' application page (mercatus.org/emergentventures/application, read
31 October 2026) says it no longer accepts AI-written applications, that submissions are tested, and that any using AI
for anything other than proofreading will be rejected. So this file lists the form's questions and the facts the owner
can draw on, and the owner writes the proposal in their own words.

**Fit, honestly: low.** The form's consent asks the applicant to confirm that the funds go to charitable, scientific or
educational purposes, with no lobbying or political activity. A grant toward a commercial product's prototypes doesn't
obviously meet that. If the owner applies, the project would need to be one with a public, educational or scientific
outcome, for example publishing the horn's simulation and the acrylic cabinet's acoustic method openly. The funding
research rates EV "a long shot (est.)" (R2 §4.6). One application per applicant and project.
