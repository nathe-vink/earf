# IDSA IDEA 2027, Concepts & Speculative Design: what we'd need

**Not drafted: the 2027 form isn't open.** idsa.org/awards-recognition/idea (read 31 October 2026) says "Entries open
early 2027" and gives no 2027 deadlines, fees or form. Its rules and FAQs hold the required materials and word limits;
read them when the cycle opens. The 2026 cycle is the guide below.

**The category.** Concepts & Speculative Design: "any product, service, or experience that does not yet exist in
production". Tropos, Hemisphere, Core and Mantle all qualify until they ship. Consumer Technology (audio) and Furniture
& Lighting are the alternatives once in production (R5 §4.5).

**Judged on:** design innovation, benefit to user, benefit to client or brand, benefit to society, appropriate aesthetics.

**2026 timing and fees (the guide for 2027):** Round 1 regular 5 Jan – 13 Feb, late to 16 Mar; Round 2 17 Apr – 4 May,
notification by 8 Jun. Non-member professional: $725 regular, $985 late, $815 for Round 2; IDSA members $435 / $660 /
$575. Round 2 must be completed and paid to be a finalist. The Impact Award is a $100 add-on.

## What to have ready by early January

- [ ] Which product, and which category: Tropos as a concept (the strongest story: the horn formed from its own acrylic)
- [ ] A short description against each of the five criteria (the brand book's position and the fact sheets are the source)
- [ ] Images: the prototype's photographs (February) or the renders, labelled as renders, as the rules allow
- [ ] Drawings and the process: the horn simulation, the parts drawn for making
- [ ] A short film: the teaser (`../../launch/out/earf-teaser-16x9.mp4`) or a making-of
- [ ] The entrant: the company's name and registration (see the HIGH END note: not yet registered)
- [ ] The fee, est. $725–$985 for Round 1 and $815 for Round 2 at non-member rates; IDSA membership may pay for itself
- [ ] The rules and FAQs read once published, for the word limits and file specifications
