# HIGH END Vienna, Start-Up Area: application draft

> **Blocked until a company is registered.** The Start-Up Area takes companies on an official register (preferably
> registered within the last 24 months) and asks for proof: a copy of the business registration and documents showing
> the legal form in which the company operates in consumer electronics. **The owner has not registered a company yet.**
> The funding research's first-week list has the steps (R2 §5: the FBN, the SF Treasurer's registration, an EIN, and a
> Delaware C-corp if a seed round is planned). **Applications close 31 December 2026.**

What it is (highendsociety.de/start-up-358.html, read 31 October 2026): a free complete exhibition stand at HIGH END,
**6–9 May 2027, Austria Center Vienna**, for up to ten young companies; an individual mention in the press releases and
the exhibition magazine; the same treatment as regular exhibitors. Travel, hotel and extra advertising are not covered.
The organiser alone decides; no appeal. Prototypes are allowed (events research, R5 §2).

**How to apply:** by email to alex[at]highendsociety.de, with the registration documents. The page asks for nothing
else, so the email can be short; the draft below adds what a juror would want to know.

---

**To:** alex@highendsociety.de
**Subject:** Start-Up Area 2027 application: EARF, San Francisco (horn loudspeakers in tinted cast acrylic)

Dear Alex,

I'd like to apply for a place in the Start-Up Area at HIGH END 2027 in Vienna.

EARF is a young company in San Francisco, registered on [date of registration] as [legal form and name]. We make hi-fi and
lighting in cast acrylic, tinted all the way through, in eight colours. Our first product is Tropos, a powered two-way
horn loudspeaker: a 2-inch compression driver on a horn formed from the same tinted acrylic as its 20 mm cabinet, a
15-inch woofer, and a Hypex FusionAmp built in. Mantle, a record player in the same acrylic, would stand beside it.

The first pair is built in January and opens a listening room in San Francisco on 19 January 2027. By May it will have
been measured, and Vienna would be its first showing in Europe and its first in front of the hi-fi trade.

Attached, as the Start-Up page asks: a copy of our business registration, and [documents showing the legal form and
our activity in consumer electronics]. I've also attached a one-page fact sheet and a few pictures (renders until the
prototypes are photographed in February).

Thank you for considering us.

With best wishes,
[Your name]
EARF · earf.world · [contact line]

---
