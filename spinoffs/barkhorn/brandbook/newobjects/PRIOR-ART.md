**Rules that follow:**
- Thin, countable, hard-edged layers with visible bond lines; no polished monoliths, no ombré, no rotated stacks, no
  neon, nothing embedded.
- Names clear of Skyline, Totem, Voie, Stacking, Corrugated and Strata.
