## Moho: a record weight

*The boundary between the crust and the mantle; it sits on a record, on Mantle or any turntable.*

Four discs of half-inch cast sheet, bonded into one puck 66 mm across and 51 mm tall, each layer a different tint, with a brass core you see through the color. It sits on the label over the spindle and presses the record flat. Turned and polished, it shows its strata at the edge like a sawn core; the order of the tints is chosen at the shop, from the line's eight. Its blanks are the discs left over from boring Lithos's rings, so it costs almost nothing in sheet.

## Lithos: a floor lamp, a core sample

*Rock, in layers: a drill core stood on end.*

A column 110 mm across and 1.12 m tall, made of 87 rings of half-inch cast sheet at Core Sample's 12.7 mm pitch in eight bands of the line's tints, in uneven thicknesses like a real core. Between every band sits a quarter-inch layer of near-clear 3030 sheet. Its light is Core Sample's own filament (the lamps lane's table lamp, which Lithos is the big sibling of): a dim-to-warm LED strip on an aluminum spine inside an opal tube, here masked except where the clear layers are, so light enters the clear layers and nothing else. As on Core Sample, the bands alternate buffed clear and bead-blasted satin; their polished outer edges glow: **the boundaries between the strata are lines of light**, while the tinted bands stay deep (a deep tint lit from inside through 17 mm would barely glow: TruCast's 2423 passes 14% at 3.2 mm). An LED uplight in the top of the bore washes the ceiling, which is the light you read by. The column stands on a blackened steel disc.

## Ionos: a radio and streamer

*The ionosphere carries radio round the curve of the Earth.*

A block of nine bonded layers of one tint, 200 × 110 × 115 mm, with a near-clear layer at its middle lit from inside, so a single line of light runs round it. You tune by turning the top: the uppermost disc, 100 mm across, turns on a bearing, clicking from station to station, and the line of light brightens as a station locks. Push the disc to play or pause. It streams and plays internet radio into Tropos or any amplifier, through line, optical and coaxial outputs on an aluminum plate at the back. No screen, no speaker, no handle.

## Cairn: bookends

*Stones stacked to mark a way.*

Five slabs of half-inch sheet, each smaller than the one below and set a little off its center, bonded into a stepped stack like stones on a trail. Two tints to a pair, never a rainbow: Fog with one layer of Dust, say, or Smoke with one of Acid. The slabs' faces are the sheet's own polish and their sides are flame-polished, so the stack reads as layered glass, the Dust running through it. A stainless tongue under the bottom slab goes under the books and holds them.

## Meridian: a wall clock

*The line from pole to pole by which we tell the time.*

Two tinted discs tell the time. An Acid disc, 110 mm across, rides on the hour arm and an Ice disc on the minute arm, each balanced by a brass weight across the center, in front of a near-clear face 360 mm across with twelve marks cut into it. Where the two discs cross, yellow and blue make green, the line's Poison: a lens of a third color that grows and shrinks as the hands meet and part, largest when the hands meet, as at a quarter past three, and gone at half past twelve. A quiet stepper behind the face turns them; there's no tick.
