# Photographed textures used by the new-object scenes

All CC0 (public domain) from Poly Haven, fetched at 2k by `fetch_textures.py` through `studio/engine/assets.py` into
`studio/assets/cache/`, which is gitignored: no texture files are committed. Each set is laid at its real size.

| Set | Used for | Source | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| oak_wood_planks | oak floors, the table and the sideboard | https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_wood_planks | CC0 |
| beige_wall_001 | plaster walls | https://polyhaven.com/a/beige_wall_001 | CC0 |
| marble_01 | the honed stone top under Ionos | https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01 | CC0 |
| book_pattern | the books' cloth | https://polyhaven.com/a/book_pattern | CC0 |

The record's vinyl grooves are procedural (concentric rings as a fine bump with an anisotropic sheen), not a texture.
