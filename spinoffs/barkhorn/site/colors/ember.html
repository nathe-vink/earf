Details

The particulars

Height
1295 mm
Width
560 mm, the horn flush with the cabinet
Depth
428 mm overall, 420 mm at the cabinet
Weight
About 73 kg; about 64 kg with the horn lifted off (modeled)
Woofer
FaitalPRO 15PR400, 15-inch, with two ports in the front’s lower corners
Horn driver
B&C DE1085TN, 2-inch compression driver
Horn
A smooth horn thermoformed from 10 mm cast acrylic in the speaker’s own color, polished inside and out, on four black posts
Amplifier
Hypex FusionAmp FA122, built in: 125 W a channel into 4 Ω, about 75 W into the 8 Ω woofer; crossed at 1 kHz in its DSP
Bass
To about 51 Hz at −3 dB, before the room adds its own; the ports tuned to 38 Hz
Loudness
Up to about 115 dB at 1 m, each
Inputs
XLR (and a link out), RCA, AES, S/PDIF coaxial and optical; USB to set it up
Lining
Polyester batts in white voile, 100 mm at the top and the bottom
Price
On request. Each pair is made to order.
Care
Dust with a soft microfiber cloth; wash with water and a drop of mild soap, then dry. No glass cleaner, alcohol or solvents: they craze acrylic. Fine scratches polish out.
Figures
Design figures, from the drawings and their simulation; measured ones follow with the first pair

Lighting

Lighting

Two lamps in the speakers’ acrylic.

A pendant and a table lamp, each with a white opal diffuser inside its color, so the color glows and the source never shows. Every shade comes off by hand.

HemispherePendant

Hung low over a table. A quarter turn takes its shade off, and another puts a new color on.

See Hemisphere

CoreTable lamp

A ball of tinted acrylic sitting low in a black acrylic base. No cord: it goes where the conversation is.

See Core

Cordless.

About five hours a charge at full brightness, longer dimmed. Set it back on its dock, a disc of polished black acrylic, and it charges in two to three hours. When the cells tire, you change the cells, not the lamp.
