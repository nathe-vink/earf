The horn

The oldest amplifier is a shape.

Long before there were amplifiers, a horn was how a small sound filled a large room: the gramophone’s, the megaphone’s, a cupped hand’s. Ours is cast in resin tinted to match its cabinet, and its driver lives in a pod whose cap twists off by hand.

Details

The particulars

Height
1293 mm
Width
625 mm at the horn, 560 mm at the cabinet
Depth
420 mm
Weight
About 72 kg; 62 kg with the horn lifted off
Woofer
15-inch, with two ports in the front's lower corners
Horn driver
2-inch compression driver
Horn
Cast resin, the cabinet's full depth, 420 mm
Amplifier
Hypex FusionAmp FA122, built in: two channels, crossed at 800 Hz
Inputs
XLR (and a link out), RCA, AES, S/PDIF coaxial and optical; USB to set it up
Lining
Recycled polyester batts in white voile, 100 mm at the top and the bottom
