# R7: Polymerising cement, 5-axis CNC and the Hypex FA122 for the first Tropos pair

**Status:** research memo, public web only, as of 31 Oct 2026. Nobody was contacted, no forms were filled in and no accounts were created.

**Conventions**
- Quotes in "double quotes" are the shop's or maker's own words, as the page returned them on 31 Oct 2026.
- **(est.)** marks my estimates and arithmetic. **(guess)** marks judgement calls.
- **(snippet)** marks a claim taken from a search-engine result when the page itself would not load. Treat these as leads to confirm, not facts.

**Context:** debut on 19–21 Jan 2027. Each Tropos has:
- a 20 mm acrylic cabinet cemented with two-part polymerising cement;
- a laminated horn of 22 layers of ½" tinted cell-cast acrylic, CNC-cut, bonded, annealed, then 5-axis machined and polished. The horn is about 630 × 366 × 238 mm.

**Two schedule risks surfaced:**
- **Annealing a ~290 mm laminated block.** Röhm's rule (thickness/3 h soak, thickness/4 h cool, ramp ≤10 °C/h) gives about **7 days per oven cycle** if the stack counts as solid. It is about 2 days if the thickest wall is ~60 mm (est.).
- **Glue-line height.** Glue lines add **≈10–30 mm** to the stack height (21 joints × 0.5–1.5 mm, est.).

### 1.4 Bonding in-house: what it would take

#### What the makers say the process needs

**Röhm/Polyvantis guideline 311-3 (07/24) and the ACRIFIX 2R 0190 data sheet (391-19, 06/24)**

These are the European documents for PLEXIGLAS® GS, the equivalent of cell-cast sheet.

- **Cement and strength:**
  - ACRIFIX 2R 0190 is a "2-Component Polymerization Adhesive". Mix in "3 to 6 % ACRIFIX® CA 0020".
  - Pot life is about 25 min at 3% (200 g, 20 °C).
  - Flash point is about 10 °C; UN 1133 ([TDS](https://www.zenit.cz/public/media/Plasty/Zenit_Technicky_List_Plasty_Lepidla_Acrifix_2R-190_EN.pdf)).
  - Shear strength on GS: 28–38 MPa unannealed, 47–57 MPa after "5 hrs at 80 °C" ([TDS](https://www.zenit.cz/public/media/Plasty/Zenit_Technicky_List_Plasty_Lepidla_Acrifix_2R-190_EN.pdf)).
  - Polymerisation adhesives give "bonds of high optical quality with up to 75% of the inherent strength of PLEXIGLAS®" ([311-3](https://www.plexiglas.de/files/plexiglas-content/pdf/verarbeitungsrichtlinie/311-3-EN-joining-PLEXIGLAS.pdf)).
- **Degassing:**
  - Use a vacuum desiccator at "negative pressure of about 0.8 bar … absolute pressure is approx. 0.2 bar".
  - "The absolute pressure must never fall below this value, because otherwise the adhesive will foam."
  - Fit a bleed valve between pump and desiccator ([311-3](https://www.plexiglas.de/files/plexiglas-content/pdf/verarbeitungsrichtlinie/311-3-EN-joining-PLEXIGLAS.pdf)).
- **Area (laminate) bonds:**
  - "Thick, i.e. heavy, cover panels and blocks … should be kept at about 0.5 to 1.5 mm from the base panel by suitable elastic spacers such as polyethylene cords."
  - Prick bubbles with a fine stainless wire.
  - Use moderator ACRIFIX MO 0070 (≤0.3%) for layers over 1.5 mm.
  - Aim for uniform layer thickness, so that polymerisation runs "at the same rate over the entire area" ([311-3](https://www.plexiglas.de/files/plexiglas-content/pdf/verarbeitungsrichtlinie/311-3-EN-joining-PLEXIGLAS.pdf)).
- **Butt joints (the 20 mm cabinet):**
  - "The optimum gap width is 2 mm, but it should not be less than 0.5 mm and not larger than 3 mm."
  - Overfill, because "the shrinkage is 15 to 20% by volume".
  - "For thicknesses > 20 mm, an additional reservoir should be provided" ([311-3](https://www.plexiglas.de/files/plexiglas-content/pdf/verarbeitungsrichtlinie/311-3-EN-joining-PLEXIGLAS.pdf)).
- **Annealing:**
  - **Before bonding:** cast GS parts "do not normally have to be annealed" before bonding. But to rule out cracking, 70–80 °C pre-annealing "may therefore be required … to relieve stress generated during sawing, milling, polishing". Check CNC-cut layers in a polariscope or with the solvent test ([311-3](https://www.plexiglas.de/files/plexiglas-content/pdf/verarbeitungsrichtlinie/311-3-EN-joining-PLEXIGLAS.pdf)).
  - **Conditions:** GS at 80 °C. "The material thickness in mm divided by 3 is the annealing time in hours, but the minimum is 2 hours."
  - **Cooling:** cooling time "in hours is the material thickness … in mm divided by 4", at no more than 15 °C/h. Remove at ≤35 °C.
  - **After bonding:** anneal "within 24 hours of bonding". Heat bonded work "no faster than 10 °C per hour. Otherwise there is a risk of bubbles forming in the joint" ([311-3](https://www.plexiglas.de/files/plexiglas-content/pdf/verarbeitungsrichtlinie/311-3-EN-joining-PLEXIGLAS.pdf)).
  - The data sheet adds that temperatures "> 70°C could also create yellowing" of the bond ([TDS](https://www.zenit.cz/public/media/Plasty/Zenit_Technicky_List_Plasty_Lepidla_Acrifix_2R-190_EN.pdf)).
- **Colour:**
  - Adhesives can make coloured sheet bleed, and "highly pigmented parts … may show reduced bond strength".
  - ACRIFIX CO colourants can tint the cement to match the sheet ([311-3](https://www.plexiglas.de/files/plexiglas-content/pdf/verarbeitungsrichtlinie/311-3-EN-joining-PLEXIGLAS.pdf), §2.6).
- **US equivalent (ACRYLITE / POLYVANTIS Sanford LLC):**
  - Reactive cement brief 1591E, 08/24 ([PDF](https://www.acrylite.co/files/content/acrylite.co/documents/fabrication-briefs/ACRYLITE-Reactive-Cement-Fabrication-Brief.pdf)).
  - "Polymerizable cement" page: flat glass or polyester working surface; scale accurate to about 1 g; optional vacuum chamber; jigs; polyester tape ([ACRYLITE](https://www.acrylite.co/resources/fabrication-manuals/acrylite-cell-cast-polymerizable-cement)).
  - The brief says: "A bonded part should be heat conditioned (annealed) before finishing the adhesive joint" ([brief 1591E](https://www.acrylite.co/files/content/acrylite.co/documents/fabrication-briefs/ACRYLITE-Reactive-Cement-Fabrication-Brief.pdf)).

**SCIGRIP Weld-On 40 and 42 (US)**
- **Weld-On 40:**
  - A "2-component, clear, medium syrupy, reactive acrylic cement". Mix "Per 100cc or grams of Comp. A add 5cc or 5 grams of Comp. B".
  - Pot life about 20 min at 75 °F. Handling strength in about 4 h at 70 °F; machine after 24 h ([ePlastics](https://www.eplastics.com/ips40-pt)).
  - Full cure 72 h; VOC ≤250 g/L ([Chemical Concepts](https://www.chemical-concepts.com/product/weld-on-40-reactive-acrylic-adhesive/)).
- **Weld-On 42:**
  - 10:1, in a 230 ml cartridge for a two-component gun with static mixer.
  - "For maximum strength, the joint should be cured for 4 hours at 158°F" ([ePlastics](https://www.eplastics.com/PLEXIGLASS-GLUE-ADHESIVE-WELD-ON-42-IPS-42-230ML-Cartridge)).

**Tru Vue case-fabrication guideline**
- Two-part cements are "recommended for the best joints". It names Weld-On 40 and Caseway PS-30.
- Its equipment list: "a cement dispensing gun", or a scale accurate to one gram, plus "A glass vacuum chamber, along with a vacuum pump, capable of evacuating the chamber to a pressure of -11 to -12 psi"; sealing with 3M #685 tape ([Tru Vue 2023](https://tru-vue.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/5/2025/10/OMA_Case_Fab_Guidelines-English.pdf)).
- Its annealing numbers are for extruded Optium, so don't use them for cell-cast.

#### Applying this to the Tropos horn (est., to confirm with a fabricator)

**Glue-line height.** 21 joints × 0.5–1.5 mm gives about 10–30 mm of extra stack height before 15–20% shrinkage (est.). Make sure the CAD stack and the 238 mm finished height allow for it. Measure the real sheet thickness too; cell-cast ½" varies within ASTM D4802 bands (see R6 §1.1).

**Cement volume.** Bounding area per layer is 63 × 36.6 cm ≈ 2,306 cm². If 50–80% of that is solid and the glue line is 0.5–1.5 mm:
- about 60–280 ml per joint;
- about 1.2–5.8 L per horn;
- about 3–15 L per pair with ~30% waste (all est.).

Röhm's own sizing rule for laminating is "length [cm] x width [cm] x 0.1" ml, about a 1 mm layer ([311-3](https://www.plexiglas.de/files/plexiglas-content/pdf/verarbeitungsrichtlinie/311-3-EN-joining-PLEXIGLAS.pdf)).

**Oven time (est.):**

| Step | Basis | Approx. cycle |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-anneal CNC-cut ½" layers (if the stress test fails) | 12.7 mm/3 = 4.2 h soak; 12.7/4 = 3.2 h cool | ≈ 1 working day |
| Post-bond anneal, if the stack counts as solid (~290 mm) | 6 h ramp at ≤10 °C/h + 97 h soak + 73 h cool | **≈ 7 days** |
| Post-bond anneal, if the thickest wall is ~60 mm | 6 + 20 + 15 h | ≈ 2 days |
| Post-machining/polish anneal | Same as the line above, by wall thickness | 2–7 days |

**Risk.** Two long anneals could take 1–2 weeks of the December window. Ask Polyvantis or the fabricator whether the thickness rule applies to the whole stack or to wall thickness.

**Registration.** Cut dowel holes into each layer (all 22) in the same CNC program (guess, a design suggestion). The stack then self-aligns on stainless pins while the cement is still fluid.

#### Safety and compliance
- **Exposure limits (MMA):** the Weld-On 40 SDS lists MMA at 40–65% and benzoyl peroxide at <15%. Limits:
  - ACGIH TLV 50 ppm (8 h) and 100 ppm STEL;
  - OSHA PEL 100 ppm ([Weld-On 40 SDS](https://www.acplasticsinc.com/media/Weld-On%2040%20SDS.pdf), snippet; the PDF returned 404 on fetch);
  - California's Title 8 PEL is reported as **50 ppm** with a 100 ppm STEL ([SDS citing Title 8](https://www.laddresearch.com/lanotattachments/download/file/id/376/store/1/21335sds_4.pdf)). Confirm in Title 8 §5155 Table AC-1; dir.ca.gov returned 404.
- **Gloves:** in a 2003 permeation study, MMA "permeated within 3 min through all glove materials" tested, including latex and nitrile ([Lönnroth 2003](https://yadda.icm.edu.pl/baztech/element/bwmeta1.element.baztech-aebdca56-8d04-49d1-99e8-a5db09e57416/c/Lonnroth.pdf), snippet). Treat disposable nitrile as splash-only, change it at once, and use laminate gloves for handling (guess). Röhm warns that inhaling the vapours and frequent skin contact "may have a mutually intensifying effect, thereby being detrimental to health and provoking allergies" ([311-3](https://www.plexiglas.de/files/plexiglas-content/pdf/verarbeitungsrichtlinie/311-3-EN-joining-PLEXIGLAS.pdf)).
- **Fire:**
  - ACRIFIX 2R 0190 flash point is about 10 °C, UN 1133 ([TDS](https://www.zenit.cz/public/media/Plasty/Zenit_Technicky_List_Plasty_Lepidla_Acrifix_2R-190_EN.pdf)).
  - Röhm: "Most adhesives constitute a fire hazard"; keep ignition sources away; ventilation must be explosion-proof ([311-3](https://www.plexiglas.de/files/plexiglas-content/pdf/verarbeitungsrichtlinie/311-3-EN-joining-PLEXIGLAS.pdf)).
  - Store the peroxide catalyst separately from the monomer (guess, standard practice; check the SDS).
- **Ventilation:** extract at floor level and at the bench; keep below the occupational limits ([311-3](https://www.plexiglas.de/files/plexiglas-content/pdf/verarbeitungsrichtlinie/311-3-EN-joining-PLEXIGLAS.pdf)).
- **Air rules:**
  - Weld-On 40 is labelled ≤250 g/L VOC ([Chemical Concepts](https://www.chemical-concepts.com/product/weld-on-40-reactive-acrylic-adhesive/)).
  - Weld-On 3, 4 and 16 are "restricted under South Coast Air Quality Management District (SCAQMD) Rule 1168" ([same page](https://www.chemical-concepts.com/product/weld-on-40-reactive-acrylic-adhesive/)). That limits solvent cements at LA-area shops.
  - Whether BAAQMD Regulation 8 Rule 51 covers EARF's use was **not verified**.

### 2.3 Polishing to optical clarity: what to expect
- On curved 3D surfaces, clarity depends on a fine finishing toolpath, then sanding and buffing. Architectural Plastics notes that surface polishing "is more variable than edge polishing" ([polishing](https://archplastics.com/polishing)).
- Röhm lists sanding and polishing among the operations that create stress ([311-3](https://www.plexiglas.de/files/plexiglas-content/pdf/verarbeitungsrichtlinie/311-3-EN-joining-PLEXIGLAS.pdf)). Plan a final anneal after polishing and before any cement or solvent touches the horn.
- **Published lead times:**
  - Architectural Plastics: prototypes 1–2 weeks; "2 weeks is common unless we are running beyond full capacity" ([FAQ](https://archplastics.com/faqs)).
  - AC Manufacturing: 2–3 days short, about 2 weeks standard.
