# EARF: events, fairs, awards and press, November 2026 to October 2027

Research date: 31 October 2026. Public web only. No forms filled, no accounts created, nobody contacted.

**How to read the tags**

- **[C]** Confirmed on the organiser's or outlet's own page, fetched on 31 Oct 2026.
- **[R]** Reported by a secondary source, or seen only in a search-result snippet. The organiser's own page was not verified.
- **[U]** Unconfirmed. Inferred from a previous edition, contradicted by another source, or the source was blocked.
- **Fit** and **Odds** are my own judgement for EARF (a new San Francisco brand with prototypes in early 2027). They are not sourced claims.

Limits of this research: the web-search budget for this session ran out partway through. Several sites refused automated access with HTTP 403: dezeen.com, stereophile.com, ifdesign.com, darko.audio, dwell.com, breradesigndistrict.it, gooddesignawards.org, icff.com and theabsolutesound.com (contact page). Two hosts did not resolve: roplasticprize.com and threedaysofdesign.dk. Either they need to be allowed in the session's network settings or the domains do not exist. Anything that depended on those sources is tagged [U]. Section 5 lists these gaps.

## 1. Bottom line

1. **Two moments can make EARF known in year one, and both suit prototypes:**
   - **Milan Design Week, 12–18 April 2027** ([fuorisalone.it][fs-home] [C]). The Salone runs 13–18 April and Euroluce, the lighting biennial, returns ([salonemilano.it][sal-dates] [C]).
   - **HIGH END, now in Vienna, 6–9 May 2027** ([highendsociety.de][he-home] [C]). Its free Start-Up Area takes prototypes, and applications close **31 Dec 2026** ([Start-Up page][he-startup] [C]).
2. **Several requested events are closed, moved or no longer running:**
   - SaloneSatellite 2027 applications closed on 15 Sep 2026; only a waiting list is left ([salonemilano.it][sat-page] [C]).
   - Design Miami/Basel was cancelled in 2025 ([The Art Newspaper][dm-basel-tan] [C]). Design Miami's site now lists only Miami, Seoul and Paris ([designmiami.com][dm-home] [C]).
   - ICFF leaves May and moves to **November 2027**, outside this window ([Home News Now][icff-hnn] [R]).
   - Sight Unseen OFFSITE ran from 2010 to 2018 and is not active ([Sight Unseen][su-about] [C]).
   - No Ro Plastic Prize call has been published since the 2023 edition ([guiltlessplastic.com][ro-gp] [C]).
3. **The big trade awards come too early for a brand whose prototypes land in early 2027:**
   - iF closes on 2–4 Nov 2026 and requires products to be on the market in 2027 ([Graphic Competitions][if-gc] [R], [Dieline][if-dieline] [R]).
   - Red Dot needs the physical product in Essen between 8 and 26 Feb 2027 ([red-dot.org][rd-fees] [C]).
   - The realistic first award is **IDSA IDEA 2027**, which opens early 2027 ([idsa.org][idea] [C]). Book iF, Red Dot and Dezeen for the 2028 cycle, once products are on sale.
4. **Date clash:** AXPONA's own site gives **16–18 April 2027** ([axpona.com][ax-attend] [C]), the same week as Milan. Earlier reports said 9–11 April ([Audiomatome][ax-sf] [R]).
5. **Cheapest home-city launch:** SF Design Week. In 2026 it ran 1–12 June and charged hosts $100–$399 ([sfdesignweek.org][sfdw] [C]). It suits a preview of the listening bar, Planet EARF.

## 3. The five to do first

**1. HIGH END Vienna Start-Up Area.** Deadline **31 Dec 2026**. Show **6–9 May 2027**.

The area gives up to ten young companies a free, complete stand. They can show standard products, prototypes or innovations. Accepted companies are named in press releases and the show magazine. Travel, hotel and extra advertising are not covered ([Start-Up page][he-startup] [C]).

The audience is large. In 2026 the first Vienna show drew 23,106 visitors, including 10,603 trade visitors and 549 media, from about 500 exhibitors ([audioXpress][he-report] [C]).

- By **1 Dec 2026**: get an official company-register extract. It must show EARF's legal form and that the business is in consumer electronics, which is the proof the organisers require ([Start-Up page][he-startup] [C]).
- By **10 Dec 2026**: write a one-page dossier for Tropos and Mantle, with renders, key specifications, the prototype schedule, a photo of the eight acrylic colours, and the team.
- By **15 Dec 2026**: email the application to alex[at]highendsociety.de ([Start-Up page][he-startup] [C]).
- If EARF is not selected, decide on a paid stand through the exhibitor form ([highendsociety.de][he-exhib] [C]).

**2. Alcova Milano 2027, plus Milan fallbacks.** Act **now**; no deadline is published. Milan Design Week runs **12–18 April 2027**.

Alcova has no formal open call. Its site asks exhibitors to email a company or project name and a short description ([alcova.xyz/about][alc-about] [C]). The founders have said most exhibitors come from earlier editions or word of mouth ([ifdm][alc-ifdm], [Interni][alc-interni] [R]). Fees and the 2027 dates are not published.

- Week of **2 Nov 2026**: email info@alcovamilano.com ([alcova.xyz/about][alc-about] [C]). Propose one installation that combines all four products: a listening room lit by Hemisphere and Core, in the eight colours. Ask for the 2027 dates, venues and fee.
- If the lead designer is under 35, write the same week to salonesatellite@salonemilano.it to join the 2027 waiting list ([salonemilano.it][sat-page] [C]).
- By **15 Dec 2026**, line up fallbacks:
  - Dropcity, at info@dropcity.org ([dropcity.org][drop] [C]).
  - Isola, at hello@isola.design ([isola.design][isola-calls] [C]).
  - A Brera Design District venue ([Brera][brera] [R]).
- By **mid-January 2027**: lock the venue.
- By **8 Feb 2027**: sign a Fuorisalone Base package (€2,000 + VAT) to be named in the official press release. Alternatively, use the free listing that opens at the end of January 2027 ([fuorisalone.it][fs-take] [C]).

**SaloneSatellite (the under-35 rule and the application window)**
- **Eligibility:** "dedicated to designers under 35" ([salonemilano.it][sat-page] [C]).
  - The site does not say on what date age is measured, and does not address companies [C, by absence].
  - Applicants submit personal details and three completed projects. Each project needs 1–3 JPGs in 16:9 format (100 MB maximum) and an English description of up to 300 characters ([how to take part, 2027][sat-2027] [C]).
  - One secondary source describes the work as "new prototypes that have not been produced or marketed" ([Indonesia Design][sat-indo] [R]).
- **2027 window:** closed. The official page says applications were accepted "up to 15th September 2026" ([salonemilano.it][sat-2027] [C]). A Belgian partner page says 30 August 2026 ([WBDM][sat-wbdm] [C], conflicting).
  - The site now says "Applications for 2027 are closed" and asks people to write to salonesatellite@salonemilano.it for information and the waiting list ([salonemilano.it][sat-page] [C]).
- **Fee:** not published.
  - Winners of SaloneSatellite's international editions receive free participation as a prize ([salonemilano.it 2022][sat-2022] [R]). That suggests the standard place is paid [U].
- **Award:** each year three projects win the SaloneSatellite Award, plus special mentions ([salonemilano.it][sat-page] [C]).
- **Next window:** the 2026 call closed on 30 Aug 2025 ([salonemilano.it][sat-2026] [C]), and the 2024 call closed on 15 Sep 2023 ([Interni][sat-interni] [R]). The **2028 call will probably close around late August to mid September 2027** [U].

- Ask each editor before sending embargoed material.
