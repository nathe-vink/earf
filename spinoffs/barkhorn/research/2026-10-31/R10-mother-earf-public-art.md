# R10: Mother EARF as public art, with paths to show, fund or commission it in 2027–2028

Prepared 31 Oct 2026. Every claim was checked live on a public page and has a link.
Markers: **[E]** = my estimate. **[U]** = not verified. **[U: search extract]** = the official page would not load (most sf.gov and sfport.com pages render blank to a fetcher), so the claim comes from the search engine's extract of that page.

## Bottom line

1. **Only one paid open call is live right now: Amsterdam Light Festival, Edition 16.** It closes **7 Dec 2026 at 23:59 CET**. Selected makers get a **€4,000 fee, and production and travel costs are covered**. The festival runs 25 Nov 2027 to 16 Jan 2028 and drew more than 1 million visitors in 2023–24. Submit the 2 m globe as an artwork by a named designer.
2. **Burning Man's 2027 honoraria closed two days ago, on 29 Oct 2026.** Burning Man rules do not allow company names on artwork, grants over $20k are rare, and the Global Art Grants ended in 2024. Treat Black Rock City as a 2028 option only, for an unbranded artist version.
3. **San Francisco has no open call that fits a new piece.** Its sculpture programmes (Big Art Loop, Patricia's Green, Civic Center) take **only existing, built works**. Most doors stay closed until the 2 m globe exists and has been photographed lit at night.
4. **The SF routes that work are permits paid for by a sponsor.** These are SF Arts Commission (SFAC) approval for temporary art on City property (about 2.5 months, under 1 year, sponsor pays everything) and the Port's artist-sponsored programme (next reviews **27 Feb** and **30 Jun 2027**, display up to 24 months) [U: search extract for the 2.5 months and the Port dates]. There are also two venues that allow brands: Salesforce Park "experiential activations" and Yerba Buena Gardens rentals with a branding fee.
5. **Illuminate SF is SF Travel's umbrella listing, not a fund.** Pieces join by being installed and paid for by someone else. The nonprofit Illuminate takes **no sponsorships or naming rights**.
6. **Patrons exist but rarely fund brand objects.** The Sijbrandij Foundation's Big Art Loop spent more than $2M in year one, with $67k–$204k per placement. The Downtown Development Corp. has more than $60M committed and funds public art through ICA SF. Embarcadero Plaza is being rebuilt ($40M), with construction expected from late 2026 to early 2028.
7. **The $150k–300k estimate for a 4 m globe looks like fabrication only.** SF placements of works that already exist budget $67k–$350k on top of the build. Plan on **$300k–650k all-in [E]** for a public 4 m version.
8. **Engineering reality check.**
   - A 2 m, 190 kg globe implies walls about 12.7 mm thick.
   - At design gusts, wind drag is about equal to the globe's own weight, so it must be anchored or ballasted [E].
   - Tinted PLEXIGLAS carries only a **10-year colour guarantee, and thermoforming voids it**.
9. **Recommended path:**
   1. Enter Amsterdam by 7 Dec 2026.
   2. Build the 2 m globe and debut it at a SF venue that allows brands in spring or summer 2027.
   3. Lock a winter 2027–28 public placement during the Illuminate SF season.

   Hold the 4 m version for 2028.

## A. San Francisco light-art and public-art routes

### A1. Illuminate SF Festival of Light

- **What it is.** The festival is presented by the San Francisco Travel Association "in collaboration with local civic, arts and cultural partners". In 2020 it spanned more than 40 works in 17 neighbourhoods, and "each year San Francisco adds new permanent and temporary installations" ([SF/Arts](http://www.sfarts.org/story/illuminate-sf-brightens-up-winter-nights-1y5WZklXtkp0uhIJULupR5/)).
- **How pieces get in.** No public submission process or open call was found [U]. The official site illuminatesf.com returned HTTP 503 twice, so I moved on. In practice a piece is installed and paid for by its own producer, then listed [E]. Recent examples and who paid for them:
  - **Let's Glow SF**, a projection festival hosted by the Downtown SF Partnership. It runs **4–13 Dec 2026** on six buildings ([DSFP](https://downtownsf.org/things-to-do/lets-glow-sf); [Golden Gate Transit](https://www.goldengate.org/events/lets-glow-sf-2026/)). Building sponsors include Hines and the Yerba Buena Partnership. A **$300,000 grant from the city's economic development office (OEWD)** paid for the Yerba Buena Lane projection for 2025 and its 2026 content rotation ([SFAC intake packet](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/Yerba_Buena_Lane_Projection_Art.pdf)).
  - **The Bay Lights**, funded by Illuminate's private philanthropy (see §D).
  - **Entwined** in Golden Gate Park, presented with the SF Parks Alliance ([ABC7](https://abc7news.com/golden-gate-park-winter-lights-entwined-peacock-meadow-san-francisco-art-installation/7390981/)). Press listings say private donations paid for it, with no city funds [U: search extract].
- **Timeline.** It has run roughly from Thanksgiving to late January [U; only third-party listings]. A hotel calendar lists **6–15 Dec 2026** for the 2026 edition ([Stanford Court](https://www.stanfordcourt.com/events/illuminate-sf-festival-of-light)) [U].
- **What this means for EARF [E].** To be "in" Illuminate SF 2027–28, the globe must be permitted and lit somewhere public between late Nov 2027 and Jan 2028, and paid for by EARF or a host. EARF then asks SF Travel to add it to the map and trail.

### A2. SF Arts Commission (SFAC): current calls

- **Temporary public art is a rolling approval, not a call.**
  - **Scope.** SFAC approval is needed for temporary art on City property, or art funded in whole or part by City funds. That includes proposals from "commercial enterprises" ([refresher](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/Temporary_Art_Refresher_v.2.pdf); [intake form, 16 Jul 2026](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/Temporary_Art_Intake_Form_-_Google_Forms_2pXrzQb.pdf)).
  - **Duration.** Under 1 year; longer needs a justification and a status report. Installations of **under 7 days can be approved by the Director of Cultural Affairs** without a committee vote ([refresher](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/Temporary_Art_Refresher_v.2.pdf); [Feb 2026 minutes](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/Feb_18_VAC_Draft_Minutes.pdf)).
  - **Required documents.**
    - a letter of approval from the agency or owner of the site
    - **3 letters of community support** and an outreach checklist (supervisor, community benefit district, cultural district)
    - a scale drawing or model, a site photo and a **rendering in the site** (renders are accepted; no prototype is required)
    - a materials list and a maintenance plan
    - a signed waiver of proprietary rights ([intake form](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/Temporary_Art_Intake_Form_-_Google_Forms_2pXrzQb.pdf))
  - **Engineering and ADA reports** are no longer filed with SFAC, because "these reports are required and reviewed by the property owner" ([Feb 2026 minutes](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/Feb_18_VAC_Draft_Minutes.pdf)).
  - **Costs.** The sponsor pays all costs, including SFAC staff time, insurance and permit fees, and also pays for removal ([guidelines](https://www.sf.gov/policy-guidelines-for-temporary-public-art)) [U: search extract].
  - **Timing.** Submit about **2.5 months before** the Visual Arts Committee meeting (third Wednesday of the month). The full Commission then votes (first Monday of the month) ([guidelines](https://www.sf.gov/guidelines-for-temporary-public-art)) [U: search extract].
- **Art on Market Street.**
  - It is a **2D poster series** on street kiosks. The 2027 series pays $12,000 per artist, with four 3-month slots through 2027 on the theme "Market Street: The Next 175 Years", tied to the 2027 Further Triennial.
  - The request for qualifications **closed 17 Apr 2026** ([plan](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/2027_Art_on_Market_Project_Plan.pdf)). The next cycle is likely Feb–Apr 2027 [E]. It is not a route for a globe.
- **Calls that closed in 2026.**
  - Patricia's Green sculpture loan (closed **21 Sep 2026**; see A3).
  - Harvey Milk Plaza request for qualifications (closed 15 Jun 2026) [U: search extract].
  - Civic Center temporary sculpture: the RFP ran Sep–Nov 2025. Torkwase Dyson's *Akua* was installed from July 2026 at a total cost of **up to $350,000**, plus a loan fee of up to $20,000 ([panel summary](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/Civic_Center_Temporary_Sculpture_2026_Artist_Review_Panel_Summary.pdf); [project plan](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/Civic_Center_Temporary_Sculpture_Project_Plan_VAC_8.20.2025.pdf)).
- **2027 open calls.** I found **no SFAC call open with a deadline after today** [U: the live calls page, sf.gov/calls-for-artists, renders blank to a fetcher].

## B. Burning Man Arts

### B1. Black Rock City Honoraria 2027

- **Status: closed.** The "2027 grant cycle is now closed" ([Honoraria page](https://burningman.org/programs/burning-man-arts/grants/brc-honoraria/)). The timeline was ([how to apply](https://burningman.org/black-rock-city/bring-your-art/art-grants-programs/brc-honoraria/submit_grant/)):
  - letter of intent (LOI) form opened **1 Oct 2026** and closed **29 Oct 2026 at 5 pm PT**
  - LOI notifications on 8 Dec 2026
  - full proposals by invitation, due 11 Jan 2027
  - grantees notified in early March 2027
- **Grant sizes** ([Honoraria page](https://burningman.org/programs/burning-man-arts/grants/brc-honoraria/)):
  - The annual budget is about **$1.5M**, including the Temple grant, across about 70–75 projects.
  - "Grants over $20,000 are rare." Burning Man typically funds **40–60% of the project budget**.
  - Payments go out in three parts (about 55%, 30% and a 15% performance deposit).
  - Requests of $30k or more need budget tiers.
  - In 2026, **$1.3M went to 75 projects** ([Journal](https://journal.burningman.org/2026/03/burning-man-arts/brc-art/introducing-brc-2026-honoraria-art-honoraria/)). In that cycle, 520 LOIs led to 382 invitations, and about 20–25% of those invited were funded.
- **Rules and requirements.**
  - Applicants need a Burner Profile. The LOI needs at least one image (a drawing, sketch or photo is fine) and a disclosure of any AI use.
  - **Physical maquettes are not accepted.**
  - The art must be on the open playa. Artists supply their own power and anchoring.
  - The grant will not pay for insurance, legal costs, travel or artist fees ([Honoraria page](https://burningman.org/programs/burning-man-arts/grants/brc-honoraria/); [grantee expectations](https://burningman.org/black-rock-city/bring-your-art/art-grants-programs/brc-honoraria/art-grants-expect/)).
- **Brands.** "Can I put my company name or donor names on my artwork? **No.**" This follows the decommodification principle ([Help Center](https://help.burningman.org/hc/en-us/articles/360024490192-Can-I-put-my-company-name-or-donor-names-on-my-artwork-)). That conflicts directly with "the brand at city scale".
- **Next cycle.** The 2028 LOI is likely to run about 1–29 Oct 2027 [E].

## E. Cost and engineering reality check

**E1. What the brief implies [E].**
- Acrylic density is 1.19 g/cm³ ([PLEXIGLAS GS/XT data](https://www.voskunststoffen.nl/files/166151/datasheet%20plexiglas%20XT%20GS.pdf)). A 2 m sphere at **190 kg therefore has walls about 12.7 mm thick**.
- At the same wall, a 4 m sphere weighs about **760 kg**. With 19–25 mm walls it would weigh 1.1–1.5 t, before the frame and lights.
- Continents in eight tints means a **mosaic of bonded or framed panels**, not one blown dome.

**E2. Who makes large acrylic spheres, and what is known about cost.**
- **Reynolds Polymer** (Colorado) thermoforms "spheres… domes" in-house ([portfolio](https://reynoldspolymer.com/portfolio/aquaria-zoos)). It built a **20 ft (6.1 m) acrylic dome room** for the Yeosu EXPO 2012 aquarium ([InPark](https://www.inparkmagazine.com/reynolds-provides-r-cast-acrylic-to-three-new-aquarium-projects/)).
- **California Quality Plastics** says it makes hemispheres up to 8 ft (2.44 m) across [U: company claim seen only through third-party extracts].
- **Plastock (UK)** sells clear **1.8 m hemispheres for £2,714 ex VAT**, about 10 mm walls ([Plastock](https://www.plastock.co.uk/products/bespoke-acrylic-hemispherical-dome)). That implies about £5.4k for a clear shell only [E].
- **Nippura** (Japan) makes laminated aquarium panels [U: search extract].
- **"Blanco": no acrylic maker by that name was found [U].** It may be a mix-up with another firm.
- **No published price** was found for a 2 m tinted sphere [U]. Expect custom tints and a 2 m size to cost several times the catalogue clear price [E].

**E3. Outdoor durability.**
- **Colour.** Clear PLEXIGLAS carries a **30-year no-yellowing guarantee** ([guarantee](https://www.ledil.com/plexiglas_guarantee)). Coloured grades carry only a **10-year colour guarantee, and it does not apply if the sheet "has been thermally moulded"** ([colour guarantee, 05/2024](https://www.zenit.cz/public/media/Plasty/Zenit_Zarucni_List_Plasty_Plexiglas_Barevna_Stalost.pdf)). EARF's tints, once formed, are effectively unguaranteed, so order spare panels and run weathering tests [E].
- **Heat and movement.** The expansion coefficient is **7×10⁻⁵ per K**, and heat plus moisture can cause **up to 5 mm per metre** of growth. The maximum permanent service temperature is **80 °C** and the ignition temperature 425 °C ([GS/XT data](https://www.voskunststoffen.nl/files/166151/datasheet%20plexiglas%20XT%20GS.pdf)).
  - Acrylic moves about 3 times as much as metals. For dark sheet, multiply the movement by 1.4 ([ACRYLITE](https://www.acrylite.co/resources/knowledge-base/article/-how-do-i-figure-out-expansion-and-contraction-of-acrylic-for-a-glazing-application)).
  - That means about 6–10 mm of diameter growth on the 2 m globe and 11–20 mm on the 4 m [E]. Fixings must float, and LED heat in a sealed globe needs venting [E].
- **Joints.** In Berlin, the AquaDom's bonded acrylic cylinder burst in Dec 2022. The 2023 expert report could not determine the cause; defects in the glued seams were one of three hypotheses ([The Local](https://www.thelocal.de/20231011/frustrating-berlin-investigators-still-stumped-by-aquarium-explosion)).
- **SF climate.** The original Bay Lights failed after a decade of fog, wind, salt and exhaust ([KQED](http://www.kqed.org/news/12017479/new-bay-bridge-light-installation-underway-after-nearly-2-year-shutdown)).
- **Fire and cleaning.**
  - Acrylic is combustible. An example acrylic sheet is rated in the more combustible class, CC2 ([ICC-ES ESR-2591](https://icc-es.org/wp-content/uploads/report-directory/ESR-2591.pdf)) [U: search extract].
  - Clean it only with mild soap. Window sprays and solvents cause crazing ([ACRYLITE](https://www.acrylite.co/resources/fabrication-manuals/clean-disinfect-and-maintain-acrylite-acrylic-sheet)) [U: search extract].

**E4. Wind and anchoring [E].**
- The force uses the drag equation F = ½ρv²·Cd·A, with Cd about 0.47 for a sphere ([drag coefficient](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drag_coefficient)).
- At 40–45 m/s gusts (about 89–101 mph), the drag is:
  - **2 m globe: about 1.4–1.8 kN, roughly 150–190 kgf, about the globe's own weight**
  - **4 m globe: about 5.8–7.3 kN, roughly 590–750 kgf**
- So both versions need ballast or anchors, sized by an engineer. The actual SF design wind speed was not verified [U]; the engineer takes it from ASCE 7 and the California code.
- Sites that forbid drilling (Civic Center) need ballast. Rooftop and garage sites (Salesforce Park, Union Square) need a load check ([Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2026/10/giant-nude-sculpture-vanishes-from-sf-s-embarcadero-after-18-months/)).

**E5. Engineer sign-off and insurance.**
- **Stamped engineering is required everywhere in practice:**
  - **Big Art Loop:** "stamped drawings/calculations" ([call](https://building180.com/bigartsfapplication)).
  - **SFAC:** engineering and ADA reports, reviewed by the property owner ([minutes](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/Feb_18_VAC_Draft_Minutes.pdf)).
  - **Port:** PCP-029 and the encroachment permit [U: search extract].
  - **Burning Man:** load calculations from 16 ft tall; anchor plan ideally drawn up by a structural engineer ([Building Safe Structures](https://burningman.org/black-rock-city/bring-your-art/art-installation-guidelines/building-safe-structures/)).
- **Insurance:**
  - City art contracts name the City and County of SF as additional insured; one SFAC template requires $1M per occurrence ([SFAC template](https://www.sfartscommission.org/sites/default/files/documents/TEMPLATEartist.doc)) [U: search extract].
  - The National Park Service requires at least $1M naming the United States ([NPS](https://www.nps.gov/goga/planyourvisit/specialparkuses.htm)).
  - TJPA wants a certificate of insurance 14 days before the event ([TJPA](https://www.tjpa.org/node/6675)).

**E6. Is $150k–300k realistic for a 4 m public globe?**
- For fabrication alone it is plausible but tight [E].
- **Deploying it publicly costs extra.** Big Art Loop budgets $67k–$204k per already-built work just to engineer, install and care for it ([Big Art Loop](https://www.bigartloop.org/)). SFAC capped *Akua* at **$350k** for about 5 months ([summary](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/Civic_Center_Temporary_Sculpture_2026_Artist_Review_Panel_Summary.pdf)).
- **Budget $300k–650k all-in [E].** Prove the 2 m version first.
