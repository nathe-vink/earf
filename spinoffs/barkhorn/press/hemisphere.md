# Hemisphere

**A pendant in the speakers' acrylic, frosted within.** "Hung low over the bar. A quarter turn takes its shade off, and
another puts a new colour on."

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | 200 mm across |
| Shade | Cast acrylic in three layers, tinted, with a frosted layer between |
| Light | 2700 K, CRI 90, its source shielded inside the hub |
| Fitting | Black anodised aluminium, with a quarter-turn catch |
| Hanging | A 600 mm drop as standard, cut to length; a black acrylic canopy 150 mm across |
| Mains | 120 V, dimmable; listing to UL 1598 to follow |
| Shades | In all eight colours, sold singly |
| Care | A quarter turn takes the shade off to wash: water and a drop of mild soap, then dry. No glass cleaner, alcohol or solvents: they craze acrylic |
| Figures | Design figures; they may change before production |
| Price | Set at launch |

**The name.** Half a globe, hung mouth down.
