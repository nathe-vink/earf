# Core

**A table lamp in the speakers' acrylic, plugged in or not.** "A ball of tinted acrylic sitting low in a black acrylic
base. Plugged in, it stays put; unplugged, it goes where the conversation is."

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | 160 mm ball, 180 mm tall |
| Weight | About 1.5 kg |
| Light | 2200 to 2700 K, warmer as it dims |
| Control | Touch the black ring: tap to switch, hold to dim |
| Power | USB-C, cable and adapter included; about five hours cordless at full brightness, longer dimmed, on cells you can replace yourself |
| Rating | Designed to meet IP44, for terraces and spilt drinks |
| Shades | In all eight colours, sold singly |
| Care | Dust with a soft microfibre cloth; wash with water and a drop of mild soap, then dry. No glass cleaner, alcohol or solvents: they craze acrylic. Fine scratches polish out |
| Figures | Design figures; they may change before production |
| Price | Set at launch |
