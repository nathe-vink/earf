# Mantle

**A turntable in cast acrylic.** The plinth floats 40 mm over its base. Rega's 24 V motor hangs beneath the deck, and the phono stage is built in.
The lid's hinges sit on brass inserts, so no screw cuts the acrylic; the motor's pod stands on soft feet, so its hum
stays out of the plinth.
