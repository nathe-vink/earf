# EARF: hi-fi and light, cast in tinted acrylic, in eight colors

**San Francisco, [DATE].** EARF makes a horn loudspeaker, two lamps and a record player in tinted cast acrylic, in
the same eight colors: Ember, Dust, Acid, Poison, Ice, Haze, Fog and Smoke. A room can be one
color or three. First shown in San Francisco at the debut, [DATE], the four pieces are made to
order in San Francisco [OWNER: confirm "in San Francisco" once the shops are chosen].

**Tropos** is a powered horn loudspeaker, 1.3 m tall:
- **The cabinet** is 20 mm tinted cast acrylic.
- **The horn** is one sheet of 10 mm acrylic in the cabinet's own color, formed over a plug and satin outside; its
  throat is turned from a solid block.
- **Above 1 kHz** a 2-inch compression driver plays through the horn; **below**, a 15-inch woofer. Each has its own
  channel of the amplifier inside.
- **A light from below** shines up through the acrylic and fills the whole cabinet with color.

**Hemisphere** is a pendant, half a globe hung mouth down, in three layers of acrylic with a frosted layer between. A
quarter turn takes its shade off, and another puts a new color on.

**Core** is a table lamp, a ball of tinted acrylic on a black base. Cordless, it runs about
five hours a charge on three standard LiFePO4 cells and goes where the conversation is.

**Mantle** is a record player whose tinted plinth floats 40 mm above a base that glows at its edge, its motor in a
pod of its own and a phono stage built in.

Each product is named for a layer of the Earth.
