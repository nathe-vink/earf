EARF: hi-fi and light, cast in tinted acrylic, in eight colours

San Francisco, [DATE]. EARF makes a horn loudspeaker, two lamps and a record player in cast acrylic tinted all the way through, in the same eight colours: Ember, Dust, Acid, Poison, Ice, Haze, Fog and Smoke. A room can be one colour or three. First shown in San Francisco on 19 January, during FOG Design+Art week, the four pieces are made to order in San Francisco [OWNER: confirm "in San Francisco" once the shops are chosen].

Tropos is a powered horn loudspeaker, 1.3 m tall:

  • The cabinet is 20 mm cast acrylic, coloured through.
  • The horn is formed from the same tinted sheet. [IF THE OWNER CHOOSES THE STACKED HORN: The horn is built up from [14–22] layers of the cabinet's tinted acrylic, stacked and shaped, so its edges read like strata in a cliff face.]
  • Above 1 kHz a 2-inch compression driver plays through the horn; below, a 15-inch woofer. Each has its own channel of the amplifier inside.
  • A lamp underneath fills the whole cabinet with colour.

Hemisphere is a pendant, half a globe hung mouth down, in three layers of acrylic with a frosted layer between. A quarter turn takes its shade off, and another puts a new colour on.

Core is a table lamp, a ball of tinted acrylic on a black base. Plugged in, it stays put; unplugged, it runs about five hours and goes where the conversation is.

Mantle is a record player whose tinted plinth floats 40 mm above a base that glows at its edge, its motor in a pod of its own and a phono stage built in.

Each product is named for a layer of the Earth.
