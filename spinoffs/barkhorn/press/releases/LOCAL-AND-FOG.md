## 1. The local note (San Francisco outlets)

**Subject:** A new San Francisco maker's first speakers play for the first time

> Hello,
>
> On [DATE], EARF, a San Francisco maker of hi-fi and lighting in tinted cast acrylic, plays its first pair of
> loudspeakers for the first time, at a debut night at [VENUE].
>
> The night opens with Session 00: one album, start to finish, played to a seated room by [ARTIST] and recorded
> live, to be pressed on colored vinyl. Then the room listens to records. A coin at the door buys a song.
>
> It's invitation only, about 120 guests; we'd be glad to have you there, or to show you the room that afternoon
> [TIME]. Pictures from the night, the first photographs of the pieces, follow within two days.
>
> [Your name], EARF · earf.world · [contact line]

*(117 words.)* **No pictures attached:** before the night, every picture is a render. If the outlet asks, send one
captioned "Render".
