## Boilerplate

**Short (50 words).** EARF is a San Francisco maker of hi-fi and lighting in cast acrylic, tinted all the way through,
in eight colours from Ember to Smoke. Its products are named for the layers of the Earth: Tropos, a powered horn
loudspeaker; Hemisphere, a pendant; Core, a table lamp; and Mantle, a record player. earf.world

**Long (100 words).** EARF is a San Francisco maker of hi-fi and lighting in cast acrylic, tinted all the way through.
Every product comes in the same eight colours, from Ember to Smoke, so a room can be one colour or three. Tropos, the
flagship, is a powered horn loudspeaker whose cabinet and horn are both cast in the tint; Hemisphere, a pendant, and
Core, a table lamp that runs plugged in or not, light the room in the same acrylic; Mantle is a record player whose
plinth floats over a base lit at its edge. Each piece is made to order. EARF is in development and not yet for sale.
earf.world

**One line.** Hi-fi and light, cast in tinted acrylic, in eight colours from Ember to Smoke.
