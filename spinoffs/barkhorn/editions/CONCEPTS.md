# EARF special editions: concepts for the judges (2026-10-31)

The owner, 2026-10-31: "Also it would be sick to do some special edition with things cast in the acrylic, like the miss
blanche chair, but not necessarily roses. Could also be cool to have some with bubbles."

Seventeen editions in three directions. No roses, no flowers in a clear chair, no feathers in a stool (prior art:
`research/prior-art.md`). Sourcing and law: `research/sourcing.md`. Making: `research/making.md`.

**What the physics allows.** A&C's TruCast chart puts the light tints near 58 to 69% transmission at 1/8 in and Smoke at
29%. Through 40 mm of any tint the light is nearly gone. So every edition body is **clear cast acrylic with thin tinted
laminae** (2 to 3 mm, the "strata"), and the object sits in a clear stratum where it can be seen. The strata are the
house look: each edition reads as a core sample of its place.

**What the making allows.** EARF laminates; it does not pour. The object goes in a pocket milled to its shape in one
clear sheet, sealed, and the next sheet is bonded over with a polymerizing cement (Weld-On 40 or ACRIFIX 2R 0190),
then annealed. Organics never go in a lamp or near an amplifier.

Products: Tropos (horn speaker), Hemisphere (pendant), Core (160 mm ball, or the "core sample" stack of discs), Mantle
(record player, 25 mm plinth), Moho (record weight), Lithos (floor lamp), Cairn (bookends), Bedrock (record stand),
Relief (lit topographic wall panel), Ionos (radio).

## A. Habitat specimens: each tint holding a true specimen of its own habitat

| # | Edition | Product, tint | Inside |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Cinder** | Cairn bookends, clear with Ember strata | One vesicular basalt cinder (about 40 mm), red-black, from a commercial cinder quarry (never a park), in a pocket in the clear middle stratum, between two 3 mm Ember laminae. A pair: the two halves of one cinder sawn apart, one in each bookend. |
| A2 | **Dallol** | Moho record weight, clear with Acid laminae | A small cluster of native sulfur crystals (Sicily, a mineral dealer) on a black basalt chip, sealed in a pocket under the label face; the Acid lamina on top. |
| A3 | **Stratum** | Core sample (stack of discs), Dust | One disc of the stack holds a 2 mm layer of washed quarry sand from the Mojave (a permitted commercial pit), sealed in a ring-shaped pocket, so the stack reads as rock strata with a true sand bed. |
| A4 | **Burl** | Cairn bookends, clear with Poison strata | A slice of salvaged redwood burl (chain-of-custody salvage, resin-stabilized, kiln-dried) in each bookend, the grain at the clear stratum. |
| A5 | **Oat** | Bedrock record stand, clear with Haze strata | Dried wild oat seed heads from the golden hills (private ranch, written permission), pressed flat in a shallow pocket along the stand's spine, out of the sun's path. |
| A6 | **Iron** | Core ball (160 mm), clear, a Fog skin | An etched slice of an iron meteorite (Gibeon or Muonionalusta, a dealer with documented provenance): the nearest thing to a planet's core anyone can hold, at the center of Core, in a dry, desiccated, sealed pocket. Battery and LED away from it. |
| A7 | **Shore** | Moho, clear with Fog strata | A rounded red chert pebble from a Marin beach shop's stock (bought, not taken), seen through the weight's top. |

## B. Bubbles by design

| # | Edition | Product, tint | Inside |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Abraham** | Moho record weight, clear with an Ice top lamina | Columns of flat lens-shaped air voids, one at each bond line of a 12-layer laminate, stacked like the frozen methane bubbles of a mountain lake: matching dished pockets milled into both faces of each joint, the cement kept on the flats. |
| B2 | **Fog bank** | Relief wall panel, clear with Fog face | A field of thousands of 0.5 to 2 mm spherical voids on three bond lines, densest at the bottom and thinning upward. Edge-lit, the bank glows. |
| B3 | **Breath** | Core ball (160 mm), clear | One 40 mm sphere of air at the center: two hemispheres, each with a hemispherical pocket, bonded. The air reads as a silver pearl from every side. |
| B4 | **Contour** | Relief wall panel, clear | One contour line of a real mountain (the 2,000 m line of Mount Shasta) traced as a closed round air channel, 3 mm across: a half-round groove milled in each mating face. Edge-lit, a silver line drawn in the air. |
| B5 | **Groove** | Moho record weight, clear with a Smoke top lamina | Session 00's first second of sound, its waveform drawn as a spiral air channel round the weight, as a record's groove is drawn: silver in clear. |
| B6 | **Rain** | Lithos floor lamp column, Acid | Vertical strings of small air spheres falling down the column like rain on black lava, far from the light source. |

Each edition is numbered, signed, and ships with a provenance card: what is inside, where it came from, the permit or
receipt, the date.
