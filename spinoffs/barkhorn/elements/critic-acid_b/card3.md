# Card: render-c3 (proof size 1200 x 800, 128 samples, denoised; the final is 2400 x 1600)

**What the frame is for.** A campaign image for a loudspeaker colour called Acid (golden yellow; its line: "A yellow
with opinions."), shown in its element: acid rain, real. A storm in sulphurous yellow-green light over wet black
volcanic rock; real rain as a 1/60 s shutter records it (thin streaks, no strings), splashes and ripple rings in the
puddles; puddles mirror the speaker; the light breaks through low behind the rain so the streaks catch it. For a
luxury brand's homepage and look book; the product sells at about $20,000 a pair.

**Fixed (do not change).** The speaker: a 1293 mm tall horn loudspeaker, a cabinet of 20 mm cast acrylic tinted golden
amber, lined in white, under a horn in tinted resin (it stands on two short posts above the cabinet, by design);
cover off; its shape, colour and parts.

**May change (current settings).**
- Camera: height 0.85 m, 9.2 m from the speaker, azimuth -12 deg (from its left), 80 mm lens, f/3.5, focus on the
  front face, verticals level. Speaker turned 16 deg.
- Sky: photographed HDRI (an overcast sunset: a dark cloud deck over a bright band low on the horizon, the sun 2.6 deg
  up), turned so the sun is behind the speaker and 42 deg to its right (out of frame right); strength 0.32, 4k.
- Sun lamp: matched to the sky's sun (azimuth 138, elevation 2.6), irradiance 6.5, angle 2 deg, colour #F4D98A. The
  hills have a low pass cut where the sun sets, and nothing on the sun's line shades the speaker.
- Lamps a photographer would bring: a 2 m reflector from the camera's left (irradiance 0.9, #F1E6B8), a 1.2 m fill from
  the right (0.2), a small 0.5 m card low in front aimed into the horn's throat (0.5).
- Haze: near, 0.0015 /m to 90 m and 22 m up; far, over the field and hills, 0.005 /m to 340 m and 13 m up; colour
  #C8CC6A, anisotropy 0.45.
- Ground: photographed black rock texture (1.4 m a tile, bump 2.0, darkened 72 %, tinted #1E1D17 at 70 %), true
  displacement 7 cm, plus modelled lava forms (pillows, ropes, grain from 9 m down to 12 cm); a water film (clear coat
  0.3, its roughness varying 0.1 to 0.45 in 25 cm patches); a coarser far field (film roughness 0.35).
- Puddles: still water at a level, filling hollows (a chain of hollows from the speaker toward the camera, so it
  mirrors), ripple rings from drops in four layers (largest 20 cm, smallest about 3 cm), surface roughness 0.04.
- Rocks: scanned boulders, darkened and wet (one near left, two behind, 160 blocks at 3 to 45 m, 220 larger at 45 to
  150 m).
- Hills: a ridge of black rock at 260 m, 9 m high, jagged, in the far haze.
- Rain, three layers: 50 drops at 1 to 3 m (far out of focus), 60,000 streaks at 3 to 60 m (5 to 16 cm long, radius
  0.8 to 1.4 mm, opacity 0.25 to 0.6 drawn per streak, fading to their ends, none ending on the ground), 150,000 finer
  at 15 to 120 m (opacity 0.15); each a water trace, refracting (roughness 0.25) and scattering the light forward;
  leaning slightly with the wind.
- Splashes: 1,500 crowns 3 to 7 mm across over the puddle chain.
- Render: AgX, Medium High Contrast, exposure -0.35, white balance 6800 K, tint -20 (10 is neutral; lower is greener).
- Finish: contrast 0.22, clarity 0.07, sharpen 0.25, vignette 0.14.
