# Card: render-c2 (proof size 1200 x 800, 128 samples, denoised; the final is 2400 x 1600)

**What the frame is for.** A campaign image for a loudspeaker colour called Acid (golden yellow; its line: "A yellow
with opinions."), shown in its element: acid rain, real. A storm in sulphurous yellow-green light over wet black
volcanic rock; real rain as a 1/60 s shutter records it (thin streaks, no strings), splashes and ripple rings in the
puddles; puddles mirror the speaker; the light breaks through low behind the rain so the streaks catch it. For a
luxury brand's homepage and look book; the product sells at about $20,000 a pair.

**Fixed (do not change).** The speaker: a 1293 mm tall horn loudspeaker, a cabinet of 20 mm cast acrylic tinted golden
amber, lined in white, under a horn in tinted resin; cover off; its shape, colour and parts.

**May change (current settings).**
- Camera: height 0.85 m, 9.2 m from the speaker, azimuth -12 deg (from its left), 80 mm lens, f/3.5, focus on the
  front face, verticals level. Speaker turned 16 deg.
- Sky: photographed HDRI (an overcast sunset sky, sun 2.6 deg above the horizon), rotated so the sun is behind the
  speaker, 10 deg right of the axis; strength 0.55, 4k.
- Sun lamp: matched to the sky's sun (azimuth ~150, elevation 2.6), irradiance 4.0, angle 2 deg, colour #F4D98A.
- Bounce: a 2.0 m area reflector from the camera's left (35 deg off axis), low, irradiance 1.1, #F1E6B8; a 1.2 m fill
  from the right at 0.5.
- Haze: a scattering volume, density 0.004 /m, anisotropy 0.45, colour #DCDD9A, to 420 m and 22 m up (above it the sky is clear of it).
- Ground: photographed black rock texture (1.4 m a tile, bump 2.0, darkened 72 %, tinted #1E1D17 at 70 %), true
  displacement 7 cm, plus modelled lava forms (pillows 2.6 m / 6 cm, 1.1 m / 4 cm, ropes 0.9 m / 3 cm, grain 30 cm and
  12 cm, swells 9 m / 5 cm); a water film (clear coat 0.3, roughness 0.33); a coarser far field to the hills (film
  roughness 0.5).
- Puddles: still water at a level, filling hollows (a chain of hollows from the speaker toward the camera, so it
  mirrors), ripple rings from drops (three layers, largest 20 cm).
- Rocks: scanned boulders, darkened and wet (one near left, two behind, 160 small blocks scattered 3 to 45 m).
- Hills: a ridge of black rock at 320 m, 20 m high, jagged, in the haze.
- Rain: 70,000 streaks from 1 m to 60 m, 5 to 16 cm long, radius 0.6 to 1.2 mm, leaning slightly with the wind;
  each a water trace, opacity 0.55, refracting (roughness 0.25) and scattering the light forward.
- Splashes: 450 crowns 4 to 11 mm across on the puddles near the speaker.
- Render: AgX, Medium High Contrast, exposure -0.35, white balance 7500 K, tint -18 (toward green).
- Finish: contrast 0.18, clarity 0.07, sharpen 0.25, vignette 0.14.
