# Card: render-c5 (proof size 1200 x 800, 128 samples, denoised; the final is 2400 x 1600)

**What the frame is for.** A campaign image for a loudspeaker colour called Acid (golden yellow; its line: "A yellow
with opinions."), shown in its element: acid rain, real. A storm in sulphurous yellow-green light over wet black
volcanic rock; real rain as a 1/60 s shutter records it (thin streaks, no strings), splashes and ripple rings in the
puddles; puddles mirror the speaker; the light breaks through low behind the rain so the streaks catch it. For a
luxury brand's homepage and look book; the product sells at about $20,000 a pair.

**Fixed (do not change).** The speaker: a 1293 mm tall horn loudspeaker, a cabinet of 20 mm cast acrylic tinted golden
amber, lined in white, under a horn in tinted resin (it stands on two short posts above the cabinet, by design);
cover off; its shape, colour and parts.

**May change (current settings).**
- Camera: height 0.85 m, 9.2 m from the speaker, azimuth -12 deg (from its left), 80 mm lens, f/4.5, focus on the
  front face, verticals level. Speaker turned 16 deg, standing about 2 cm deep in water.
- Sky: photographed HDRI (an overcast sunset: a dark cloud deck over a bright band low on the horizon, the sun 2.6 deg
  up), turned so the sun is behind the speaker, 20 deg right of the camera's axis (just out of frame right); strength 0.24, 4k.
- Sun lamp: matched to the sky's sun (azimuth 148, elevation 2.6), irradiance 11.0, angle 2 deg, colour #E8DC7A. Nothing on
  the sun's line shades the speaker.
- Lamps a photographer would bring: a 2 m reflector from the camera's left (irradiance 0.9, #F1E6B8), a 1.2 m fill from
  the right (0.2), a small 0.5 m card low in front aimed into the horn's throat (0.5).
- Haze: one box, 0.0012 /m, to 400 m and 15 m up, colour #C8CC6A, anisotropy 0.5.
- Ground: photographed black rock texture (0.8 m a tile, bump 3.0, darkened 55 %, tinted #1E1D17 at 70 %), true
  displacement 7 cm, plus modelled lava forms (pillows, ropes, grain from 9 m down to 12 cm); a water film (clear coat
  0.3, its roughness varying 0.05 to 0.35 in 25 cm patches); the same rock coarser on the far field (3 m a tile,
  film roughness 0.18), flat to the horizon (no hills).
- Puddles: still water at a level, filling hollows (a chain of hollows from the speaker toward the camera, so it
  mirrors), ripple rings from drops in four layers (largest 20 cm, smallest about 3 cm), surface roughness 0.02.
- Rocks: scanned boulders, darkened and wet (one near left, two behind, 160 blocks at 3 to 45 m, 220 larger at 45 to
  150 m, 40 more at 25 to 80 m).
- Rain, three layers: 30 drops at 1.5 to 4 m (far out of focus), 90,000 streaks at 5 to 60 m (4 to 18 cm long, radius
  0.8 to 1.4 mm, opacity 0.1 to 0.6 drawn per streak, fading to their ends, none ending on the ground), 300,000 finer
  at 15 to 120 m (opacity 0.08); each a water trace, refracting (roughness 0.25) and scattering the light forward;
  leaning slightly with the wind.
- Splashes: 3,000 crowns 4 to 10 mm across over the puddle chain, opacity 0.25.
- Render: AgX, Medium High Contrast, exposure -0.35, white balance 6200 K, tint -18 (10 is neutral; lower is greener).
- Finish: contrast 0.22, clarity 0.07, sharpen 0.35, vignette 0.14.
