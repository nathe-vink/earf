# Poison P1 and P2: indoors without plants (round 31f), stopped

**The ask:** the owner, 2026-10-20: "poison's still doesn't communicate poison to me. The purple plants also look fake relative to everything else." The coordinator's response was two indoor concepts built on glass:
- **P1:** the poison cabinet of a Victorian pharmacy.
- **P2:** a chemist's poison store.

**Built:**
- `kit_ext/poison_p_el.py`: the bottles (hollow hex, ribbed, round and square glass, with corks, stoppers, caps and liquid), the drawn labels (Victorian POISON with a skull and crossbones, and GHS DANGER with the skull diamond and UN number), the shelving, a brass scale and hazard tape.
- `poison_p1.py` and `poison_p2.py`: the two sets.

**P1 proof** (`renders/2026-10-18/barkhorn/proofs31/element-poison_p1-p01.jpg`, 1200 × 800, 128 samples): fails the read test at 600 px. The judge's verdict:
- **The place:** "an old apothecary or a bar's back room".
- **The danger:** glass bottles on the floor to trip on, with no poison named.
- **Real or CG:** "a computer render": the bottles repeat a few models in neat rows, with no dust or wear and no labels you could read. At that size the labels are a few pixels each.

**P2** was stopped before its proof finished.

**Stopped:** the owner, 2026-10-20, via the coordinator: "Took a look at poison. It needs to be outside and incorporate nature. It loses the EARF vibe if not." The bottle models stay committed; nothing more of P1 or P2 is to be rendered.
