# Poison F: a forest of real poisons (round 31h)

## The brief

The owner, 2026-10-20, verbatim: "For poison, the algae idea is too goofy. What other poisonous plants or animals are there? You could combine a lot of them into a forest or rainforest biome, alongside the red caps."

The lead's corrected brief adds two earlier notes from the owner:
- "purple plants (foxgloves) look fake": no foxglove, no monkshood, nothing purple.
- "Needs shrooms around it, or a snake, or both."

The two biomes:
- **F1, a European poison wood:** fly agarics, an adder, a fire salamander, lords-and-ladies, deadly nightshade's berries, yew and death caps.
- **F2, a tropical rainforest:** dart frogs, an eyelash viper, angel's trumpet, castor bean, dumb cane, rosary pea, and the red caps.

**Gates:**
- The read test: one sentence at 600 px from a fresh judge. It must name poisonous or toxic plants or mushrooms.
- A position-balanced blind duel against the live A4.
- Finals at 2400 × 1600 and 1600 × 2000.

N2 (the algae lake) was dropped; it never went live.

## Sources (2026-10-20)

**Animals:** there is no CC0 scan of an adder, a fire salamander, a dart frog or an eyelash viper:
- Poly Haven: no snake, frog or salamander.
- ambientCG: none.
- Smithsonian Open Access 3D (3d-api.si.edu, no key needed): a fossil boa's vertebrae, nothing living.

Modelled snakes read as toys (`poison_a2.md`), so under the brief's rule the animals are left out. The plants and fungi carry the read.

**Settings:**
- The photographed woods, from Poly Haven: F1 `autumn_forest_01` (beech in autumn over golden litter and mossy boulders), F2 `rainforest_trail`.
- Litter: F1 `leaves_forest_ground`, F2 `mud_forest`.
- Beech bark: `chinese_hackberry_bark` (smooth grey with lichen).

**Poisons:**
- F1: fly agarics and death caps from the kit's `amanita` piece; lords-and-ladies in berry (`poison_p_el.arum`, new); nightshade from the kit.
- F2: the one toxic tropical scan, `anthurium_botany_01` (an aroid like dumb cane, with calcium oxalate).
- No scans or models: angel's trumpet, castor bean, rosary pea.

## Duel: the live A4 against F1 proof 6

`critic/ab/PROMPT-DUEL.txt`, 1200 × 800 each. Four fresh judges, two per order. Position is balanced, but each judge saw one order only.

**Mean:** A4 6.53, F1 5.0. All four prefer A4, so F1 is 1.5 behind and does not go up (the bar was within about 0.5).

**What drove it, in all four:**
1. **The caps:** oversized, repeated "clones… kitbashed", crowding the foreground and upstaging the product. A4's five small caps stay a quiet accent.
2. **The light:** pass 5's low golden backlight left the speaker flat-lit. Its woofer reads as "a flat dark disc", where A4's sun gives shafts, rim highlights on the surround and modelled faces.
3. **The set:**
   - the beech trunk reads as "a flat grey slab… a billboard" against A4's redwood bark;
   - the litter-covered bank behind reads as "a murky, flat brown haze… a texture card";
   - the yews look "pasted-in" and "floating";
   - the berry spikes and nightshade clutter the lower right.

## The owner's choice, and the realism pass

The owner, verbatim: "Poison: A, the new pass". The note, verbatim: "Poison needs more realism too." So F1b is the picture. One realism pass before the finals, aimed at what the judges and the A4 calibration named:
- **Caps:** forest-lit scarlet (dull 0.12–0.24) rather than glowing; four sizes; some torn; stems sunk, with moss and litter over the bases (one strew per group: a strew scatters over the whole ground before trimming to its circle, and one per cap ran out of memory).
- **Berries:** translucent (SSS 0.45, scale 6 mm), a waxy bloom (roughness 0.3), uneven sizes, 15% missing.
- **Death caps:** two, sunk into the moss.
- **A4's own tells:** the moss mat's edge made ragged and patchy; each fern its own scale and lean; wood sorrel clumps (shrub_sorrel_01); the litter's repeat broken (`poison_p_el.detile`: a second lookup, turned 37° and scaled 1.37, under a broad noise mask).
