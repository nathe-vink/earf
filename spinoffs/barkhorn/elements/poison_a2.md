The four finals are all on the new drivers, each stitched from two strips with the finish applied once:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Landscape | A2 first | 6.8 | 6.2 | A |
| Landscape | A first | 6.5 | 6.0 | A |

**A wins every round.** On average, A2 trails by 0.55 on the landscape and by 0.5 on the phone frame, which is right at the margin.

The judges agreed on three faults in A2:
- **The agarics.** They read as toy-like CG, with saturated caps and white dots. They are the reddest thing in the frame, so they pull the eye off the green product. In one frame a fern frond crosses a cap.
- **The foxgloves.** They read as strings of pink beads.
- **The light shafts.** Three judges out of four say A2 lost A's shafts. This is my change: the sun disc went from 0.6° to 2° to soften the shadow across the woofer ring, and the wider disc also blurs the shafts in the haze. The fix cost more than the flaw it fixed.

A2 won one point: the fuller fern stand at the trunk's foot.

The judges said the product itself is identical in both and caps both scores. One noted a hard horizontal shadow band across the woofer bay, which the 2° sun was meant to soften.
