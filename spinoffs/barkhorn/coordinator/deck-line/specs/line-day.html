The line

Four pieces, named for the Earth.

Pendant

Hemisphere

A shade that swaps in a quarter turn.

Table lamp

Core

Cordless, about five hours a charge.

Record player

Mantle

Floats on its own light.

Speaker

Tropos

A powered horn loudspeaker.

A render, in Poison.

Each piece takes its name from the Earth. Tropos is Greek for a turn; the troposphere, the layer of air where sound travels, is named for it. Core, Mantle and Hemisphere are the planet’s own parts. Here all four share one room, in Poison, one of the eight colors. It is a render, not a photograph: Tropos with its cover off, Core cordless.
