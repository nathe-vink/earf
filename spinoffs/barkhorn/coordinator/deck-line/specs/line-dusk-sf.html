The line

Four pieces, named for the Earth.

Pendant

Hemisphere

A shade that swaps in a quarter turn.

Table lamp

Core

Cordless, about five hours a charge.

Record player

Mantle

Floats on its own light.

Speaker

Tropos

A powered horn loudspeaker.

A render, in Poison.

Each piece takes its name from the Earth. Tropos is Greek for a turn; the troposphere, the layer of air we live in, is named for it. Core, Mantle and Hemisphere are the planet’s own parts. Here all four share one room at dusk with the lights on, in Poison, one of the eight colors, at the darker stock tint the line now uses: Tropos’s thick cabinet reads near-black and its horn deep green. It is a render, not a photograph: Tropos with its stepped horn and its cover off, on its lit plinth; Core cordless; Mantle as Strata Float, its deck floating on steel posts over a strip of warm light; Hemisphere with its color outside. A daytime version of the same room exists.
