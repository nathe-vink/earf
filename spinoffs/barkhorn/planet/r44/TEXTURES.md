# Round 44: photographed surfaces

From look 2's round 3 (the coordinator, 31 October 2026: "Photographed CC0 textures are allowed ... procedural-only surfaces are why every lane's critics keep saying CG"), the room's oak, plaster, leather and rug are scans. `fetch_textures44.sh` fetches them; the binaries live in `studio/assets/cache/`, which git ignores. `kit44.SCANS = False` puts the procedural surfaces back.
