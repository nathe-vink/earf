# Mother EARF at 2 m: technical sheet and production budget (Amsterdam Light Festival, Edition 16)

Round 43, 31 October 2026. Drafts for the owner; **nothing is submitted.** The festival's call closes 7 December 2026,
23:59 CET. The concept text, artist statement and portal checklist are the brand lane's
(`r47-brand: spinoffs/barkhorn/brandbook/festival/amsterdam/`); this sheet fills its bracketed figures.

**What it is.** Mother EARF as designed and signed (rounds 33 to 41): six slotted discs of cast acrylic, three
meridians and three parallels, the axis leaning 23.4°, each disc tinted through in its own colour (green, orange and
violet meridians; red, blue and yellow parallels), the polished rims glowing. **The 2 m size is a proposal for the
owner**; the design as signed is 1.25 m. Every figure here is an estimate *(est.)* to be confirmed by a fabricator and a
structural engineer. Nothing has been asked of anyone.

## Wind: a correction

Earlier notes (R10, and this lane's message of 31 October) said the open discs catch **less** wind than a closed
sphere. **That is wrong for flat discs.** A flat disc face-on has a drag coefficient of about 1.2, against 0.47 for a
sphere, and the globe always shows one meridian nearly face-on and two others at an angle. Estimate *(est.)*:

| Gust | Pressure | Drag (wind normal to one meridian; the other two at 60°; 25 % shielding) | Overturning moment at 1.55 m | Weight to resist it, factor 1.5, on a 0.75 m lever |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 m/s | 390 Pa | 2.2 kN | 3.4 kNm | 0.7 t |
| 30 m/s | 560 Pa | 3.2 kN | 4.9 kNm | 1.0 t |
| 35 m/s | 770 Pa | 4.3 kN | 6.7 kNm | 1.4 t |
| 40 m/s | 1,000 Pa | 5.7 kN | 8.8 kNm | 1.8 t |

So the plinth carries **about 1.0 to 1.8 t** in all (the globe's own 0.4–0.5 t counts toward it), or it is widened, or
fixed to the quay where the festival allows it. Each disc also takes about 2 to 3 kN face-on, which is why 25 mm is
preferred at 2 m and why the slotted joints need a structural check. The festival's own site rules decide the design
gust; the engineer signs it off.

## Durability (two months outdoors, December and January)

- **Colour.** Tinted cast acrylic carries a 10-year colour guarantee for flat sheet; the guarantee does not cover
  thermoformed sheet (R10, section E3). The discs are **cut flat, never formed**, so the guarantee applies.
- **Movement.** Acrylic moves about 0.07 mm per metre per °C; a 2 m disc grows or shrinks about 1.5 mm over a
  20 °C swing. The slots get 2 mm of play and the armature's clamps float.
- **Water and cold.** The LEDs and drivers are IP67; the grooves drain at the low point; the plinth is vented.
- **Cleaning.** Mild soap and water only; solvents craze acrylic.
- **Fire.** Acrylic is combustible: the LEDs run cool, the drivers are fused and sit in the steel plinth, outside the
  acrylic.
- **Vandalism and climbing.** The lowest point at 0.55 m and the open discs invite climbing; the festival's own
  stewarding and a low barrier ring at 1 m are assumed *(est.)*.
