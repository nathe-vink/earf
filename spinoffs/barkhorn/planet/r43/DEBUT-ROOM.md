> **The debut moves to spring (owner, 2026-10-31); date and venue to be set.** The spring options are in `VENUES.md`. The 19 January dates below are kept as researched.

Researched **31 Oct 2026** from public web pages only (every date read below is 31 Oct 2026 unless stated). Nobody was contacted, and nothing was submitted or signed up for. Pages that returned 403 or 404 were not retried; they are noted. *(inference)* marks my reading, not something a source says. This is research, not legal advice.

The brief: one to three nights. The first is Tue 19 Jan 2027, and the others may be 21 to 23 Jan, during FOG week (FOG is at Fort Mason 21 to 24 Jan; its gala is 20 Jan). The room holds 60 to 120 guests, a pair of large speakers playing records, and drinks from a licensed partner. Fort Mason's Firehouse and Store House are in `STOREFRONTS.md` and are not repeated here.

## 3. The zero-proof option

**Why it needs no licence.** Under California law an "alcoholic beverage" is a liquid "which contains one-half of 1 percent or more of alcohol by volume" ([B&P §23004](https://california.public.law/codes/ca_bus_and_prof_code_section_23004)). Drinks under 0.5% ABV are outside ABC, so no Type 58 is needed and nothing counts toward the cap. At Fort Mason, the USPP permit is for "events serving alcohol", so a dry bar needs none *(inference)*. EARF may then pour itself, sell drinks or include them in a ticket *(inference)*. Exceptions: a public (not invitation-only) Fort Mason event dispensing drinks may still need the NPS Temporary Food Event permit (above). Check each product's label for 0.0% versus "<0.5%".

**What it looks like** *(inference)*. Two bartenders and a short menu of three drinks:
1. A Ghia spritz (aperitif and soda, or Le Spritz cans).
2. A NA bottled cocktail or spirit-style highball.
3. A house soda.

Allow about 2.5 drinks a guest. For 120 guests that is 300 drinks: roughly 9 bottles of Ghia at 17 pours each (about $340 retail) plus 150 Le Spritz cans (about $750 retail), with ice, garnish and glass hire on top. That gives about **$1,200 to $2,000 a night** before staff.

- **First choice: the Firehouse for 19 Jan.** Use the partner bar under FMCAC's USPP permit, which avoids ABC and the 36-event cap. Ask FMCAC now for the permit form, its fee and whether officers will be required.

- **Fallback for 21–23 Jan if Fort Mason is full:** Arader Galleries (cheap, partner can pour, 10 pm music curfew), or Palihotel for a hosted, unticketed night paid off by its own bar.
