# Storefronts for plan A-401, and fallback debut rooms

Round 43. Every listing below was read on 31 October 2026 from public broker and listing pages. Nobody was contacted. *(Inference)* marks my reasoning, and "est." marks a figure I estimated. Vacant to Vibrant publishes no storefront inventory: matches are made privately ([vibrantsf.org/faqs](https://www.vibrantsf.org/faqs)). These listings are spaces to propose to V2V or to lease directly.

**Pages that would not load (not retried):**
- Returned 403: LoopNet (253 Grant Ave), Crexi (712–714 Montgomery), Realmo (722 Montgomery), Cityfeet (30 Maiden Ln), JLL (650 Davis).
- Returned 404: CBRE 170 Post.

**Asking rent:** only one listing publishes it (22 Battery). Every other one says "contact broker".

**Plan A-401 for reference:** the plan is 25 ft wide and 50 ft deep, front to back:
- the shop, 21 ft, which includes the window plinth (about 1.5 m, or 5 ft);
- the bar, 11 ft;
- the listening room behind the drapes, 12 ft;
- the back with the WC, 6 ft.

**How the plan adapts** *(inference)*:
- **Shallower than 50 ft:** fold the bar into the shop. The lean run has no fixed bar, and the portable bar stands in the shop on catered nights. That gives a 39 ft minimum (21 + 12 + 6), or 33 ft with a 15 ft shop.
- **Wider than about 35 ft:** put the listening room beside the shop instead of behind it, as two bays.
- **Narrower than 20 ft:** the ten chairs no longer fit as two rows of five facing the stacks. Use one row of six and a bench.
