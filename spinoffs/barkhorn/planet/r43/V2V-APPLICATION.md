# Vacant to Vibrant: the application, drafted

Round 43. Researched 31 October 2026 from public web pages only. Nobody was contacted, and nothing was filled in or submitted. *(Inference)* marks my own reasoning. Every date in brackets is the date I read the page.

> **FLAG 1: EARF is not a registered business yet.**
> The programme's public pages do not say that applicants must be registered. But a business plan, a grant, a lease (SF New Deal signs the lease, below) and the shop's first sale all need a legal entity *(inference)*. San Francisco requires registration within 30 days of starting business ([SF Treasurer](https://sftreasurer.org/business/register-business), read 31 Oct 2026; sf.gov's FBN guide says 15 days: [sf.gov](https://www.sf.gov/step-by-step--file-fictitious-business-name-fbn), read 31 Oct 2026). The minimum set, with official cost and time, is below. Allow about 3 weeks for the LLC route and 1 to 2 months for the sole-proprietor route, because of the newspaper notice.
>
> | Step | Cost | Time | Source (read 31 Oct 2026) |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | EIN (IRS) | Free | "Immediately online" | [irs.gov](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number) |
> | California LLC: Articles (LLC-1) | $70 | Online, "5 days or less"; on 30 Oct 2026 SOS was reviewing online LLC filings received 27 Oct | [SOS forms](https://sos.ca.gov/business-programs/business-entities/forms/limited-liability-companies-california-domestic); [SOS processing times](https://www.sos.ca.gov/business-programs/business-entities/processing-times) |
> | LLC Statement of Information (LLC-12) | $20 | Due within 90 days of the Articles (third-party sources only) | [SOS forms](https://sos.ca.gov/business-programs/business-entities/forms/limited-liability-companies-california-domestic) |
> | LLC annual franchise tax | $800 a year | Third-party sources only. FTB's page returned 403, so this is not confirmed from an official page | ftb.ca.gov (blocked) |
> | *Or* a sole proprietorship trading as "EARF": Fictitious Business Name | About $65 for the first name (County Clerk form, 07/24); other city pages say $57 or $66 | Publish in a court-approved SF paper for 4 weeks, "typically 1 to 2 months" | [sf.gov FBN](https://www.sf.gov/step-by-step--file-fictitious-business-name-fbn); [County Clerk form](https://www.sf.gov/sites/default/files/2022-03/Fictitious%20Business%20Name%20Statement%20%2801.22%29.pdf) |
> | SF Business Registration | $55 + $4 state fee (2026-27, up to $100k of SF gross receipts) | Certificate mailed 10 to 15 business days after payment. Needs a FEIN/SSN, the legal structure and the location address | [SF Treasurer](https://sftreasurer.org/business/register-business) |
> | CA seller's permit (CDTFA) | Free online | Not stated. Sales at one location for 90 days or less need a *temporary* seller's permit | [CDTFA](https://www.cdtfa.ca.gov/services/permits-licenses.htm) |
>
> The fastest compliant path *(inference)* is an EIN on day 1, an LLC filed online on day 1 (live by about day 5), SF registration on about day 6 (certificate by about day 20), and a seller's permit once the address is known.

> **FLAG 2: Vacant to Vibrant cannot reliably deliver a storefront for 19 January.**
> Review takes 30 to 45 days. After that, "the matching process can take time and may range from two to six months" ([vibrantsf.org/faqs](https://www.vibrantsf.org/faqs), read 31 Oct 2026). An application sent in the first week of November would get a decision in early to mid-December and a space between February and June 2027 *(inference)*. POPUP.md's "decision about mid-December, sign the short lease in December" leaves out the matching step.

> **FLAG 3: the funding is corrected (the coordinator, 31 Oct).** The $50,000 product raise is fully allocated and does not pay for the pop-up. See §5.
