# Planet EARF: venues for a spring debut (two windows: Thu 18 March and late April 2027)

The owner chose "C" on 31 Oct 2026, so **the debut moves to spring 2027. The owner sets the date and the venue.** This file lists the options for that choice, ranked, and makes one pick for each window. It sits beside `DEBUT-ROOM.md`, which holds the January research: the Fort Mason drinks rules and the zero-proof bar still apply.

**How this was researched.** Everything comes from public web pages read on 31 Oct 2026. Page dates are given where the page shows one. Nobody was contacted, and nothing was booked, held or applied for. *(inference)* marks my reasoning; *(guess)* marks a figure no source gives. No venue publishes availability for March or April 2027, so every date below has to be asked for.

## What the debut needs

- **Guests:** 60 to 120.
- **Sound:** the one Tropos pair, playing records at real listening volume.
- **The globe:** Mother EARF at 1.25 m.
- **Drinks:** a licensed partner bar (`PARTNERS.md`), or zero-proof.
- **Budget:** **$4,000**. This is the debut line in the $50,000 ask (`spinoffs/barkhorn/README.md` on r46-owner-notes). It has to cover the room, permits, drinks and photography.

## Two facts that apply to every room

1. **Mother EARF will stand on a plinth, not hang.** At 1.25 m in 25 mm acrylic it weighs about **190 to 208 kg** (`../README.md`, round 39). It hangs only "from the slab on a rated fixing … and needs an engineer". No rented room offers that.
   - Fort Mason's Firehouse limits each beam point to **50 lb** (FMCAC packet, below).
   - On a low plinth it fits any room with about 2 m of clear floor *(inference)*.
   - It also has to exist by then. It is about $9,000 to $12,000 one-off and is **not** in the $50,000 ask, so the owner has to decide whether it is built for the debut.

### 1. Gray Area, the Grand Theater (2665 Mission St, Mission)

A 1940 movie palace that Gray Area runs as an art-and-technology venue. Its rooms, from the [venue rental page](https://grayarea.org/services/venue-rental/) (modified 7 Apr 2026):

| Room | Size | Capacity | Seated |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grand Theater (the whole ground floor) | 6,000 sq ft | 800 | 450 |
| **Half Theater** (stage, lobby and bar) | 4,000 sq ft | 400 | 250 |
| **Lobby** ("large windows facing lively Mission Street") | 800 sq ft | 80 | 50 |

The page offers "modern sound, light, and visual capabilities … and full bar packages". It gives no current rates; bookings go through an Airtable form, and 501(c)(3) groups get community rates.

**Cost.** The only published prices are on the [2019 rate sheet](https://grayarea.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/Gray-Area-Venue-Rental-2019.pdf):
- Half Theater **$3,000 a day**; Lobby $1,000; Grand $5,000. Each includes 8 hours; extra hours $150.
- Deposit: 20%, non-refundable. $1M liability insurance required.
- Required staff: one security guard per 100 guests at $35 an hour; housekeeping at $25 an hour; a technician at $50 an hour.

| Room | 2019 total | 2027 estimate |
|---|---|---|
| Half Theater, 120 guests (two guards and a technician for about 6 hours) | about **$3,600–4,000** *(inference)* | **$4,000–5,000** *(guess)* |
| Lobby | about $1,300 | — |

**Drinks.** The bar is in-house ("full bar packages"). Our partner probably cannot pour *(inference)*. The bar's takings or a package are separate from the room fee.

**Lead time.** Not published. Guess: book about 3 months ahead, so by **mid-December** for 18 March and **mid-January** for 29 April.

**Tropos at volume:** **yes.** A theatre with its own sound system and no homes in the hall *(inference)*.

**Mother EARF:** on a plinth on the stage or in the lobby. The 2019 sheet lists plinths at $5. A theatre may have a rigging grid; ask, but plan on the plinth.

**In photographs** *(inference)*: a high, ornate 1940 hall with a proscenium. It reads as an art event: the speakers on a stage, the globe glowing in the dark. The Lobby, with its big windows on Mission Street, makes the better street-and-glass picture.

**Why first.** It is the only room with published prices near the line, the capacity to take 120 without seatings, and sound as its core business.

**Risks:**
- 2027 prices are unknown.
- Our partner bar is probably excluded.
- The Mission, not downtown or the Marina.

### 2. The Vacant to Vibrant pop-up (the debut in our own storefront)

**The idea.** Apply now and hold the debut as the opening night of the storefront the programme matches us with (`V2V-APPLICATION.md`, `STOREFRONTS.md`).

**The timeline.** Review takes "30 to 45 days"; then "the matching process can take time and may range from two to six months" ([vibrantsf.org/faqs](https://www.vibrantsf.org/faqs), read 31 Oct 2026). An application sent the week of 2 November gets a decision in early to mid-December and a space between February and June 2027 *(inference)*.

**Cost to the debut line.** The shop itself is paid by the grant ($2,000 to $10,000), partners and deposits, not by this line (`../r42/POPUP.md`). Free rent was promised in 2023; the current FAQ does not say so. The debut night adds:
- the partner's Type 58 authorization, $100 a night;
- one indoor-event permit, $586;
- glassware, photography and a door person.

That comes to about **$1,500 to $3,000** *(guess)*, **under the $4,000 line**.

**Lead time and the latest date for a decision.**
- **18 March is not realistic.** It would need a match by about **1 February**, leaving 4 to 6 weeks for:
  - the lease;
  - a TUA if the last use was not retail (about 2 weeks);
  - the build-in (about 1 week);
  - the permits.
  
  That is the very start of the "two to six months" window *(inference)*.
- **29 April is possible.** It needs a match by about **15 March** *(inference)*. If none has come by then, fall back to option 1 or 3, and hold the storefront's opening night later as a second event.

**Capacity: the catch.** Plan A-401 is drawn for occupancy **under 50** (planned 36), and V2V favours spaces under 5,000 sq ft. 60 to 120 guests do not fit at once *(inference)*. Two ways round it:
- **Seatings:** three of about 40 on the night, the listening room taking ten at a time.
- **Two nights:** Thursday and Friday.

Both use more of the partner's 36 Type 58 nights.

**Tropos at volume:** it depends on the space. The shortlist favours ground floors with no homes above: 311 Grant is "fully vacant"; 333 O'Farrell has hotel rooms above (`STOREFRONTS.md`).

**Mother EARF:** on the window plinth, as in the round 43 renders. Hanging it needs the landlord's consent and an engineer.

**In photographs** *(inference)*: the best of all. The debut happens in EARF's own room, matching the renders: the window, the shop, the listening room. Every later visit looks like the debut photos.

**Why second.** It is cheapest and photographs best, but its date is not ours to choose, and it cannot hold 120 at once.

### 3. Fort Mason Firehouse (2 Marina Blvd)

**The room.** 27 × 44 ft and 14 ft high, 1,188 sq ft. 120 empty, 110 theatre, 80 banquet; the NPS fire marshal sets the final number from the floor plan ([FMCAC packet](https://fortmason.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/09/firehouse_info_pkt_2024.pdf), PDF dated 9 Oct 2024).

**Cost.**

| Source | Rate | Block |
|---|---|---|
| [Here Comes the Guide](https://www.herecomestheguide.com/wedding-venues/northern-california/fort-mason-center) (updated 12 Oct 2026) | **$4,200–6,500** "depending on day of week, time of year, and space reserved" | a 10-hour rental |
| [Wedding-Spot](https://www.wedding-spot.com/venue/148/fort-mason-center-for-arts-culture/) (undated, likely older) | **$1,760–5,500** | half-day and full-day options |

Wedding-Spot also lists a 10% historical preservation fee. No spring, weekday or non-profit rate is published. A full day is **$600 to $3,150 over the line** after the fee. A weekday half-day might come in under it *(inference)*.

**Drinks.** The partner pours under a **US Park Police permit through FMCAC**, due 6 weeks ahead; no ABC filing (`DEBUT-ROOM.md` §2). That is **by 4 Feb** for 18 March and **by 18 Mar** for 29 April. The floor plan and vendor list are due 4 weeks ahead.

**Lead time.** Not published; call (415) 345-7557. Guess: book by **mid-January** for 18 March and by mid-February for April.

**Tropos at volume.** Yes, indoors. FMCAC "reserves the right to request a demonstration of volume levels" and to "enforce or request changes". Music ends by 11 pm (Wedding-Spot).

**Mother EARF.** **Plinth only**: "wood beam construction which does not allow for substantial weight to be hung … a maximum load of 50lbs" a point (packet, Appendix C).

**In photographs** *(inference)*: a white historic firehouse by the bay, with a 14 ft beamed ceiling; clean and calm.

**The date.** **Thu 18 March falls on the Superfair's first day at Pier 2** (if the aggregator's dates hold), so parking and rooms will be tight. The late-April window is clear unless the Art Fair slips.

**Why third.** It was the January first choice, but it is over the line and the globe cannot hang.

### 5. A hi-fi shop or listening-bar partner

**AudioVision SF.** It has moved to **1628 California St** (Nob Hill) ([Nordost](https://nordost.com/blog/venue/audio-vision-san-francisco/)). Its three listening rooms are "relatively small but treated", for one or two listeners ([Archimago, 21 Feb 2026](https://archimago.blogspot.com/2026/02/images-from-san-francisco-audio-vision.html)). It runs its own weekend events, such as the Reference Listening Experience on 16–17 May 2026. No rental is offered. **Too small for the debut**, but a natural co-host for a press listening the week before *(inference)*. Its outreach email is already drafted (owner's Gmail drafts).

**Side A** (2814 19th St, Mission). A restaurant and vinyl listening lounge, opened 1 May 2025 ([Resy, Aug 2025](https://blog.resy.com/2025/08/side-a-san-francisco/)). No published capacity, sound system or buyout terms. A buyout would be a minimum spend, and the Tropos would replace its own system: a special favour *(inference)*. Its outreach email is also drafted.

**Bar Shiru** (Oakland). Group bookings are only for 7 to 15 people ([Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/san-francisco/bars/bar-shiru)). No.

**Why fifth.** The right audience, the wrong size. Use them as partners (a pre-listening, a selector, a guest list), not as the room.
