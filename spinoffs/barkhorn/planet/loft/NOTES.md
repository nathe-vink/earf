`loft_card.py`, the measuring tool), its reply verbatim in `critic/VIEW-rN.json`, and what was done.

(median 27.9 vs 19.4, crush 0.07 vs 0.98 %, clipping 0 vs 0.3 %, blue median 19 vs 2): AgX. The screens' images and
the niches' glow were warmed (they read blue-white at 3300 K).
