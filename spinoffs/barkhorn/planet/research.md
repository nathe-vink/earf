# Planet EARF: reference research for the mockup

*Reference brief for the Planet EARF 3D mockups, compiled 18 October 2026. Claims link to their sources, and my own inferences are marked.*

## Summary

The best listening bars treat the speakers as furniture and the room as an instrument: horns go in the corners or into the back-bar, the record wall doubles as a diffuser, the light stays calm and absorption is hidden everywhere. Planet EARF can do the same in colour. Put Atmosphere pairs in the corners and in lit back-bar niches ([Brilliant Corners](https://thespaces.com/brilliant-corners-dalston-listening-bar/); [All Blues](https://musictech.com/features/interviews/inside-5-of-nyc-best-hi-fi-bars-suono-all-blues-nowadays-public-records-nightmoves)). Make the bar front one back-lit spectrum of the eight colours, following [Kuramata's back-lit acrylic](https://www.mplus.org.hk/en/collection/objects/kiyotomo-sushi-bar-2013306/), [Eliasson's rainbow ring](https://www.designboom.com/art/olafur-eliasson-your-rainbow-panorama) and [Buren's 13 filters](https://www.dezeen.com/2016/05/12/daniel-buren-colours-sails-frank-gehry-fondation-louis-vuitton-site-specific-installation-paris/). Let the light move through the colours over the night, as [Turrell's sequences](https://www.architecturalrecord.com/articles/7899-twilight-epiphany) do. Acrylic sets two rules. It reflects sound, so pair it with micro-perforated panels, lit acoustic ceilings and absorber behind tinted slats. High-proof alcohol crazes it, so the working bar top should be glass or stone ([ACRYLITE chart](https://www.usplastic.com/catalog/files/44251chemicalchart11-2003.pdf)). In San Francisco, three early choices drive most of the permitting and cost: 49 occupants or 50 and over, DJs or records played by staff, and a Type 48 bar licence or a food-led restaurant.

## 1. Listening bars worth learning from

**Bar Shiru, Oakland.** The Bay Area's first analog-only vinyl bar opened in 2019.
- *Room:* Studio KDA split a 1,500 sq ft double-height concrete shell into small zones around a communal area. It added 2 in acoustic ceiling panels, jazz photos that double as absorbers, and maple screens plus a 15 ft record wall that diffuse sound ([Studio KDA](https://studiokda.com/project/bar-shiru/)).
- *Sound:* Line Magnetic LM-812 horn-loaded two-ways, modelled on the Lansing Iconic, sit at the foot of the shelves on tube amps ([The Absolute Sound](https://www.theabsolutesound.com/articles/bar-shiru)).
- *Rules:* parties are capped at six ([7x7](https://www.7x7.com/hi-fi-bars-san-francisco-bay-area-2659499464.html)), and Sundays have a silent hour ([KALW](https://www.kalw.org/2026-05-28/listening-bars-challenge-us-to-sit-down-shut-up-and-tune-in)).
- *Business:* after seven years it has yet to turn a profit; these bars are labour-intensive ([KALW, 2026](https://www.kalw.org/2026-05-28/listening-bars-challenge-us-to-sit-down-shut-up-and-tune-in)).

**San Francisco's current crop.** None is known to be built around horns or colour.
- **Harlan Records** (an alley off Union Square): McIntosh and Thorens, a wall of more than 2,000 records behind the bar, walnut shelving and leather booths. The bartenders choose the records ([Offbeat SF](https://offbeatsf.com/venue/harlan-records/)).
- **Le Fantastique** (Hayes Valley): vintage McIntosh with oversized speakers and a private "record room" ([7x7](https://www.7x7.com/hi-fi-bars-san-francisco-bay-area-2659499464.html)).
- **Bar Crenn** (Cow Hollow): Ethan Tobman's geometric wood panelling under a gold ceiling ([7x7](https://www.7x7.com/hi-fi-bars-san-francisco-bay-area-2659499464.html)).
- **Phonobar** (370 Grove St): a custom DJ console and a small dance floor ([SF Standard](https://sfstandard.com/2022/07/18/3-bars-in-sf-that-only-play-vinyl-records/)).
- **Side A** (Mission): more than 1,000 records ([SF Chronicle](https://www.sfchronicle.com/food/restaurants/article/side-a-music-menu-20217806.php)).
- **Yokai** (SoMa) has closed ([The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/san-francisco/reviews/yokai)).

**JBS, Tokyo.** A pair of Thorens TD-124s and a Mark Levinson preamp feed a custom tube amp driving Altec speakers. Wood-clad walls hold more than 10,000 records beside a long steel-topped bar. The room is softly lit and the volume moderate ([The Vinyl Factory](https://thevinylfactory.com/features/jbs-jazz-bar-a-vinyl-sanctuary-in-the-midst-of-tokyo/)).

**Bar Martha, Tokyo.** Large Tannoy GRFs run on McIntosh and Audio Research amps, with Garrard 301 and EMT turntables ([StereoNet](https://stereonet.com/forums/topic/577073-listening-bar-bar-marthatokyo)). Behind an austere door with a single neon sign are the records and a nine-page whisky list, and the volume rises through the evening ([Pen](https://pen-online.com/travel/le-bar-martha-ladresse-de-quartier-par-excellence/)).

**Basie (Ichinoseki) and Eigakan (Tokyo).** Sugawara has run the same JBL system at Basie since opening around 1970 ([Openers](https://openers.jp/en/lounge/lounge_movie/20200904-basie)). Records line the wall behind the bar and two more walls ([Tokyo Jazz Joints](https://tokyojazzsite.com/?p=2694)). At Eigakan, the owner cut horn speakers from Japanese ash himself, and they loom over the small café ([National Geographic](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/culture/article/japanese-jazz-kissa-cafe)). This is the precedent for the horn as sculpture.

**Meikyoku Kissa Lion, Tokyo.** A two-storey classical "listening theatre" (founded 1926, rebuilt 1950). Its 3 m wooden speakers are built into the back wall "like a church pipe organ". Talking is not allowed, and LP concerts are played at 3 pm and 7 pm ([The Vinyl Factory](https://www.thevinylfactory.com/features/meikyoku-kissa-lion-tokyo)).

**Spiritland, London.** About 2,200 sq ft ([The Vinyl Factory](https://www.thevinylfactory.com/news/spiritland-kings-cross-space)) with around 70 seats ([Zipcube](https://www.zipcube.com/us/ap/event/e65s1ydhvhr8)). At the far end is a one-off Living Voice system: two "colossal" subwoofers, horn-loaded mids and a 300B valve amp. A long bar runs into an open kitchen, with radio studios behind glass ([Electronic Sound](https://www.electronicsound.co.uk/features/long-reads/spiritland/)). A Barbican branch opens on 20 October 2026 with Living Voice R252s ([DJ Mag](https://djmag.com/news/new-spiritland-listening-bar-open-londons-barbican-centre)).

**Brilliant Corners, London.** Two pairs of 1970s Klipschorns stand in the dining room's four corners, driven by McIntosh tube amps through an Isonoe valve mixer and analog end to end. The room is deliberately humble, lit by moon-like globes "without superfluous colour or flashing" ([The Spaces](https://thespaces.com/brilliant-corners-dalston-listening-bar/)).

**Space Talk, London.** EBBA and Charlotte Taylor arranged four zones, each with its own sound and light, around a curved steel bar. Concealed absorptive panelling, designed with acoustician Ethan Bourdeau, covers more than half the wall area ([Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/2024/11/10/space-talks-listening-bar-london-ebba-charlotte-taylor/amp/)).

**Public Records, Brooklyn.** A 6,000 sq ft compound whose Sound Room, lined in perforated plywood, holds 200 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Records_(venue))). Each corner stacks two Void subwoofers, an Ojas Quad 15, an Altec 1505B multicell horn and a super-tweeter. Devon Turnbull (Ojas) and Global Audio designed the system on Altec principles ([ProSoundWeb](https://www.prosoundweb.com/global-audio-adds-custom-sound-to-brooklyns-public-records-venue/)). Upstairs, Arup gave the walls an undulating profile beneath an acoustic-fabric ceiling ([Hospitality Design](https://hospitalitydesign.com/news/f-b/upstairs-public-records-brooklyn/)).

**All Blues, New York.** In the listening theatre, a JBL Paragon flanked by Hartsfields faces leather chairs. Behind the bar, JBL 4343s sit in the cabinets beside the bottles and play in mono to suit the long, narrow bar. Loud guests get "yellow cards" ([MusicTech](https://musictech.com/features/interviews/inside-5-of-nyc-best-hi-fi-bars-suono-all-blues-nowadays-public-records-nightmoves)).

**Eavesdrop, Brooklyn.** A 1,000 sq ft bar by House Under Magic. Danley SH60 horns and Seaton subwoofers are built into a lit cabinet ([Hospitality Design](https://hospitalitydesign.com/news/f-b/eavesdrop-brooklyn/)); the SH60 is a 60°×60° Synergy Horn ([Danley](https://www.danleysoundlabs.com/products/sh60/)). A wall of mirrors at varying depths diffuses the sound, absorption is hidden, and the wooden ribs carry LEDs ([InsideHook](https://www.insidehook.com/drinks/eavesdrop-brooklyn-bar); [Arch Paper](https://www.archpaper.com/2022/06/brooklyn-eavesdrop-custom-elements-active-listening/)).

**Gold Line, Los Angeles.** Altec Malibus stand among the whiskies, flipped so the horns sit at ear level. Altec Valencias serve the back lounge, run on McIntosh through a Bozak mixer, and 3,000 records are within reach. Only The Wild Ones in Venice builds a subwoofer into its bar ([MusicTech](https://musictech.com/features/hi-fi-bars-los-angeles/)).

**In Sheep's Clothing, Los Angeles (closed 2020).** 1973 Klipsch corner horns, with walls designed to reinforce their output. Cork floors and reclaimed-wood walls absorb sound, and capacity was 50 ([Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/news/theres-a-hidden-cocktail-bar-and-hi-fi-lounge-in-this-arts-district-pizza-parlor-082018)).

**Bar Bon Funk, Singapore.** A 110 m² space in three zones by Nice Projects. Vintage speakers are built into a breeze-block bar topped with yellow marble ([Yellowtrace](https://www.yellowtrace.com.au/nice-projects-bar-bon-funk-singapore-cocktail-listening-bar/)), and custom Altec A5s on McIntosh serve 47 seats ([Life in Arpeggio](https://www.lifeinarpeggio.com/blog/bar-bon-funk-music-lounges-cocktails-new-bahru-audiophile-restaurants-singapore)).

## 2. Acrylic, resin and back-lit interiors

- **Shiro Kuramata.** At the Kiyotomo sushi bar (Tokyo, 1988; now at M+), a ceiling panel of metal and back-lit acrylic gives diffuse light over the seats ([M+](https://www.mplus.org.hk/en/collection/objects/kiyotomo-sushi-bar-2013306/)). His surviving Comblé bar in Shizuoka is acrylic, glass and terrazzo ([Wallpaper](https://wallpaper.com/travel/bars/comble-bar-shizuoka-shiro-kuramata)). The Oblomov bar had acrylic lamps with synthetic roses inside ([Phillips](https://phillips.com/article/142886941/beauty-suspended-in-time)).
- **Ichio Matsuzawa Office, AWT bar** (Art Week Tokyo 2025). Thick acrylic with more than 90% transparency was heat-formed into curved, free-standing partitions that bend reflections ([designboom](https://www.designboom.com/design/invisible-awtbar-distorts-reality-heated-transparent-acrylic-glass-panels-ichio-matsuzawa-office-11-20-2025/)).
- **SelgasCano.** Second Home London has a yellow acrylic tube on its façade, curving clear acrylic walls inside, and stairs wrapped in translucent yellow and orange acrylic ([Architectural Record](https://www.architecturalrecord.com/articles/12293-second-home-by-selgascano)). Their 2015 Serpentine Pavilion used double-layer coloured ETFE that filtered daylight "like a stained-glass window" and glowed at night ([Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/2015/03/25/selgascano-2015-serpentine-pavilion-design-london/amp/)).
- **Sabine Marcelis.** Her *Dawn* lights embed one white neon tube in cast resin ([Wallpaper](https://www.wallpaper.com/design/sabine-marcelis-way-with-light-and-colour-makes-her-a-fixture-in-store-interiors)). Her counter for Het Nieuwe Instituut sets glass blocks between a cast-resin top and base ([Nieuwe Instituut](https://nieuweinstituut.nl/en/projects/ontwerpplatform/sabine-marcelis)). Both are models for the EARF lamp and bar.
- **California Light and Space**, EARF's local lineage. De Wain Valentine came to Los Angeles in 1965 to teach plastics technology and cast large translucent polyester-resin sculptures ([Meer](https://www.meer.com/en/17802-dewain-valentine)). Helen Pashgian casts translucent, vividly coloured resin works ([Gazelli](https://gazelliarthouse.com/artists/47-helen-pashgian/)).
- **Charles Hollis Jones** ("Mr. Lucite"), still designing in Los Angeles ([Incollect](https://incollect.com/node/100267)). Dealer listings include his acrylic "Waterfall" barstools for Playboy's Pip's club ([1stDibs](https://www.1stdibs.com/creators/charles-hollis-jones/furniture/seating/)).
- **Daniel Buren, *Observatory of Light*** (Fondation Louis Vuitton, 2016). He applied 13 filters, from golden yellow to azure, to Gehry's glass sails, and the effect shifted with the hour and the season ([Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/2016/05/12/daniel-buren-colours-sails-frank-gehry-fondation-louis-vuitton-site-specific-installation-paris/)).
- **Dichroic film.** At Stereo Mike in Naples (Carmine Abate, 2023), dichroic films cast blue, red, yellow, orange and green across white tiles ([Domus](https://domusweb.it/en/interiors/gallery/2024/01/14/ironic-and-iridescent-a-new-bar-in-naples.html)). 3M sells such film as a retrofit for glass ([3M](https://3m.com/3M/en_US/p/d/v000096552)).
- **Translucent bar materials.** ACRYLITE's "LED colour-changing" sheet was used to clad a bar. It looks black when unlit and glows in the LEDs' colour, with no hot spots ([ACRYLITE](https://www.acrylite.co/applications/architectural-and-decorative-lighting-fixture/creating-perfect-harmony-with-this-color-changing-panel)). 3form Chroma is a thick, optical-grade resin rated for counters, and 3form lists an edge-lit Chroma countertop ([3form](https://alpha.3-form.com/materials/chroma)).
- **Acrylic horns.** The Ferguson Hill FH001 is a clear-acrylic horn speaker, Atmosphere's nearest product cousin ([Sound & Vision](https://www.soundandvision.com/content/see-through-speakers-0)).

## 3. How a back-lit acrylic bar is built

- **Edge-lit.** Light-guide acrylic (ACRYLITE LED, PLEXIGLAS EndLighten) takes LEDs 1–3 mm from a polished edge, and thicker edges take in more light ([ACRYLITE FAQ](https://www.acrylite.co/resources/faq/acrylite-led-light-guiding-edge-lit-sheet)). Each grade suits a set distance between LED strips, from up to 12 cm (LD12) to 48–96 cm (LD96) ([LD12](https://plastics.ulprospector.com/datasheet/e236063/acrylite-led-8n-ld12); [LD96](https://plastics.ulprospector.com/datasheet/e236066/acrylite-led-8n-ld96)). PLEXIGLAS LED edge-lit sheet comes in 6–8 mm for light paths of 300–600 mm lit from both sides, 8–10 mm for 600–1,200 mm, and 10 mm for up to 2,000 mm ([Plexiglas](https://plexiglas-shop.com/ie/products/plexiglas-led)). So a bar-front panel about 1 m tall, lit at its top and bottom edges, needs the 8–10 mm grade. Edge lighting is best for shelves and shallow panels.
- **Back-lit.** LED tape sits in a cavity behind a diffuser. As a rule of thumb, keep the LED pitch about equal to the distance from the diffuser, or hot spots show ([Lumistrips](https://us.lumistrips.com/lumistrips-blog/led-pitch-and-distance-to-cover-for-optimum-backlighting/)). One guide suggests 2–4 in between the LEDs and a frosted panel ([DG Floors](https://dgfloors.com/bar-shelf-lighting/)). 3form's ready-made Chroma lightbox pairs a diffusion layer with an LED bar ([3form](https://www.3-form.com/parametre/solutions-ready_to_go_solutions-200_partitions-200.34.02.php)). *My inference:* tinted 20 mm acrylic is transparent, so it needs an opal or white layer behind it to glow evenly, like Atmosphere's white lining.
- **Do not use acrylic for the bar top.**
  - *Alcohol:* ACRYLITE says alcohols are typically not resistant ([ACRYLITE](https://www.acrylite.co/resources/knowledge-base/article/what-chemicals-is-acrylite-acrylic-sheet-resistant-to)). Its chemical chart rates ethanol above 30%, which covers every spirit, "not resistant", and 15–30% as limited ([ACRYLITE chart](https://www.usplastic.com/catalog/files/44251chemicalchart11-2003.pdf)). Stress from machining or cleaning lowers the crazing threshold further ([PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10052915/)), and harsh solvents or cleaners can micro-craze it ([AIC](https://resources.culturalheritage.org/pmgtopics/2009-volume-thirteen/13_27_Casella.html)).
  - *Scratches:* standard sheet is 3H pencil hardness, abrasion-resistant coated sheet 5H ([ACRYLITE](https://www.acrylite.co/resources/knowledge-base/article/what-is-the-astm-for-the-pencil-hardness-test)).
  - *Heat:* continuous service is about 160–200°F ([eplastics](https://www.eplastics.com/blog/physical-properties-plexiglass-g-sheet); [Gund](https://thegundcompany.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/ACRYLIC-by-The-Gund-Co-TDS.pdf)), so no flamed garnishes on it.
  - *Cast versus extruded:* cast acrylic resists solvent crazing better than extruded ([Piedmont](https://www.piedmontplastics.com/products/cast-acrylic-sheet)).
  - *Food contact:* acrylic for food contact is covered by 21 CFR 177.1010 ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/177.1010)). Ask the supplier for a compliance certificate and an NSF/ANSI 51 listing.
  - Use glass or stone on top, and acrylic for the front, shelves and walls.
- **Bottle shelves.** Vendors sell 6 mm and 8 mm edge-lit acrylic shelves rated for full bottles ([MAX Illumination](https://markets.financialcontent.com/bpas/article/prlog-2026-1-21-max-illumination-redefines-cabinet-displays-with-new-edge-lit-led-technology)). Other options are LED strips along glass shelf edges, or panels set 4–6 in behind the bottles. High-CRI warm white keeps labels readable ([Toast](https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/on-the-line/bar-lighting-ideas)).
- **Rough costs.** Installed back-lit onyx costs about $120–360 per sq ft, of which backlighting is $20–50 ([DG Floors](https://dgfloors.com/onyx-countertops/)); onyx material alone runs $40–250 per sq ft ([Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/onyx-countertop-cost.htm)). I found no reliable public price for thick tinted cast acrylic or 3form Chroma, so get quotes.
- **California fabricators.**
  - TAP Plastics: San Leandro, founded 1949, 14 West Coast locations, cut-to-size and fabrication ([Cience](https://www.cience.com/company/tap-plastics/-3562568511090305397)).
  - Acrylonics: a custom acrylic fabricator in San Jose ([Cience](https://www.cience.com/company/acrylonics/7844730637816300589)).
  - Charles Hollis Jones's studio in Los Angeles (above).
  - EARF's own casting supplier is probably the best source for colour-matched panels.

## 4. Acoustics of a hard, reflective room

- **How the precedents do it.**
  - Bar Shiru: absorbing ceilings and photo panels, with screens and a record wall as diffusers ([Studio KDA](https://studiokda.com/project/bar-shiru/)).
  - Public Records: perforated plywood in the Sound Room ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Records_(venue))), and undulating walls under an acoustic-fabric ceiling upstairs ([Hospitality Design](https://hospitalitydesign.com/news/f-b/upstairs-public-records-brooklyn/)).
  - Space Talk: concealed panelling over more than half the walls ([Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/2024/11/10/space-talks-listening-bar-london-ebba-charlotte-taylor/amp/)).
  - Eavesdrop: a mirror diffuser with hidden absorption ([InsideHook](https://www.insidehook.com/drinks/eavesdrop-brooklyn-bar)).
  - In Sheep's Clothing: cork floors and reclaimed-wood walls ([Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/news/theres-a-hidden-cocktail-bar-and-hi-fi-lounge-in-this-arts-district-pizza-parlor-082018)).
- **Targets.** Aim for a reverberation time of about 0.5–0.7 s for restaurants and 0.8–1.0 s for a bar with music ([UNCuyo](https://aulaabierta.ingenieria.uncuyo.edu.ar/mod/resource/view.php?id=76054); [Commercial Acoustics](https://commercial-acoustics.com/sound-advice/rt60-chart/)). Flutter echo is likely when hard parallel surfaces are more than 3 m apart with absorption below 0.4 ([calculator](https://acoustic.samesound.ru/en/analysis/flutter-echo)), so one surface in each facing pair should absorb or scatter sound ([Vicoustic](https://vicoustic.com/blogs/articles/what-is-flutter-echo)). For the mockup: never put two acrylic walls facing each other.
- **Treatments that keep the acrylic look.**
  - *Micro-perforated acrylic.* CNC-perforated acrylic panels absorb sound without any fibrous backing, and stacking layers widens their narrow absorption band ([PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9859406/); [Forum Acusticum](https://dael.euracoustics.org/confs/fa2023/data/articles/000943.pdf)). Barrisol's Microsorber is available as clear foil or Plexiglas with 0.2–0.8 mm holes ([Archello](https://archello.com/product/microsorberr)). RPG's Clearsorber is a Class A polycarbonate foil with about 92% light transmission ([BIMobject](https://www.bimobject.com/en-us/rpg-acoustical-systems/product/clearsorber-foil-single-layer)).
  - *Lit acoustic ceilings.* Barrisol Acoustic Light A15 is translucent, made for backlighting, and rated NRC 0.55–0.80 ([NBS](https://www.nationalbimlibrary.com/en/barrisol/barrisol-acoustic-light/?lo=en)). Barrisol's A30 micro-perforated membrane rates NRC 0.55 on its own and 0.85 with insulation above ([Barrisol](https://barrisol.com/uk/stretch-ceiling/stretch-ceilings-range/barrisol-acoustics/a30-microacoustic)).
  - *Record walls.* Shelved media scatter mid and high frequencies, and irregular depths work better than even ones ([HiFi Report](https://www.hifireport.com/can-a-bookshelf-work-as-an-acoustic-diffuser-yes-with-a-few-caveats/)).
  - *Tinted panels and horns (my inference).* Set slotted or perforated tinted acrylic 50–100 mm in front of dark absorber, lit from inside the gap. Horns with controlled directivity also put less sound onto the side walls.

## 5. San Francisco specifics

- **Where it fits.**
  - **Hayes Valley.** Phonobar and Le Fantastique already form a hi-fi cluster here ([SF Standard](https://sfstandard.com/2022/07/18/3-bars-in-sf-that-only-play-vinyl-records/); [7x7](https://www.7x7.com/hi-fi-bars-san-francisco-bay-area-2659499464.html)). *My inference:* design-retail foot traffic suits an EARF showroom.
  - **SoMa.** The late-night club district ([Suiteness](https://www.suiteness.com/suites/united-states/california/san-francisco/guides/nightlife)); Folsom between 7th and 8th is now an entertainment zone ([CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/sanfrancisco/news/sf-approves-5-more-entertainment-zones-valencia-pier-39-ellis-folsom-yerba-buena)). *Inference:* its high industrial ceilings suit 1.3 m speakers.
  - **Union Square, Chinatown and the Tenderloin's edge.** Harlan's alley is the model. The Ellis Street entertainment zone and "Vacant to Vibrant" pop-ups make a low-risk trial: free rent and grants, and more than half of the pop-ups have gone on to long leases ([sf.gov](https://www.sf.gov/news-mayor-lurie-welcomes-four-new-small-businesses-to-union-square-and-downtown); [Local News Matters](https://localnewsmatters.org/2026/03/16/bcn-22-sf-pop-ups-march-15-2026/)).
  - **Mission.** Valencia from 16th to 21st is an entertainment zone ([CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/sanfrancisco/news/sf-approves-5-more-entertainment-zones-valencia-pier-39-ellis-folsom-yerba-buena)). However, the Mission Alcoholic Beverage SUD has barred new bars since the 1990s, while bona fide restaurants are exempt ([SF Examiner](https://www.sfexaminer.com/news/brewpubs-would-be-banned-from-serving-hard-liquor-in-mission-district-under-tightened-restrictions/article_bcfb0630-ea33-5327-aa17-87e3cb7d6273.html); [§249.60](https://gocodebook.com/library/us/ca/san-francisco-planning/article-2-use-districts/sec.-249.60.-mission-alcoholic-beverage-special-use-district)). A food-led concept is the practical route here.
  - **North Beach.** "Opening after opening" over the past year ([SF Standard, 2026](https://sfstandard.com/2026/01/13/15romolo-north-beach-bar-reopens/)).
  - **Dogpatch.** Bars cluster at Third and 20th, beside The Pearl events complex ([SF Chronicle](https://preview-prod.w.sfchronicle.com/food/wine/article/Meet-School-Night-Dogpatch-s-weeknight-only-bar-12989338.php); [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/san-francisco/nightlife/the-pearl)).
- **Typical sizes.** I found no published figures for San Francisco bars. The precedents above range from 1,000 to 2,200 sq ft with 47–70 seats, close to the 50-occupant line.
- **Permits and code (high level).**
  - *Liquor.* Type 48 bar licences are capped at one per 2,000 residents per county, so a new bar has to buy one secondhand, for roughly $150,000–500,000 or more ([broker guide](https://liquorlicensecost.com/guides/california-bar-license-cost/)). Transfers may need a "public convenience or necessity" finding ([BOS example](https://sfbos.org/ftp/uploadedfiles/bdsupvrs/resolutions12/r0083-12.pdf)).
  - *Entertainment.* A Place of Entertainment permit covers DJs and live performance until 2 am. A Limited Live Performance permit covers entertainment as a secondary use ending by 10 or 11 pm. Pre-recorded music indoors without a live performer needs no permit. The process includes a 30-day posted notice and a hearing. 50 or more occupants triggers a Fire Department Place of Assembly permit, and the city may require one security guard per 100 patrons ([sf.gov](https://www.sf.gov/get-place-entertainment-permit-your-business)).
  - *Noise* (Police Code §2909). Commercial premises may produce at most 8 dBA above ambient at the property line, plus at most 8 dBC above the low-frequency ambient for permitted venues. Residential interiors are limited to 45 dBA at night ([Art. 29](https://www.sf.gov/sites/default/files/2024-02/21_CACOSF_2023_Article29RegulationofNoiseGuidelines.pdf)). Bass is the binding limit.
  - *Agent of Change* (2015). A venue that complies with its permits cannot be a noise nuisance, and nearby new homes must disclose it ([Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2015/03/ordinance-to-protect-san-francisco-s-music-venues-makes-headway)).
  - *Occupancy.* Fewer than 50 occupants is Group B; 50 or more is A-2 assembly ([San Diego bulletin, CBC §303](https://www.sandiego.gov/development-services/forms-publications/technical-bulletins/BLDG3-1)).
  - *Plastics.* Acrylic panels count as light-transmitting plastics, which need a CC1 or CC2 classification and an interior-finish rating ([CBC §2606](https://gocodebook.com/library/us/ca/ca-building-code/chapter-26-plastic/section-2606-light-transmitting-plastics)). Some resins, such as 3form Varia, reach Class A ([NYC DOB](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/pdf/65-05-m.pdf)). Raise the area of acrylic with the Department of Building Inspection (DBI) early.
  - *Fees.* "First Year Free" waives first-year permit fees for new businesses. It is authorised to 30 June 2026 and renewal has been proposed, so check whether it is still running ([Treasurer](https://www.sftreasurer.org/file/fyf-report20250pdf/download); [sf.gov](https://www.sf.gov/news-mayor-lurie-announces-legislation-to-help-small-businesses-open-grow)).

## 6. Lighting design ideas with the eight colours

- **A room per colour.** Cruz-Diez's *Chromosaturation* is three connected chambers of red, green and blue light ([MFAH](https://emuseum.mfah.org/objects/60406/visit); [PAMM](https://www.pamm.org/?p=54485)).
- **A spectrum in sequence.** Eliasson's 150 m ring is glazed with glass laminated with up to four coloured foils, and 116 uplights keep it reading after dark ([designboom](https://www.designboom.com/art/olafur-eliasson-your-rainbow-panorama); [LEDinside](https://www.ledinside.com/node/view/24916)).
- **Colour over time.** Turrell's *Twilight Epiphany* runs LED sequences at sunrise and sunset from just four colours: royal blue, green, red and warm white ([Architectural Record](https://www.architecturalrecord.com/articles/7899-twilight-epiphany); [Architect](https://www.architectmagazine.com/technology/lighting/light-and-lightness_o/?o=1)). Flavin's Chiesa Rossa installation moves through green, blue, pink, gold and ultraviolet from night to dawn to day ([Fondazione Prada](https://www.fondazioneprada.org/project/dan-flavin/?lang=en); [press release](https://www.fondazioneprada.org/wp-content/uploads/Fondazione-Prada_Laraaji_PR.pdf)).
- **Tinted light on a wall.** In Barragán's Capuchinas chapel, Goeritz's yellow glass intensifies the orange walls, and a gold-leaf triptych reflects the light ([Barragán Foundation](https://barragan-foundation.org/works/list/capuchin-convent-chapel)).
- **Indirect colour.** In Holl's St Ignatius chapel, back-painted baffles show only reflected colour, paired with lenses in the complementary colour ([Steven Holl](https://www.stevenholl.com/project/st-ignatius-chapel/)).
- **Light that responds to sound.** Toyo Ito's Tower of Winds has about 1,300 lamps and 12 neon rings that react to noise and wind ([Architecture Lab](https://architecturelab.net/tower-of-winds-toyo-ito-and-associates)).
- **The listening-bar range.** At one end, Brilliant Corners' calm globes and Eavesdrop's LED ribs. At the kitsch end, Nightmoves' rainbow light-up dance floor ([MusicTech](https://musictech.com/features/interviews/inside-5-of-nyc-best-hi-fi-bars-suono-all-blues-nowadays-public-records-nightmoves)).

## Design moves for the mockup

1. **A four-corner listening room.** Two Atmosphere pairs stand in the corners on low Smoke plinths, horn mouths near seated ear height. *Refs:* [Brilliant Corners](https://thespaces.com/brilliant-corners-dalston-listening-bar/); [Public Records](https://www.prosoundweb.com/global-audio-adds-custom-sound-to-brooklyns-public-records-venue/); [Gold Line](https://musictech.com/features/hi-fi-bars-los-angeles/).
2. **Speakers in the back-bar.** A Fog pair sits in lit niches among the bottles, playing in mono along the bar. *Ref:* [All Blues](https://musictech.com/features/interviews/inside-5-of-nyc-best-hi-fi-bars-suono-all-blues-nowadays-public-records-nightmoves).
3. **A spectrum bar front.** An 8–9 m front of 20 mm cast-acrylic panels runs Ember → Dust → Acid → Poison → Ice → Haze → Fog → Smoke. Each panel sits over a white liner with a 50–100 mm LED cavity behind, or over an 8–10 mm edge-lit sheet lit from top and bottom. *Refs:* [Eliasson](https://www.designboom.com/art/olafur-eliasson-your-rainbow-panorama); [Buren](https://www.dezeen.com/2016/05/12/daniel-buren-colours-sails-frank-gehry-fondation-louis-vuitton-site-specific-installation-paris/); [DG Floors](https://dgfloors.com/bar-shelf-lighting/); [Plexiglas](https://plexiglas-shop.com/ie/products/plexiglas-led).
4. **A dark glass or stone top** overhangs the lit front. *Ref:* [ACRYLITE chart](https://www.usplastic.com/catalog/files/44251chemicalchart11-2003.pdf).
5. **Edge-lit bottle shelves** in 8–10 mm light-guide acrylic, LEDs in a rear channel. Display shelves are tinted; working shelves are warm white. *Refs:* [ACRYLITE](https://www.acrylite.co/resources/faq/acrylite-led-light-guiding-edge-lit-sheet); [Toast](https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/on-the-line/bar-lighting-ideas).
6. **A floor-to-ceiling record wall** of uneven depth, with the DJ desk set into it. *Refs:* [Bar Shiru](https://studiokda.com/project/bar-shiru/); [HiFi Report](https://www.hifireport.com/can-a-bookshelf-work-as-an-acoustic-diffuser-yes-with-a-few-caveats/).
7. **Tinted slats over absorber** on one wall of every parallel pair. *Refs:* [Space Talk](https://www.dezeen.com/2024/11/10/space-talks-listening-bar-london-ebba-charlotte-taylor/amp/); [flutter rule](https://acoustic.samesound.ru/en/analysis/flutter-echo).
8. **A glowing acoustic ceiling** of translucent micro-perforated membrane, back-lit in one slowly changing colour. *Refs:* [Barrisol A15](https://www.nationalbimlibrary.com/en/barrisol/barrisol-acoustic-light/?lo=en); [Kiyotomo](https://www.mplus.org.hk/en/collection/objects/kiyotomo-sushi-bar-2013306/).
9. **A colour clock.** Fog and Ice at opening, Acid and Dust at sunset, Ember and Haze late, Smoke at last call. *Refs:* [Turrell](https://www.architecturalrecord.com/articles/7899-twilight-epiphany); [Flavin](https://www.fondazioneprada.org/project/dan-flavin/?lang=en).
10. **A one-colour private listening room**, for example an Ember room with Ember speakers. *Refs:* [Chromosaturation](https://www.pamm.org/?p=54485); [Le Fantastique](https://www.7x7.com/hi-fi-bars-san-francisco-bay-area-2659499464.html).
11. **A complementary wash** grazing the wall behind each pair, for example violet behind Acid. *Ref:* [Holl](https://www.stevenholl.com/project/st-ignatius-chapel/).
12. **Thermoformed, micro-perforated Fog screens** dividing the bar from the listening room. *Refs:* [AWT bar](https://www.designboom.com/design/invisible-awtbar-distorts-reality-heated-transparent-acrylic-glass-panels-ichio-matsuzawa-office-11-20-2025/); [MPP partitions](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10208320/).
13. **A massive, isolated DJ booth** clad in Smoke. *Ref:* Nowadays' steel-and-marble booth, whose added mass lets subwoofers sit beside the decks ([MusicTech](https://musictech.com/features/interviews/inside-5-of-nyc-best-hi-fi-bars-suono-all-blues-nowadays-public-records-nightmoves)).
14. **EARF lamps on the tables**, neon in tinted resin, each matching the nearest speaker. *Ref:* [Marcelis *Dawn*](https://www.wallpaper.com/design/sabine-marcelis-way-with-light-and-colour-makes-her-a-fixture-in-store-interiors).
15. **A plain door with one small neon sign**, so the glowing room comes as a surprise. *Ref:* [Bar Martha](https://pen-online.com/travel/le-bar-martha-ladresse-de-quartier-par-excellence/).

## Open questions for the owner

- **Site.** What floor area and ceiling height, and which neighbourhood? Are there homes above or beside it? That decides the bass limits.
- **Capacity.** 49 or fewer (Group B, no Place of Assembly permit), or 50 and over (A-2)?
- **Licence.** A bar on a purchased Type 48 licence, or a food-led restaurant? In the Mission, food is the practical route.
- **Etiquette.** Quiet, kissa-style listening, a social DJ bar like Gold Line, or both at different hours?
- **DJ.** A DJ booth means a Place of Entertainment or Limited Live Performance permit; bartenders playing records may not need one. Where would the DJ sit: in the record wall, behind the bar, or on a stage?
- **Speakers.** How many Atmosphere pairs, in which colours? Fixed, or rotating for sale like a showroom? Does Atmosphere need subwoofers?
- **Colour.** All eight colours equally, one dominant colour, or a room per colour? Static, programmed, or reacting to sound?
- **Fabrication.** Can EARF cast the bar panels and shelves in its own tints? What is the budget for acrylic and lighting?
- **Records.** How big is the collection, in which genres, and is it vinyl only?
- **Hours.** Open past 10 or 11 pm? Any outdoor sound?
- **Product role.** Should the record player and lamp prototypes appear in the mockup?
