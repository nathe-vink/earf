# Listening bar, SF Bay Area: market research

Compiled 2026-10-31 from public web pages only. Every factual claim links to its source. My own reasoning is marked "(inference)". Listing rents come from public listing pages, which are often undated; where a page showed an update date, it is given. Nobody was contacted.

The concept being tested: about 2,500 sq ft, 80 to 99 occupants, cocktails, a selector and a record wall, ticketed listening sessions, and a shop wall selling the maker's own speakers (about $19,000 a pair), lamps ($545 to $895), a record player (about $5,000) and small objects.

## 1. Comparable listening bars

### Bay Area

**Bar Shiru (Uptown Oakland, opened 2019).** It is at [1611 Telegraph Ave, Ste 100](https://www.7x7.com/hi-fi-bars-san-francisco-bay-area-2659499464.html) and caps parties at six ([7x7, March 2023](https://www.7x7.com/hi-fi-bars-san-francisco-bay-area-2659499464.html)).
- **Owners and history:** Daniel Gahr and Shirin Raza left tech jobs to open it in 2019 ([KALW, 2026-05-28](https://www.kalw.org/2026-05-28/listening-bars-challenge-us-to-sit-down-shut-up-and-tune-in)). Raza is general manager and Gahr is creative director ([East Bay Magazine, 2025-04-28](https://www.eastbaymag.com/?p=10618)).
- **Hours:** It opens at 5 pm, Monday to Saturday ([East Bay Magazine, 2025-04-28](https://www.eastbaymag.com/?p=10618)).
- **Programming:** Programming includes High Fidelity Listening Sessions, a Selector Series and Silent Sundays ([East Bay Magazine](https://www.eastbaymag.com/?p=10618)). Silent Sundays ask guests to stay silent for an hour ([KALW](https://www.kalw.org/2026-05-28/listening-bars-challenge-us-to-sit-down-shut-up-and-tune-in)).
- **Ticket price:** A ticketed session on 2026-08-10 ("Records of Resistance") cost $13 per person and sold out ([Tock](https://www.exploretock.com/barshiru/event/610576)).
- **Sound system:** It includes a Line Magnetic 34-IA tube amplifier and a VPI turntable, and upkeep costs "thousands a year" ([SF Standard, 2025-09-03](https://sfstandard.com/2025/09/03/latest-authentic-dining-experience-record-scratch/)). The system was built by Adam Wexler of Resolution Audio Video ([In Sheep's Clothing](https://insheepsclothinghifi.com/listening-bars-we-love-bar-shiru-oakland/)).
- **Economics:** After seven years, Gahr says it has not turned a profit, and he has bought fewer records since about 2022: "There's just not a lot of money" ([KALW, 2026-05-28](https://www.kalw.org/2026-05-28/listening-bars-challenge-us-to-sit-down-shut-up-and-tune-in)). Costs of goods, labour and insurance keep rising ([East Bay Magazine, 2025](https://www.eastbaymag.com/?p=10618)).
- **Not found:** seats, square footage and drink prices.

**Harlan Records (Union Square, SF, 18 Harlan Pl).**
- **Origin and team:** It replaced Bar Fluxus in the Hotel des Arts and is run by Eric Passetti, Will Herrera and Katya Skye ([offbeatsf](https://offbeatsf.com/venue/harlan-records/)).
- **Hours:** It opens at 2 pm daily and 4 pm on Sundays ([7x7, 2023](https://www.7x7.com/hi-fi-bars-san-francisco-bay-area-2659499464.html)).
- **Sound system:** Thorens turntable, McIntosh amplifiers and JBL speakers, vinyl only ([offbeatsf](https://offbeatsf.com/venue/harlan-records/)).
- **Format:** Bartenders curate the music, and silence is not required. It was still operating in May 2026 ([KALW](https://www.kalw.org/2026-05-28/listening-bars-challenge-us-to-sit-down-shut-up-and-tune-in)).

**Le Fantastique (Hayes Valley, 22 Franklin St).**
- **Format:** A seafood, wine and audio bar with a Green Room lounge meant to evoke a listening bar ([7x7](https://www.7x7.com/hi-fi-bars-san-francisco-bay-area-2659499464.html); [Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/san-francisco/reviews/harlan-records)).
- **Sound system cost:** Owner Robbie Wilson said the McIntosh-heavy system cost more than $150,000 ([SF Chronicle, 2022-08-08](https://preview-prod.w.sfchronicle.com/food/wine/article/bay-area-japanese-jazz-bars-17354742.php)). This is the clearest public figure for a Bay Area listening-bar system.
- **Status:** I found no 2025 or 2026 confirmation that it is still open.

**Yokai, now Superprime (East Cut / SoMa, 545 Mission St).**
- **Planned budget:** Owner Marc Zimmerman planned a McIntosh system for $10,000 to $15,000 ([SF Chronicle, 2022](https://preview-prod.w.sfchronicle.com/food/wine/article/bay-area-japanese-jazz-bars-17354742.php)).
- **Conversion:** By 2025 the space was Superprime Steakhouse, "formerly his izakaya Yokai," running Technics decks and 1970s JBL 4341 monitors ([SF Standard, 2025-09-03](https://sfstandard.com/2025/09/03/latest-authentic-dining-experience-record-scratch/)). This is a pivot from listening izakaya to steakhouse, not a closure (inference).

**Bar Crenn (Cow Hollow, 3131 Fillmore).** The casual side of Atelier Crenn, with five lounge tables and a turntable fed from a record shelf ([7x7, 2023](https://www.7x7.com/hi-fi-bars-san-francisco-bay-area-2659499464.html)). KALW calls its clientele wealthy ([KALW, 2026](https://www.kalw.org/2026-05-28/listening-bars-challenge-us-to-sit-down-shut-up-and-tune-in)).

**Side A (Mission, restaurant).** It is co-owned by DJ Caroline Brown and chef Parker Brown. The DJ booth stands in front of about 2,500 albums, with three ceiling-mounted Tub's Audio speakers and acoustic wall panels ([SF Standard, 2025-09-03](https://sfstandard.com/2025/09/03/latest-authentic-dining-experience-record-scratch/)).

**Phonobar (370 Grove St).** A vinyl-only bar with a custom DJ console ([SF Standard, 2022](https://sfstandard.com/2022/07/18/3-bars-in-sf-that-only-play-vinyl-records/)). It "turns records into a dance party" ([SF Standard, 2025](https://sfstandard.com/2025/09/03/latest-authentic-dining-experience-record-scratch/)). I found no current figures.

**Moongate (Chinatown).** A "lunar-inspired listening lounge" upstairs from Mister Jiu's ([SF Standard, 2025](https://sfstandard.com/2025/09/03/latest-authentic-dining-experience-record-scratch/)).

### Elsewhere in the US

**Public Records (Gowanus, Brooklyn, opened April 2019).**
- **Space:** About 6,000 sq ft in a former ASPCA building. The Sound Room holds up to 200 people ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Records_(venue))).
- **Revenue streams:** A café that sells records and zines, a vegan restaurant (the Atrium), an upstairs cocktail lounge (opened December 2022), an indoor-outdoor space (the Nursery), and a consultancy launched in 2024 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Records_(venue))).
- **Sound system:** Built by Devon Turnbull (OJAS) and Jason Ojeda, with a custom Isonoe rotary mixer ([DICE](https://dice.fm/venue/public-records-w2qg)). No cost has been published.
- **Funding:** One aggregator lists $300K raised, which is unverified ([CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/public-records)).
- **Expansion:** The team is building a separate 1,000-capacity venue at Bush Terminal, due in late 2026 ([Commercial Observer, 2025-12](https://commercialobserver.com/2025/12/public-records-bringing-new-live-events-venue-brooklyns-sunset-park/)).
- **Takeaway:** Its economics rest on many revenue lines (café by day, food, ticketed shows, two bars), not on the listening room alone (inference).

**Eavesdrop (Greenpoint, Brooklyn, opened March 2022).**
- **Space:** About 1,000 sq ft with 36 seats, all seated ([InsideHook, 2022-04-15](https://www.insidehook.com/food-new-york/eavesdrop-brooklyn-bar)). That is a 25-seat listening room plus 15 to 20 bar seats ([BK Mag, 2025-09](https://www.bkmag.com/2025/09/09/best-hi-fi-listening-bars-brooklyn/)).
- **Hours and booking:** Open daily 5 pm to midnight ([InsideHook](https://www.insidehook.com/food-new-york/eavesdrop-brooklyn-bar)). Back-room reservations come in two slots, 5 to 8 pm and 8 pm onward. Bartenders run the music until 8 pm, then guest DJs take over ([Copper/PS Audio, 2024-08-30](https://psaudio.com/blogs/copper/fidelity-visits-eavesdrop-the-brooklyn-jazz-kissa)).
- **Sound system:** Danley SH60 mains with Seaton Submersive subwoofers ([RA, 2022-02-18](https://ra.co/news/76850)), driven by Bryston amplifiers ([Copper](https://psaudio.com/blogs/copper/fidelity-visits-eavesdrop-the-brooklyn-jazz-kissa)).
- **Not found:** prices and financing.
- **Takeaway:** Reserved, time-slotted seats work as the cover charge (inference).

**Gold Line (Highland Park, Los Angeles, opened about November 2018).**
- **Ownership:** A partnership of Stones Throw founder Peanut Butter Wolf, label manager Jason McGuire and restaurateur Tyler Bell, located under the label's offices ([RA](https://ra.co/news/42597)).
- **Format:** About 7,500 records on loan from the founder's collection, open 5 pm to late, priced "1 of 4" ([Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/bars/gold-line)).
- **Sibling bar:** The team later opened a second bar, Palomino, across the street ([Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/reviews/palomino-bar)).
- **Status:** No closure was found.
- **Takeaway:** This is the closest precedent for a record label (a brand) running a bar (inference).

**Other data points.**
- ESP HiFi (Denver) runs vintage Klipschorns and Cornwalls ([Westword](https://www.westword.com/music/esp-hifi-a-japanese-style-listening-bar-and-cafe-is-coming-to-denver-12105712/)). Its team opened XO in Kansas City in 2024 with 35 seats plus a 25-seat patio ([Kansas City Magazine](https://kansascitymag.com/opening-this-weekend-a-japanese-inspired-hifi-cocktail-lounge/)).
- The Pretender (Noblesville, IN, 2026) is 2,000 sq ft for about 50 guests, with "more than $60,000" of audio gear ([Hoodline, 2026-07-31](https://hoodline.com/2026/07/noblesville-s-new-pretender-bar-puts-60-000-hi-fi-on-display/)).

### Tokyo kissa model

- **Scale over time:** Jazz kissa peaked at about 600 nationwide in the mid-1970s ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz_kissa)). Estimates for today run from about 100 in Tokyo to about 500 across Japan ([Japan Nakama](https://www.japannakama.co.uk/creativity/photography/vanishing-world-of-japanese-jazz-culture/)).
- **Why they declined:** Cheaper home stereos ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz_kissa)), plus high rents, ageing owners and ageing buildings ([Japan Nakama](https://www.japannakama.co.uk/creativity/photography/vanishing-world-of-japanese-jazz-culture/)).
- **JBS (Shibuya):** About 10,000 records. The owner, Kobayashi, runs it alone ([Vinyl Factory](https://thevinylfactory.com/features/jbs-jazz-bar-a-vinyl-sanctuary-in-the-midst-of-tokyo/)), and it charges no cover, which is rare in Tokyo ([Tablejourney](https://tablejourney.com/japan/tokyo/nightlife/jbs-shibuya/)).
- **Bar Martha (Ebisu):** A wall of about 6,000 records played on Tannoy GRFs, open 19:00 to 02:00. Sources disagree on the cover: one guide gives a ¥800 cover, while Tablejourney gives drinks from ¥800 and a cover only on event nights ([Tablejourney](https://tablejourney.com/japan/tokyo/nightlife/listening-bars/)).
- **Eigakan:** I found no public figures for it.
- **The model:** One-person staffing, small rooms, a modest cover or one-drink minimum, and owner-occupied or low-rent premises. It works only at a cost base far below a 2,500 sq ft SF lease (inference).

## 2. Bar economics benchmarks

**Pour cost (liquor cost).**
- The typical range is 18 to 24%, and BevSpot's median bar is "just above 20%" ([BevSpot](https://bevspot.com/blog/whats-average-pour-cost-bar-industry/)).
- A working range for a full-service bar is 20 to 24%. Wine bars run 25 to 35% ([Vanta Insights](https://vantainsights.com/insights/bar-pour-cost-percentage)).
- Backbar argues the 18 to 22% rule of thumb is misleading and that targets should follow the bar's own mix ([Backbar](https://academy.getbackbar.com/2026-pour-cost-targets-bar-profitability)).

**Gross and net margin.**
- Average bar gross margin is about 75 to 80% ([Toast](https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/on-the-line/bar-profit-margin)).
- Bar net margins are commonly quoted at 10 to 15% ([7shifts](https://www.7shifts.com/blog/bar-profit-margin); [Sculpture Hospitality](https://www.sculpturehospitality.com/blog/is-owning-a-bar-profitable)), and 7 to 10% for bars that serve food ([Small Business Trends](https://smallbiztrends.com/bar-profit-margin/)).
- The audited-style figure is lower: the National Restaurant Association's median pretax income for full-service restaurants was **2.8% of sales in 2024** ([NRA, 2025-08-20](https://restaurant.org/research-and-media/media/press-releases/new-resource-from-national-restaurant-association-provides-insights-into-operational-realities/)).

**Labour.**
- A 36.5% full-service median is widely attributed to the same NRA abstract, but I could not see it on an NRA page ([NRA analysis page](https://restaurant.org/research-and-media/research/restaurant-economic-insights/analysis-commentary/elevated-labor-costs-had-a-significant-impact-on-restaurant-profitability-in-2024/)).
- General restaurant benchmarks put labour at 25 to 35% ([Vanta Insights](https://vantainsights.com/insights/bar-profit-margins)).
- Prime cost (labour plus cost of goods) targets are 55 to 65% ([7shifts](https://www.7shifts.com/blog/opening-bar-costs/)).

**Occupancy (rent and related costs as a share of sales).** The NRA's 2024 medians are 5.7% for full-service restaurants and 6.0% for urban full-service ([NRA, 2025-09-03](https://restaurant.org/research-and-media/research/restaurant-economic-insights/analysis-commentary/restaurant-occupancy-costs-were-more-than-5-of-sales-in-2024/)). Consultant rules of thumb go up to 8 to 10% ([Restaurant Business](https://restaurantbusinessonline.com/advice-guy/how-figure-out-prime-cost-occupancy-percentages)).

**Sales per square foot.**
- Under $150 per sq ft leaves little chance of profit, and $250 to $325 corresponds to roughly 5 to 10% profit ([Bloom Intelligence PDF](https://info.bloomintelligence.com/hubfs/Miscellaneous%20Downloads/Restaurant%20Benchmarks.pdf)).
- Average performers run $200 to $325 and high performers $400 to $600 ([Metrobi](https://metrobi.com/blog/average-restaurant-revenue-how-do-you-compare/)).
- A Toast benchmark gives about $27 per seat, measured per seat-hour, not per year ([Toast](https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/on-the-line/7-critical-restaurant-benchmarks)).
- No bar-specific per-seat figure was found.

**SF prices and costs.**
- **Cocktails:** Square's SF menu data averages $14 for a contemporary cocktail, with a range of $9 to $24 (undated) ([Square](https://squareup.com/us/en/menu-pricing/bar-prices-in-san-francisco)). The SF Chronicle reports drinks around $20 even without alcohol ([SF Chronicle](https://preview-prod.w.sfchronicle.com/food/wine/article/mocktail-drink-price-bar-20796528.php)).
- **Wine:** One SF restaurant's average glass of white rose from about $16 to over $18 ([SF Standard, 2025-10-28](https://sfstandard.com/2025/10/28/wine-prices-san-francisco/)).
- **Tips:** Full-service tips averaged 19.1 to 19.2% in Q2 and Q3 2025 ([Toast, 2025](https://pos.toasttab.com/news/2025-summer-restaurant-trends)).
- **Minimum wage:** SF's minimum is **$19.61/h from 2026-07-01**, up from $19.18 ([SF OLSE PDF](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/2026_Historical_San_Francisco_Minimum_Wage_Rates_M9ynRNo.pdf); [McGuireWoods](https://www.mcguirewoods.com/client-resources/alerts/2026/6/san-francisco-hourly-minimum-wage-increases-to-19-61-on-july-1-how-employers-should-prepare/)).
- **Health care mandate:** SF's employer health spending rates (HCSO) for 2026 are $2.74/h for employers with 20 to 99 staff and $4.11/h for 100 or more. The 2027 rates are $2.99 and $4.49 ([Risk Strategies](https://www.risk-strategies.com/blog/san-francisco-health-care-security-ordinance-rates-for-2026); [HUB, 2026-09](https://www.hubinternational.com/en-us/hub-resources/employee-benefits-compliance-bulletins/2026/09/san-francisco-hcso-contribution-rates-for-2027/)). These add about 14 to 21% to an hourly wage at minimum pay (inference).

**Ticketed listening.**
- Bar Shiru charges $13 ([Tock](https://www.exploretock.com/barshiru/event/610576)).
- London candlelit "Playback" album sessions charge £10 ([eventslist, 2026](https://eventslist.co.uk/london/events/playback-the-1975-candlelit-sessions)).
- Tokyo covers run about ¥800 where charged ([Tablejourney](https://tablejourney.com/japan/tokyo/nightlife/listening-bars/)).
- At these prices tickets cover programming, not rent: 40 seats × $15 × 2 sessions × 4 nights ≈ $4,800 a week (inference).

## 3. Build-out costs

**Construction, hard costs per sq ft, excluding equipment, furniture and soft costs.** Terrapin's guide was updated July 2026 ([Terrapin](http://terrapincg.com/news/high-end-restaurant-construction-cost-per-square-foot)).
- Nationally: second-generation space $125 to $250, warm shell $175 to $325, cold shell $250 to $450.
- Pacific Coast build-outs (SF, LA, Seattle, Portland): $375 to $650.
- SF and Bay Area ground-up: $650 to $950.
- California premium: 12 to 18% over other Western states.
- Bar-forward concepts: $350 to $575 ground-up. Millwork, AV, lighting and glycol lines drive the cost.

Other references:
- Cushman & Wakefield's 2025 retail fit-out survey puts in-line retail at about $211 per sq ft in Northern California ([via GrowthFactor](https://www.growthfactor.ai/resources/blog/retail-build-out-costs)).
- A second-generation space costs 30 to 50% less to build out than a cold shell ([via GrowthFactor](https://www.growthfactor.ai/resources/blog/retail-build-out-costs)).

**For 2,500 sq ft (inference):**
- Second-generation bar: about $0.45M to $0.9M, using $180 to $350 per sq ft with a Bay Area uplift.
- Cold shell: about $0.95M to $1.6M, using $375 to $650 per sq ft.
- Both before equipment, sound, licence and pre-opening costs.

**Bar equipment (fragmentary, undated vendor prices).**
- Underbar well cooler $1,099, vertical back-bar cooler $1,254, three-bowl bar sink $599 ([TRUiC](https://truicbusinessideas.com/bar/purchasing)).
- Undercounter glasswasher A$2,000 to A$8,000 (2026) ([Hospitality Hub](https://www.hospitalityhub.com.au/commercial-undercounter-glasswasher-costs-the-numbers-that-change-the-business-case-2026/f/27594)).
- No sourced ice-machine price was found.
- A commercial bar package (bar die, underbar, coolers, ice, glasswasher) would plausibly cost $40k to $120k (inference). Get dealer quotes.

**Acoustic treatment.**
- Commercial wall panels cost about $4 to $8 per sq ft for material ([Live Sound, 2023](https://customdigital.livesoundint.com/october-2023/page-35)).
- Premium products cost about $30 to $75 per sq ft installed ([LatestCost](https://latestcost.com/baux-acoustic-panels-price-installation-costs/)).
- No priced restaurant case study was found.
- Isolation to neighbours (room-in-room construction, isolated ceilings) is a construction item, not panels, and is the larger cost (inference).

**Liquor licence (San Francisco).**
- **Type 47/48 market price:** A broker said a full licence that sold for about $250,000 in 2022 now goes for about $100,000, with low sales at $70,000 to $85,000 ([NBC Bay Area, 2026-05-20](https://www.nbcbayarea.com/news/local/liquor-license-prices-san-francisco/4087673/)).
- **New cheaper licences:** Two non-transferable licence types are offered with a $19,840 fee: 10 Hospitality Zone licences downtown and 5 Type 87 licences in outer neighbourhoods. Applications ran 2026-09-14 to 2026-09-25 ([sf.gov](https://www.sf.gov/es/neighborhood-restricted-restaurant-liquor-licenses)). Both are restaurant licences only, and none of the target neighbourhoods is a Type 87 area.

## 4. Rents, vacancy and concessions

**Citywide San Francisco.**
- **Kidder Mathews (CoStar data), Q3 2026:** vacancy 6.6%, average asking rent $36.56 per sq ft per year, up 9.66% year on year ([Kidder Mathews PDF](https://kidder.com/wp-content/uploads/market_report/retail-market-research-san-francisco-2026-3q.pdf)). Its transactions table includes San Mateo County, so this is a city-plus-Peninsula average (inference).
- **Matthews (CoStar), Q2 2026:** asking rent $44.22, vacancy 5.3% ([Matthews](https://www.matthews.com/insights/san-francisco-retail-q2-2026)).
- **MMCG, Q3 2025:** ground-floor asking rents about $42 to $45 triple net (tenant pays taxes, insurance and maintenance on top); West of Van Ness vacancy about 2.1%; Yerba Buena 47.8% ([MMCG](https://www.mmcginvest.com/post/san-francisco-retail-market-conditions-q3-2025-overview)).
- **SF Office of Small Business, September 2026:** citywide retail vacancy 5.8% in Q2 2026 against 20.3% in Union Square ([SF OSB, Commercial Leasing Trends](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/SF_CRE_Leasing_Trends.pdf)).

**Concessions.**
- SF's Office of Small Business says no report tracks deal terms or TI packages for small tenants. Personal guarantees appear in "nearly every lease" since COVID, and it has seen one-way renewal options priced at the landlord's fair market rent ([SF OSB](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/SF_CRE_Leasing_Trends.pdf)).
- The only sourced restaurant TI allowance range is national: $40 to $100 per sq ft where a landlord wants a food anchor. Core retail allowances fell 8 to 15% in H1 2026 ([Terrapin](https://terrapincg.com/news/tenant-improvement-buildout-costs-retail)).
- SF office TI runs about $20 per sq ft for large tenants, which is not retail ([Commercial Observer, 2025-04](https://commercialobserver.com/2025/04/san-francisco-office-market-concessions-leasing/)).
- I found no sourced SF retail free-rent figure. Three to six months is a plausible ask for a heavy build-out (inference only).

**Oakland and the East Bay.** Kidder's Q2 2026 East Bay retail figures give vacancy of 5.7% (up 40 basis points) and asking rent of "$2.38/SF," down 2.32% ([Kidder Mathews](https://kidder.com/market-reports/east-bay-retail-market-report/)). The unit reads as monthly, about $28.56 a year (inference). No submarket rents were found for Uptown, Temescal or Jack London.

## 5. Financing

**SBA 7(a).**
- **Size and guarantee:** Up to $5M. SBA guarantees 85% of loans up to $150k and 75% above that ([SBA](https://www.sba.gov/partners/lenders/7a-loan-program/terms-conditions-eligibility)).
- **Rate caps:** For variable rates, the base rate plus 6.5% for loans up to $50k, 6.0% for $50k to $250k, 4.5% for $250k to $350k, and 3.0% above $350k ([SBA](https://www.sba.gov/partners/lenders/7a-loan-program/terms-conditions-eligibility)).
- **Prime rate:** The WSJ prime rate was 7.00% the week of 2026-10-16 ([Bankrate](https://www.bankrate.com/rates/interest-rates/wall-street-prime-rate/)). So a loan over $350k is capped at about 10.0%, and one from $50k to $250k at 13.0% ([Bankrate SBA rates](https://www.bankrate.com/loans/small-business/sba-loan-rates/)).
- **Terms:** Working capital generally runs 10 years or less. Equipment can run longer than 10 years where its useful life exceeds 10 years, plus up to 12 months for installation, and the same installation allowance applies to leasehold improvements. Real estate runs up to 25 years ([SBA](https://www.sba.gov/partners/lenders/7a-loan-program/terms-conditions-eligibility)).
- **Fees for FY2027** (loans approved 2026-10-01 to 2027-09-30, SBA Information Notice 5000-881797): the upfront fee on the guaranteed portion is 2% up to $150k, 3% from $150k to $700k, and 3.5% to 3.75% above $700k. The annual service fee is 0.55% ([ClearValue Lending](https://clearvaluelending.com/blog/sba-7a-fy2027-fees-upfront-guaranty-fee-explained)). Check the notice itself.
- **Equity injection:** At least 10% of project cost for startups (businesses one year old or less), reinstated by SOP 50 10 8 from 2025-06-01. A seller note can count for at most half of it, and only on full standby ([Starfield & Smith, 2025-05](https://starfieldsmith.com/2025/05/best-practices-a-review-of-equity-injection-requirements-under-sop-50-10-8/)). Lenders set their own requirements above that floor ([sba7a.loans](https://www.sba7a.loans/sba-7a-down-payment-calculator/)), and some see bars as high-risk ([sba7a.loans](https://www.sba7a.loans/sba-7a-loans-small-business-blog/sba-7a-loans-for-bars-and-clubs/)).
- **Personal guarantees:** Required from every owner of 20% or more ([sba7a.loans](https://www.sba7a.loans/sba-7a-loans-small-business-blog/sba-7a-loans-for-bars-and-clubs/)).
- **Practical equity:** 20 to 30% equity is a realistic expectation for a startup bar (inference). I found no source for that range.

**SBA 504.** The borrower puts in at least 10%, the CDC (Certified Development Company, the SBA's partner lender) up to 40% and a bank 50%, on 10-year terms for equipment and 25 for real estate ([SBA](https://www.sba.gov/partners/lenders/7a-loan-program/terms-conditions-eligibility)). It is relevant only if the bar buys its building (inference).

**Liquor licence financing.**
- One lender guide says SBA proceeds usually fund a licence only inside the purchase of a business as a going concern ([sba7a.loans](https://www.sba7a.loans/sba-7a-loans-small-business-blog/liquor-store-loans/)).
- California does not allow a lien on a liquor licence, so lenders need other collateral ([Starfield & Smith, 2021](https://starfieldsmith.com/2021/07/best-practices-perfecting-liquor-license-liens/amp)).
- With SF Type 48 prices down to about $100k ([NBC Bay Area, 2026](https://www.nbcbayarea.com/news/local/liquor-license-prices-san-francisco/4087673/)), the licence is more likely to come out of equity than debt (inference).

**Investor equity.** Restaurant deals usually return capital first, then split profits.
- One example sends 90% of profits to investors until they recover their investment plus 25%, then reverts to the ownership split ([LegalClarity](https://legalclarity.org/how-do-restaurant-investors-get-paid-equity-and-debt/)).
- After that payback, operators typically take 60 to 80% of distributions ([Haute Lawyer](https://www.hauteliving.com/hautelawyer/legal-questions/hospitality/investing-in-restaurants-nightlife-legal-guide)).
- These are illustrative, not market data.

**Community rounds and revenue-based financing.**
- **Rikki's (Castro, SF):** A women's sports bar that raised $425K on Wefunder (October 2024 to February 2025) as a revenue share: 5% of revenue until investors get 1.5× back, with a $100 minimum ([KingsCrowd](https://kingscrowd.com/rikkis-bar-sf-on-wefunder-2024/)). It had more than 80 investors and opened 2025-06-11 ([Mission Local](https://missionlocal.org/2025/06/fans-whoop-and-holler-for-rikkis-san-franciscos-first-womens-sports-bar/); [Axios](https://www.axios.com/local/san-francisco/2025/06/12/san-francisco-s-first-women-s-sports-bar-opens-in-the-castro)). This is the closest local precedent.
- **Umbrella Dry Bar:** A 2× revenue-share offer that did not fund ([KingsCrowd](https://kingscrowd.com/umbrella-dry-bar-on-wefunder-2023/)).
- **NextSeed brewery:** An older revenue-share offer at 1.45× and 7.5% of revenue ([Republic](https://republic.com/insights/learn/revenue-sharing-notes-investing-in-debt-instruments)).
- **Mainvest:** The main restaurant-focused crowdfunding platform shut down in June 2024 after its banking partner Synapse failed, freezing funds. It had raised $30M for more than 450 businesses ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2024/05/225317-funding-portal-mainvest-to-cease-crowdfunding-operations/); [Lawyers for Civil Rights](https://lawyersforcivilrights.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Mainvest-Collapse-Letter-to-FINRA-and-FDIC-1.pdf)). It is no longer an option.
- **JAZU (London listening bar):** Raised £8,500 of a £12,000 Kickstarter goal by June 2024 ([DJ Mag](https://djmag.com/news/london-listening-bar-jazu-crowdfunding-open-permanent-space)). Reward-based crowdfunding raises small sums.

- **Measured halo effect:** ICSC found a new store lifts trade-area online sales by 6.9%, and by 13.9% for emerging direct-to-consumer brands, while a closure cuts them by 11.5% ([Retail TouchPoints](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/topics/omnichannel-alignment/icsc-research-new-store-openings-boost-online-sales-6-9/2)). An earlier ICSC study found a 37% lift in web traffic ([ICSC](https://www.icsc.com/uploads/about/ICSC_Halo_Effect_III_Report.pdf)).
- **Shop wall arithmetic (inference):** At $19,000 a pair, one pair a month is about $228K a year in gross sales, which is about the bar's entire rent. Retail margin on own-brand goods, not drink sales, may decide whether the venue pays. No public precedent gives the conversion rate.
