# San Francisco rules for a listening bar: licences, permits, codes

> **Correction (round 42, checked against [Planning Code sec. 102](https://gocodebook.com/library/us/ca/san-francisco-planning/article-1-general-zoning-provisions/sec.-102.-definitions)):** Nighttime Entertainment includes "Restaurants and Bars which present such activities", meaning entertainment that needs a Place of Entertainment permit. A bar with a POE selector is therefore Nighttime Entertainment where the district controls differ, such as Hayes-Gough, where it is a Conditional Use. With a Limited Live Performance permit it stays a Bar. RULES.md uses the corrected reading.

Research note, 2026-10-31. Public web sources only. The bar in question has about 2,500 sq ft, 80 to 99 occupants, a DJ or selector playing vinyl, cocktails and spirits, light snacks, and retail of design objects. Every claim is linked to its source. My own reasoning is marked **(inference)**. Fees and prices are dated. Code text read from third-party mirrors (gocodebook, corada) should be checked against the adopted code before anyone files.

## 1. California ABC licences

### What each licence allows

| Type | Allows | Food required? | Minors? | Quota-capped? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 41 On-sale beer and wine, eating place | Beer and wine on premises | Yes: run as a bona fide eating place ([ABC FAQ](https://www.abc.ca.gov/licensing/frequently-asked-questions)) | Allowed, as in any restaurant ([Permit Place](https://permitplace.com/?p=1175)) | No. It is a "non-general" licence at a flat $1,135 ([ABC fees](https://www.abc.ca.gov/licensing/license-fees/application-fee-schedules)) |
| 42 On-sale beer and wine, public premises | Beer and wine on premises | No ([Permit Place](https://permitplace.com/?p=1175)) | "Not allowed to enter and remain" ([ABC FAQ](https://www.abc.ca.gov/licensing/frequently-asked-questions)) | No (non-general, $1,135) |
| 47 On-sale general, eating place | Beer, wine and spirits on premises | Yes: a bona fide eating place with "suitable kitchen facilities" and "an assortment of foods commonly ordered at various hours of the day" ([ABC FAQ](https://www.abc.ca.gov/licensing/frequently-asked-questions)) | Allowed ([Permit Place](https://permitplace.com/?p=1175); ABC's FAQ does not say) | Yes. On-sale general licences are capped at 1 per 2,000 county residents ([ABC FAQ](https://www.abc.ca.gov/licensing/frequently-asked-questions)) |
| 48 On-sale general, public premises | Beer, wine and spirits on premises | No ([Permit Place](https://permitplace.com/?p=1175)) | "Not allowed to enter and remain"; "No person under 21 years of age is allowed on public premises" ([ABC FAQ](https://www.abc.ca.gov/licensing/frequently-asked-questions)) | Yes (same quota) |

### Original ABC fees (effective 2026-01-01)

- Original licence, Type 47 or 48: **$19,840**, and only for winners of the priority drawing ([ABC](https://www.abc.ca.gov/licensing/license-fees/application-fee-schedules)).
- Original licence, Type 41 or 42: **$1,135** plus the annual fee ([ABC](https://www.abc.ca.gov/licensing/license-fees/application-fee-schedules)).
- Person-to-person transfer of a general licence: **$1,565**. Premises-to-premises transfer: $975. Exchanging a 47 for a 48: **$125** ([ABC](https://www.abc.ca.gov/licensing/license-fees/application-fee-schedules)).
- Annual fee for a 47 or 48 in a city over 40,000 people: **$1,595** on ABC's upcoming schedule. ABC says fees rise 3.31% on 2027-01-01 ([ABC annual schedule](https://www.abc.ca.gov/licensing/license-fees/upcoming-license-fees/annual-fee-schedule/); [ABC upcoming fees](https://www.abc.ca.gov/licensing/license-fees/upcoming-license-fees/)). That puts the 2026 fee near $1,545 **(inference)**. Type 41 is $580 a year and Type 42 is $730.

### Transfer-market prices for a 47 or 48 in San Francisco County

- **May 2026: about $100,000 net to the seller**, with some sales at $70,000 to $85,000, according to broker Cameron DeRuosi. He said the price was about $250,000 in 2022 ([NBC Bay Area, 2026-05-20](https://www.nbcbayarea.com/news/local/liquor-license-prices-san-francisco/4087673/)).
- **May 2025: "low six figures," about 40% below the pre-pandemic high**, according to broker Patrick Totah (Restaurant Realty). In the same article, operators reported buying full licences for $150,000 (a Type 47) and $120,000, and quoted "about $100,000 including broker fees." Before the pandemic, prices were $250,000 or more ([SF Standard, 2025-05-17](https://sfstandard.com/2025/05/17/sf-liquor-license-bar-restaurant-cheap/)).
- One listing for an SF County 47/48 asked $119,000 net, plus a 10% finder's fee and a $2,450 escrow fee. The listing date is not shown ([BizQuest](https://images.bizquest.com/asset-sales/san-francisco-county-47-48-liquor-license-for-sale/BW2266163)).
- **Working range: $70,000 to $150,000, with a central case of about $100,000 to $120,000 all-in before ABC fees (inference).** Brokers do not price 47s and 48s separately, and the exchange between them costs $125 (inference).

### Is there a priority list or quota drawing for SF?

- ABC's priority drawings run each September in counties that have new general licences under the quota. San Francisco does **not** appear in the 2026 list of standard priority announcements. It appears only under Type 87 and special-legislation announcements ([ABC drawings page](https://www.abc.ca.gov/licensing/priority-registration-drawings)). So SF is over quota, and a new bar has to buy a 47 or 48 on the transfer market **(inference)**.
- **Type 87 (neighbourhood-restricted):** five new licences were offered in September 2026 at a $19,840 fee. They are for bona fide eating places only, cannot be transferred, and are limited to census tracts in Bayview, Excelsior, Ocean Ave, the Sunset (Noriega/Judah and Taraval), Portola and Visitacion Valley. Applications ran 2026-09-14 to 09-25 ([ABC, 2026-08-17](https://www.abc.ca.gov/?p=46768); [sf.gov](https://www.sf.gov/es/neighborhood-restricted-restaurant-liquor-licenses)).
- **Hospitality-zone licences (SB 395, 2025):** ten new on-sale general licences were offered for restaurants in the downtown hospitality zone in September 2026. They cannot be transferred, cannot be sold above the fee, and expire as a programme after 2033-07-01 ([sf.gov](https://www.sf.gov/es/neighborhood-restricted-restaurant-liquor-licenses); [Digital Democracy](https://calmatters.digitaldemocracy.org/bills/ca_202520260sb395)). Neither programme fits a no-food bar. A downtown Type-47-style operation could apply in a 2027 window, if ABC repeats it **(inference)**.

### ABC process and timeline

1. File the transfer application, signed by both buyer and seller ([ABC FAQ](https://www.abc.ca.gov/licensing/frequently-asked-questions)). When money changes hands, the full price must sit in escrow, which closes only after ABC approves in writing ([BizBen broker blog](https://www.bizben.com/blog/liquor-license-transfer-process-tips-from-broker-specialist)).
2. **30-day posting** of form ABC-207 at the premises, plus mailed notice to residents and owners within **500 ft**. The protest window runs 30 days from the later of the posting date and the mailing date ([ABC-510](https://www.abc.ca.gov/form/abc-510)). ABC cannot deny a licence on a protest alone ([ABC-510](https://www.abc.ca.gov/form/abc-510)).
3. **Undue concentration (B&P 23958.4(a))** exists if the police reporting district has 20% more reported crime than the average, or if the census tract's ratio of on-sale licences to population exceeds the county's ratio ([B&P 23958.4](https://california.public.law/codes/business_and_professions_code_section_23958.4)).
4. **Who makes the public convenience or necessity (PCN) finding:** for bona fide eating-place licences (41 and 47), **ABC** decides. For other licences, including **48 and 42**, the **local governing body** decides, within 90 days of a completed application. If it misses the 90 days, ABC may still issue ([B&P 23958.4(b)](https://california.public.law/codes/business_and_professions_code_section_23958.4)). In SF, the request is a letter to the Board of Supervisors. It is heard in the Public Safety and Neighborhood Services Committee and adopted by resolution. SFPD's ABC liaison unit may recommend conditions ([BOS fact sheet](https://sfbos.archive.sf.gov/sites/default/files/PC_or_N_Request_Fact_Sheet.pdf)). Example: a Type 48 transfer to Reggie and Maude's at 456-460 Larkin was found to serve PCN, prepared in committee 2026-07-23. Its conditions were no noise audible beyond the premises except as allowed by an Entertainment Commission permit, loitering control and litter control ([Res. 459-26](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/r0459-26.pdf)).
5. **Timeline:** ABC says "most investigations take approximately 55-65 days." By law a licence cannot issue in under 30 days, and longer waits are common. A transferee may operate on a **120-day temporary permit** only if the premises was operating in the 30 days before applying ([ABC FAQ](https://www.abc.ca.gov/licensing/frequently-asked-questions)). A broker puts the realistic figure at up to 3 months ([BizBen](https://www.bizben.com/blog/liquor-license-transfer-process-tips-from-broker-specialist)). With a Board PCN, plan on **3 to 5 months** from filing to issuance (inference).

### Type 47 versus 48 for a bar that serves only snacks

- A 47 requires regular meal service, a suitable kitchen and food available through the day ([ABC FAQ](https://www.abc.ca.gov/licensing/frequently-asked-questions)). Snacks do not meet that. ABC disciplines licensees who run a 47 as a bar (inference). **For a snacks-only bar, the correct licence is the 48.**
- What the 48 costs the bar: no one under 21 may enter, including retail customers ([ABC FAQ](https://www.abc.ca.gov/licensing/frequently-asked-questions)). It is a "Bar" use for Planning, which needs Conditional Use authorization in many districts (section 3). PCN goes to the Board of Supervisors, not ABC ([B&P 23958.4](https://california.public.law/codes/business_and_professions_code_section_23958.4)).
- What the 47 gives: minors allowed, so all-ages retail and daytime events are possible. It counts as "Restaurant" for Planning, which is principally permitted in more districts than Bar (section 3). PCN is decided by ABC. The cost is a real kitchen, a Category 3 health plan check, ventilation and meal service (inference). The purchase price on the market is the same (inference). Converting later from 47 to 48 costs $125 ([ABC](https://www.abc.ca.gov/licensing/license-fees/application-fee-schedules)).

## 2. Entertainment Commission

### What needs a permit

- Police Code 1060 counts **"recorded music presented by a live DJ on the premises"** as entertainment ([sf.gov, What is entertainment](https://sf.gov/information/what-entertainment); [Ord. 100-13](https://sfbos.org/ftp/uploadedfiles/bdsupvrs/ordinances13/o0100-13.pdf)).
- **Indoor pre-recorded music with no live performer** (a playlist or jukebox) needs no permit ([sf.gov LLP page](https://sf.gov/get-limited-live-performance-permit); [sf.gov POE page](https://www.sf.gov/get-place-entertainment-permit-your-business)).
- A bartender who chooses and changes records as part of the show is probably a "live DJ." Records spun as background by bar staff are a grey area that the Commission should rule on in the intake meeting **(inference)**.

### The two permits

| | Limited Live Performance (LLP) | Place of Entertainment (POE) |
|---|---|---|
| Meant for | Entertainment that is secondary to the business, for example a restaurant with music ([sf.gov](https://sf.gov/get-limited-live-performance-permit)) | Entertainment that is a primary use, or ongoing entertainment up to 2 am ([sf.gov](https://www.sf.gov/get-place-entertainment-permit-your-business)) |
| Limits | Must end by **10 or 11 pm** depending on zoning. Performance area **at most 200 sq ft** ([sf.gov](https://sf.gov/get-limited-live-performance-permit)) | Hours set by the permit and the zoning |
| Application fee, FY 2026-27 | **$612** (amendment $205) ([sf.gov fees](https://www.sf.gov/check-your-entertainment-permit-fees)) | **$2,708** (major amendment $1,305) ([sf.gov fees](https://www.sf.gov/check-your-entertainment-permit-fees)). Fees rise 2 to 3% each July |
| Process | Email staff, apply, intake meeting, neighbour outreach, **30-day yellow notice posted**, other departments review, public hearing (the Commission meets the 1st and 3rd Tuesday), then the permit issues once required inspections pass ([sf.gov](https://sf.gov/get-limited-live-performance-permit)) | Same steps; the packet also goes to Planning and Police ([sf.gov](https://www.sf.gov/get-place-entertainment-permit-your-business)) |

- **Timeline example:** a 2026 POE applicant (Golden Eye Social, EC-1906) mailed notice to occupants within 150 ft on 2025-12-02, and its hearing was set for 2026-02-03. The staff file lists an interior limit of **84 dBA / 94 dBC** ([EC-1906 packet](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/EC-1906_The_Golden_Eye_Social_POE_application_packet_2xaEV9l.pdf)). Plan on **2 to 3 months** from a complete application (inference).
- **Sound test:** Commission sound inspectors test the system and set a limit for the permit, for example 100 dBC at Mezzanine after a 112 dBC peak ([EC minutes, 2016](https://www.sfgov.org/entertainment/node/371)).
- **Police Code Article 29:** on commercial property, sound may not exceed **ambient + 8 dBA** at any point outside the property plane. Venues regulated by the Commission must also stay within **low-frequency ambient + 8 dBC**. Inside a dwelling on the same multi-unit residential property, the limit is **ambient + 5 dBA**, measured 3 ft from any wall with windows closed. Fixed sources may not exceed 45 dBA (10 pm to 7 am) or 55 dBA in sleeping or living rooms. The ambient is never taken as below 35 dBA indoors or 45 dBA elsewhere ([Art. 29 text, sf.gov 2023 guidelines](https://www.sf.gov/sites/default/files/2024-02/21_CACOSF_2023_Article29RegulationofNoiseGuidelines.pdf)). The 2025 ordinance below says it "will align the Police Code with modern sound measurement standards," so check whether section 2909 was changed ([OSB review of File 250537](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/250537_Leg_Review.pdf)).
- ABC conditions in SF now tie noise to the entertainment permit, so a Commission sound violation is also an ABC violation ([Res. 459-26](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/r0459-26.pdf)).

### Reforms in 2025 and 2026

- **PermitSF nighttime and entertainment ordinance (File 250537), effective 2025-08-16:** POE and Extended Hours permits no longer go to DBI, and LLP and Fixed Place Amplified Sound permits no longer go to Planning. It also removes redundant noticing ([sf.gov PermitSF](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-permitsf-legislation); [OSB review](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/250537_Leg_Review.pdf)).
- **Entertainment zones:** Cole Valley passed on 2025-02-07. Mayor Lurie then signed five more, for example Valencia between 16th and 21st, Folsom between 7th and 8th, and Yerba Buena Lane, and launched Castro and Upper Market ([sf.gov](https://www.sf.gov/news-mayor-lurie-signs-legislation-creating-five-new-entertainment-zones-across-san-francisco-launches-castro-entertainment-zone)). The zones cover outdoor and street events and open containers. They do not replace a venue's own permit (inference).
- **Vacant to Vibrant:** downtown pop-ups with subsidised rent. Thirty storefronts have been activated since 2023, and the first 2026 openings were announced in March 2026 ([sf.gov](https://www.sf.gov/news-mayor-lurie-welcomes-four-new-small-businesses-to-union-square-and-downtown); [Local News Matters, 2026-03-16](https://localnewsmatters.org/2026/03/16/bcn-22-sf-pop-ups-march-15-2026/)). It could host a pop-up trial, but a pop-up LLP needs a Planning temporary-use authorization and lasts 60 days, renewable once ([sf.gov](https://sf.gov/get-limited-live-performance-permit)).

## 3. SF Planning

- **Use category:** "Bar" is a Retail Sales and Service use for drinking on the premises "where no person under 21 years of age is admitted," with ABC licence types **23, 42, 48 or 61**. Music venues with a Type 90 licence are excluded ([Planning Code 102, gocodebook 2025](https://gocodebook.com/library/us/ca/san-francisco-planning/article-1-general-zoning-provisions/sec.-102.-definitions)). A Type 47 operation is therefore a Restaurant, not a Bar **(inference from the licence list)**. Retail of design objects is a separate accessory or retail use. "Nighttime Entertainment" means dance halls, discotheques and nightclubs ([same](https://gocodebook.com/library/us/ca/san-francisco-planning/article-1-general-zoning-provisions/sec.-102.-definitions)). A seated listening bar should stay a Bar plus an entertainment permit, not Nighttime Entertainment (inference).
- **District controls (ground floor), from the zoning tables (gocodebook mirror of the 2025 code):**

| District | Bar | Restaurant | Nighttime Entertainment | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NC-3 (moderate scale) | **P** | P | P | No limit ([sec. 712](https://gocodebook.com/library/us/ca/san-francisco-planning/article-7-neighborhood-commercial-districts/sec.-712.-neighborhood-commercial,-moderate-scale-district-nc-3)) |
| Hayes-Gough NCT | **P** | P | C | P 6 am to 2 am ([sec. 761](https://gocodebook.com/library/us/ca/san-francisco-planning/article-7-neighborhood-commercial-districts/sec.-761.-hayes-gough-neighborhood-commercial-transit-district)) |
| Valencia Street NCT | **C** | P | C | P 6 am to 2 am ([sec. 762](https://gocodebook.com/library/us/ca/san-francisco-planning/article-7-neighborhood-commercial-districts/sec.-762.-valencia-street-neighborhood-commercial-transit-district)) |
| SoMa NCT | **C** | P | n/a | P 6 am to 2 am ([sec. 753](https://gocodebook.com/library/us/ca/san-francisco-planning/article-7-neighborhood-commercial-districts/sec.-753.-soma-neighborhood-commercial-transit-district)) |
| North Beach NCD | **C**, and only in a space last legally used as a Bar | C, with the same rule | C | NP 2 am to 6 am ([sec. 722](https://gocodebook.com/library/us/ca/san-francisco-planning/article-7-neighborhood-commercial-districts/sec.-722.-north-beach-neighborhood-commercial-district)) |

- **Mission Alcoholic Beverage SUD** (roughly 14th St to Cesar Chavez, Guerrero to Potrero, which includes most of Valencia): a new Bar is a **Conditional Use** and "shall use a liquor license transferred from another Bar from within the SUD" ([sec. 249.60](https://gocodebook.com/library/us/ca/san-francisco-planning/article-2-use-districts/sec.-249.60.-mission-alcoholic-beverage-special-use-district)). That cuts the pool of licences a buyer can use there (inference). A 2025 proposal (File 250682) would principally permit a Bar that replaces a Bar or Restaurant in North Beach. Its final status was not confirmed ([OSB review](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/250682_Leg_Review_.pdf)).
- **Dogpatch and UMU** controls were not verified in this round. Check Planning Code sec. 843 before choosing a site there.
- **Notice:** Prop H (November 2020) removed neighbourhood notification for most principally permitted storefront changes in NC districts and set a target of 30 days for small-business permits. The Mission St, 24th St-Mission and SoMa NCT controls were not amended ([SF Planning](https://sfplanning.org/save-our-small-businesses-initiative)). The 2023 package (approved 2023-12-06) lifted bar restrictions on Haight, Sacramento and Union streets, dropped change-of-use notice in the Eastern Neighborhoods mixed-use districts, and put bars and full-liquor restaurants into the expedited Community Benefit Priority Processing for Conditional Use ([sf.gov](https://sf.gov/news/mayor-london-breeds-small-business-reforms-approved-board-supervisors)). A Conditional Use still carries its own hearing notice (inference).
- **PermitSF priority processing (File 250538, effective 2025-08-16):** Conditional Use hearings are guaranteed **within 90 days of a completed and accepted application** ([sf.gov PermitSF](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-permitsf-legislation)). The mayor's office said a Conditional Use could previously add up to 10 months and $150,000 ([SF Examiner; figure from a search summary, not re-read](https://www.sfexaminer.com/news/business/city-pols-work-to-loosen-rules-to-fill-vacant-storefronts/article_7817e858-f0ed-4dfb-8d65-18618caa1d49.html)).
- **Cost:** the Planning fee calculator shows a flat **$3,147** for a Conditional Use with no construction cost (page undated). The FY 2025-26 schedule took effect 2025-08-29 ([SF Planning fee calculator](https://feecalculator.sfplanning.org/); [SF Planning news](https://sfplanning.org/news/new-fee-schedule-now-effect)). With a land-use consultant or attorney, a contested Conditional Use realistically costs $15,000 to $40,000 (inference).
- **Timeline:** where Bar is P, Planning reviews the change of use within the building permit, with a 30-day target. Where it is C, expect **4 to 6 months** (inference: 1 to 2 months to a complete application, then up to 90 days to the hearing, then the appeal period).

## 4. Health, fire, building and plumbing code

### Department of Public Health (Environmental Health, FY 2026-27, revised 2026-09-01)

- **Plan check:** Category 2, "Prepackaged food facilities/bar/lounges," **$1,241**. Category 3, restaurants with food preparation, is billed as an 8-hour review at $276 an hour (about $2,208, inference) ([DPH fee schedule](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/FY27_Environmental_Health_Fees_9-1-26.pdf)).
- **Permit-to-operate application, including the first inspection: $470.** Zoning referral $196 and fire referral $156 ([same](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/FY27_Environmental_Health_Fees_9-1-26.pdf)).
- **Annual licence:** "Bars / Taverns (with or without food prep)" show **$0** for 2026-27 ([same](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/FY27_Environmental_Health_Fees_9-1-26.pdf)). This matches Prop M (2024), which waived 49 annual licence fees covering 87% of bars and nightclubs ([CBS SF](https://www.cbsnews.com/sanfrancisco/news/san-francisco-supervisors-eliminate-annual-license-fees-for-restaurants-bars); [SF Treasurer](https://sftreasurer.org/prop-m-2024-business-tax-reform)).
- **Timeline:** state law requires plans to be approved or rejected within **20 working days** ([H&S Code 114380](https://california.public.law/codes/ca_health_and_safety_code_section_114380)). DPH asks for the permit application **at least 1 month before opening** ([DPH application](https://sf.gov/sites/default/files/2023-07/PlanCheckApplication.pdf)).

### Fire Department

- **Place of Assembly permit** is required at a capacity of **50 or more** ([sf.gov](https://sf.gov/get-entertainment-permit-your-indoor-event); [sf.gov LLP page](https://sf.gov/get-limited-live-performance-permit)). The fee is **$446**, and the premises must be inspected before the permit issues ([SFFD permits](https://sf-fire.org/permits)).
- Fire plan review of the tenant improvement goes through the building permit. SFFD does not publish fees or timelines on the permits page ([SFFD](https://sf-fire.org/permits)).

### Building code, 2025 CBC, effective in SF from 2026-01-01

All filings from 2026-01-01 use the 2025 California codes with SF amendments, with no grace period ([sf.gov](https://www.sf.gov/resource--2025--current-san-francisco-building-codes)).

- **Sprinklers (903.2.1.2):** required for an A-2 fire area that exceeds **5,000 sq ft**, or has an occupant load of **100 or more**, or is on a floor other than the level of exit discharge ([Building Code Trainer](https://buildingcodetrainer.com/?p=2596); [Building Code Forum quoting IBC](https://www.thebuildingcodeforum.com/forum/threads/903-2-1-2-group-a-2-sprinkler-required.12231/)). The State Fire Marshal has confirmed that a change from M to A-2 with an occupant load over 100 must sprinkler ([SFM interpretation 18-007](https://34c031f8-c9fd-4018-8c5a-4159cdff6b0d-cdn-endpoint.azureedge.net/-/media/osfm-website/what-we-do/code-development-and-analysis/code-interpretation/18-007_change_occupancy_exisiting_bldg.pdf)). **At 2,500 sq ft on the ground floor and 99 occupants, no sprinkler system is triggered.** One more occupant triggers it (inference).
- **Occupant load factors (Table 1004.5):** unconcentrated assembly (tables and chairs) **15 sq ft net**, standing space **5 sq ft net**, concentrated chairs only 7 sq ft net ([I Dig Hardware](https://idighardware.com/2025/04/decoded-calculating-the-occupant-load-2/)). Booths and bar stools count by seats (fixed seating, section 1004.6) (inference).
  - **Check (inference):** 2,500 gross minus about 600 sq ft of back bar, storage, toilets and retail leaves about 1,900 sq ft net of tables, which gives about 127 occupants. To stay at 99 or fewer, the floor needs fixed seats (booths, banquettes, bar stools) and little open standing area, and the 99 must be the calculated load, not just a posted sign.
- **Exits:** one exit is allowed up to **49** occupants in Group A, with a 75 ft common path in the model IBC Table 1006.2.1 ([Open Exam Prep](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/are-pa/ch06-egress-life-safety/exits-travel-distance); not re-read in the 2025 CBC). At 80 to 99 occupants, **two exits** are needed.
- **Panic hardware (1010.2.9):** required on latched or locked egress doors serving Group A spaces of **50 or more** ([I Dig Hardware](https://idighardware.com/2022/12/panic-hardware-requirements-follow-up/)).

### Plumbing fixtures (California Plumbing Code 422)

- The occupant load comes from the CBC (Table 1004.5). Plumbing Table 4-1 applies only to state buildings and schools ([CBSC Bulletin 24-03, 2024-10-11](https://dgs.ca.gov/-/media/Divisions/BSC/06-News/Information-Bulletins/2024/BSC-Bulletin-24-03-CPC-Plumbing-Fixtures.pdf)). The count assumes 50% male and 50% female, and fractions round up ([CPC 422.1, 2025](https://gocodebook.com/library/us/ca/ca-plumbing-code/chapter-4-plumbing-fixtures-and-fixture-fittings/422.1-fixture-count)).
- **A-2 "restaurants, pubs, lounges, nightclubs":**
  - Water closets: male 1 for 1 to 50, 2 for 51 to 150. Female 1 for 1 to 25, 2 for 26 to 50, 3 for 51 to 100.
  - Urinals: 1 per 200 males.
  - Lavatories: 1 for 1 to 150 of each sex.
  - Sources: [search summary of gocodebook Table 422.1](https://gocodebook.com/library/us/ca/ca-plumbing-code/e/2022/chapter-4-plumbing-fixtures-and-fixture-fittings/422.1-fixture-count); [San Diego TB PLMB 4-1, 2023, worked A-2 example](https://www.sandiego.gov/sites/default/files/dsdplmb-4-1.pdf).
- **For 99 occupants (50 M / 50 F):** 1 male WC, 2 female WC, 1 urinal (0.25, rounded up), 1 lavatory each **(inference from the bands)**. That is about 4 fixtures in total. Drinking fountain and service sink: the model table typically requires 1 service sink, and a drinking fountain that a bar can usually meet with water service. **Not verified for the 2025 CPC; confirm with DBI (inference).**
- **All-gender:** for all-gender toilet facilities, the minimum is the aggregate of the male and female counts ([CPC 422.1.1](https://gocodebook.com/us/california/california-plumbing-code/plumbing-fixtures-and-fixture-fittings/fixture-counts-accessibility-and-specialties)). Multi-user rooms may serve both sexes when water closets are in privacy compartments and urinals are screened (422.2 exception 4). Single-use rooms must be signed for either sex (422.2.1) ([CPC 422.2](https://gocodebook.com/library/us/ca/ca-plumbing-code/chapter-4-plumbing-fixtures-and-fixture-fittings/422.2-separate-facilities)). Under AB 1732 (H&S Code 118600), every single-user toilet in a business must be signed all-gender ([enrolled AB 1732](https://www.leginfo.ca.gov/pub/15-16/bill/asm/ab_1701-1750/ab_1732_bill_20160823_enrolled.htm)). So **3 or 4 single-user all-gender rooms** (one with a urinal) would meet the count (inference).

### Accessibility (CBC 11B, 2025)

- **5%** of seating and standing spaces at dining surfaces, including bars, must comply with 11B-902 and be dispersed through the space ([11B-226.1 and 226.2, DSA 2025 guide](https://www.corada.com/documents/2025CBCPG/division-2-scoping-requirements)).
- **Bar counters over 34 in:** where food or drink is served at a counter over 34 in, a **60 in minimum** section of the main counter must comply with 11B-902.3 ([11B-226.3](https://www.corada.com/documents/2025CBCPG/division-2-scoping-requirements)). That section must be **28 to 34 in** high with knee and toe clearance ([11B-902, DSA 2025 guide](https://www.corada.com/documents/2025CBCPG/division-9-built-in-elements)).
- **Accessible route:** 36 in minimum clear width, the standard 11B-403.5.1 figure; this was not re-read for the 2025 edition **(inference)**.

## 6. Other

- **Business registration:** the 2026-27 fee for $0 to $100,000 of SF gross receipts is **$55 plus a $4 state fee** ([SF Treasurer](https://sftreasurer.org/business/register-business)). It is waived in year one under First Year Free (below).
- **Seller's permit (CDTFA):** **no charge**, but a security deposit may be required, with a $2,000 minimum when one is set ([CDTFA](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/taxes-and-fees/faqseller.htm); [CDTFA manual](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/taxes-and-fees/manuals/cpm-04.pdf)).
- **Gross receipts tax:** businesses with **$5 million or less** in SF gross receipts are exempt under Prop M (2024) ([SF Treasurer](https://sftreasurer.org/prop-m-2024-business-tax-reform)). Proposition C on the 2026-11-03 ballot would raise the exemption to $7.5 million ([KALW, 2026-05-14](https://www.kalw.org/bay-area-news/2026-05-14/san-franciscos-proposition-c-explained)).
- **Music licences:** BMI starts at "a little more than a dollar a day," scaled by occupancy and by live, DJ or recorded music ([BMI](https://www.bmi.com/licensing/entry/bars_and_restaurants); [IBJ/Bloomberg, 2025-07-09](https://www.ibj.com/articles/restaurants-bars-say-theyre-getting-squeezed-by-rising-music-licensing-costs)). ASCAP's minimum is about $360 a year, with recorded music at about $2.66 to $4.01 per occupant (2023 guide) ([The Pricer](https://www.thepricer.org/?p=23157)). Vendor guides put SESAC at about $334 a year, with GMR negotiated per venue; these figures were not verified ([SpotOn](https://www.spoton.com/blog/music-for-restaurants-bars-legally-licensing-streaming/)). The National Restaurant Association reports an average of **$4,500 a year** for all music licences ([IBJ/Bloomberg](https://www.ibj.com/articles/restaurants-bars-say-theyre-getting-squeezed-by-rising-music-licensing-costs)). For about 99 occupants with a DJ as the core offer, expect **$2,500 to $6,000 a year across all four** (inference).
- **Minimum wage:** **$19.61 an hour from 2026-07-01** ($19.18 from 2025-07-01) ([OLSE](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/2026_Historical_San_Francisco_Minimum_Wage_Rates_M9ynRNo.pdf)).
- **Health Care Security Ordinance 2026:** **$2.74 per hour payable** for employers with 20 to 99 workers and $4.11 for 100 or more. Employers with 0 to 19 are exempt. It applies to employees after 90 days who work at least 8 hours a week ([Newfront](https://www.newfront.com/blog/the-2026-san-francisco-hcso-expenditure-rates)).
- **First Year Free:** extended by Ord. 134-26 **through 2027-06-30** (or until its funds run out). It waives permit fees, initial licence fees and the first business registration fee for new businesses with $5 million or less in SF gross receipts at a commercial location. The waiver applies to applications filed within one year of starting business, across city departments including Planning, DBI, Fire, DPH, Police and the Entertainment Commission. It does not cover fees collected for state agencies such as ABC ([Ord. 134-26](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/o0134-26.pdf)). Registering the business late, close to filing the permits, makes the one-year window cover more of them **(inference)**.

## Recommended path (inference throughout, built on the facts above)

**Licence: buy a Type 48 on the transfer market.** At $70,000 to $150,000 this is the cheapest it has been in at least a decade ([NBC 2026](https://www.nbcbayarea.com/news/local/liquor-license-prices-san-francisco/4087673/)). It matches a snacks-only concept and carries no meal-service risk. Accept 21-and-over, retail included. Choose a Type 47 only if the concept grows a real kitchen and needs minors on site or a Restaurant-zoned site. If so, the ABC price is the same, the health plan check is about $1,000 more, the kitchen build adds roughly $50,000 to $150,000, and PCN is decided by ABC rather than the Board of Supervisors.

**Site screen first**, because it drives everything else:
1. Bar is P on the ground floor: NC-3, Hayes-Gough NCT. Avoid C districts and the Mission SUD unless the site was last legally used as a bar.
2. The space must fit **99 or fewer by calculated load**, with mostly fixed seating, which avoids sprinklers.
3. Look for an existing bar or restaurant space with two exits, toilets and a grease interceptor, which cuts both the TI scope and the fees.
4. Avoid shared walls with dwellings. Sound isolation sets both the budget and the permit's dB limit.

**Critical path (months from a signed lease):**

| Months | Work |
|---|---|
| 0 to 1 | Register the business, which starts First Year Free. Intake email to the Entertainment Commission. Architect starts the TI drawings. Negotiate the Type 48 and open escrow. |
| 1 to 4 | DBI permit review (Planning reviews in the same permit where Bar is P). DPH plan check runs in parallel (20 working days). File the ABC transfer when the premises address is fixed. The 30-day posting and 500 ft mailing run in this window. PCN letter to the Board of Supervisors, heard within 90 days. |
| 3 to 4 | File the POE so its 30-day notice and hearing (2 to 3 months) end with construction. |
| 4 to 8 | Construction and sound isolation. Commission sound test. Final inspections by DBI, SFFD (Place of Assembly) and DPH (permit to operate). ABC issues once the premises is final. Escrow closes. |
| **About 8** | **Opening, where Bar is P.** |
| **About 11 to 12** | **Opening, where a Conditional Use is needed** (add 3 to 4 months before the DBI permit can issue). |

The longest-lead items are the DBI permit and the construction. ABC and the Board PCN (3 to 5 months) and the POE (2 to 3 months) fit inside that window if they are filed by month 1 and month 3.
