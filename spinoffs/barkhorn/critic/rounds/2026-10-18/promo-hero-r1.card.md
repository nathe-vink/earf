# Card: h1

What it is for: the home page's hero image, full width at the top of the brand's site: the whole range at once. The
eight colours of one loudspeaker stand on a gentle arc in spectrum order (red, orange, golden yellow, green, blue,
violet, water-clear, smoked black), covers off so each woofer shows in its coloured ring, on a pale paper sweep, lit
from behind and low so each tinted acrylic cabinet throws its colour onto the floor in front of it. The site's title
sits over the upper part of the frame, and each speaker in the picture is a link.

Fixed (never yours to change): the speakers themselves, their shape, colours and parts. Each is a 1293 mm tall horn
loudspeaker: a cabinet of 20 mm tinted cast acrylic, lined in white, under a horn of tinted resin, sold at about
$20,000 a pair.

May change: the set (sweep colour, floor, what surrounds them), the light (direction, size, number, colour), the camera
(height, distance, lens, framing), the arrangement (spacing, arc, turn of each speaker) and the finish (grade,
contrast). The frame is 2560 x 1280.
