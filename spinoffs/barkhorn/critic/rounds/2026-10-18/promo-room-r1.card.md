# Card: r1

What it is for: the scale image on the brand's site and in its look book: a pair of green speakers in a living room,
so a visitor sees how big they are (each 1293 mm tall, 560 mm wide) and how they live at home. It sits under the
heading "A pair of Poison."

Fixed (never yours to change): the speakers themselves, their shape, colour and parts: horn loudspeakers with cabinets
of 20 mm tinted cast acrylic, lined in white, under horns of tinted resin, covers on (green cloth discs), sold at about
$20,000 a pair.

May change: everything else: the room (architecture, walls, floor, windows), the furniture and objects in it and their
materials, the light (time of day, windows, lamps), the camera (height, lens, framing) and the finish. The frame is
2880 x 1620.
