# Card: the EARF look book

**What it is.** A short deck the owner shares with friends, makers and possible partners to show a line of floor
standing horn loudspeakers. It is read on a screen (1920 x 1080 pages), one page at a time.

**What each page is for.**

- The cover: the brand (EARF, its logo: the globe beside the name drawn in the marks' shapes) and what it makes, and the
  line at a glance (eleven marks by line).
- A divider for each line (Bark, Cloud, Crag): the line's name, one sentence on how its speakers are made, its marks.
- A page for each speaker: its mark, its line and number, its name, four facts (surface, cabinet, horn, cover), and
  two photographs, the cover on and the cover off, side by side.
- Two pages of the horn, from the side and from behind, on one speaker of each line.
- The specification beside the general arrangement drawings, then the section and the parts drawn apart.

**Fixed (not yours to change).** The speakers and every render of them; their names, materials and colours; the three
lines and their names; the marks (each line's shape, each speaker's colour) and their acrylic drawing; the brand's
name (EARF), its globe and its wordmark (the letters drawn in the marks' shapes); the drawings. The owner chose these.

**Yours to change.** Layout, sizes and spacing, the type's sizes and weights, the order within a page, the copy's
wording (keeping its facts), the page backgrounds behind the type, how the photographs are cropped and placed, and
anything missing that a reader needs.

**The type.** Instrument Sans (headings and copy) and IBM Plex Mono (labels), both loaded from Google Fonts.
