The globe, as it draws itself

Five globes, each drawn by one dot in one unbroken line at one steady speed. Every stroke belongs to a circle, and the last frame is the logo, so the globe and its ident are one thing.

      One line

      Where two circles cross, the dot veers through a turn too short to see. Where it must reach another circle, it runs on along a line it has already drawn. So it never stops, never turns a corner, and never leaves a stroke outside a circle.

      One speed

      The dot keeps one speed the whole way, measured along the line. It speeds up once as it starts and slows once as it ends.

      Lines and filled

      Lines: each circle drawn in its Cloud's colour. Filled: each circle's tint sweeps in behind the dot. On paper the tints multiply where they overlap, as tinted acrylic does. As light, the colours add towards white.
