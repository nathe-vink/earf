The ident

The globe drawn as the eight Clouds. A pen traces a ring of circles, each taking a speaker's colour as it closes, and the larger circle forms between them. Then the name.

    The wheel

    The colours

    Each circle is a Cloud's tint, and where two overlap the tints multiply, as two sheets of tinted acrylic do laid one on the other. Fog's white frosts what lies under it.

    The pen

    It draws each circle from the point where it touches the rim, clockwise, then glides along the rim to the next. The glides draw the larger circle. It finishes at the centre, as a record's spindle.
