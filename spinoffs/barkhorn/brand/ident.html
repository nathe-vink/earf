The ident

The globe drawn as the eight Clouds: eight circles through one centre, each in its speaker's acrylic tint. Then the name. Choose how the wheel comes on, and on what ground.

    Pen

    A dot draws each circle at one steady speed, from where the circle meets the wheel's edge, and the circle's tint sweeps in behind it. Between circles it swings along that edge to the next, drawing nothing, so it never stops or turns a corner.

    Bloom

    The eight circles start stacked as one, grown from a dot, and part in one turn. Stacked, the tints multiply to nearly black, as eight sheets of the acrylic would. As light, they add to white.

    Paper and light

    On paper, the tints multiply where circles overlap, and Fog's white frosts what lies under it. As light, the colours add towards white, and Smoke's black shades.
