# The debut night: lighting a bar with Hemisphere and Core (round 43, 2026-10-31)

A plan for the lamps at EARF's debut night, sized from the fab kits and the first-articles plan. Mantle plays at the
bar. This is a working plan for the owner to adjust to the room once it's chosen.

## What goes where

| Where | What | How many | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Over the bar | Hemisphere, rim 750 above the counter, 900 apart | 6 over a 5.4 m counter | About 500 lux on the counter under each (round 42's 590 lm at the COB); the counter reads as one glowing line. |
| Tables | Core, one a table | 10 (two-tops and four-tops) | The ball is the table's candle: the light on faces, low, from below. |
| The record player | Mantle on the back bar, a Core beside it | 1 + 1 | Mantle's base glows in its tint. A Core in the same tint beside it ties them. |
| The door | One Hemisphere and one Core in Smoke, off, on a plinth | 1 + 1 | The "off" look, so people see both states. |

**Tints:** a run along the bar in the speakers' order (Ember, Dust, Acid, Poison, Ice, Haze), then Fog and Smoke on
the tables. Or one tint for the night (Ember), if the room is busy. Either works, because the shades swap by hand in a
minute.

## Power

- **Hemisphere:**
  - **plug-in swag** (the proposed first listing): each pendant's listed Class 2 adapter at an outlet, or one power
    strip per three;
  - **hardwired:** an electrician hangs the canopies on the room's boxes.

  The draw is 4.4 W at the COB, about 5.5 W at the wall, so **33 W for six**. The dimmer is the low-voltage in-line
  dimmer on each cord, or one phase-cut wall dimmer if hardwired.
- **Core:** about 4.7 h at full brightness on cells at the 90 % limit, and about 5.8 h at the bar setting (2 W).
  - Doors at 7 and close at 1 is 6 h. **Run at the bar setting from full charge (6.5 h), or keep a spare set of cells
    charging behind the bar.** The cells are standard LiFePO4 18650s behind four screws, and a swap takes about two
    minutes with a driver.
  - Twelve spare cells (four lamps' worth) and a four-bay LiFePO4 charger cost about $60 to $80 [estimate].
  - For a long event, run the tables nearest the walls on their USB-C cables.

The "then" figures are each unit's BOM with the mould cost (Hemisphere's $3,900, Core's $1,500) taken out. They are a
one-off shop's rates; RFQ quotes at 10 will be lower.

**Cheaper night:** 4 Hemispheres and 8 Cores cuts it to about **$16,200**. Fewer than that and the bar stops reading
as a line.
