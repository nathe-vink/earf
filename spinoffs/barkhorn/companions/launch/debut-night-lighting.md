# The debut night: lighting a hired gallery room with Hemisphere and Core (round 43, revised 2026-10-31)

The debut has moved from a bar of our own to a hired or lent room at or near Fort Mason: **Tuesday 19 January 2027**, just before FOG Design+Art (21–24 January). The
bar lane is pricing rooms such as the Firehouse. Until the room is chosen, this plan assumes a **generic gallery-type
room for about 120 people standing**:
- about 150–200 m² of open floor;
- white walls and a hard floor;
- a ceiling of 3.5–5 m with the house's own track lights;
- ordinary 120 V outlets on the walls;
- **no ceiling feed we may use, and no drilling.**

Mantle plays at its own station, and Tropos has its own lane's station.

Every cost here is an **estimate [E]**; quotes and the room's own rules replace them. Lamp counts are on the moulds
already paid for (`first-articles.md`, cash table): each extra Hemisphere is its BOM less the moulds, about **$672**,
and each extra Core about **$527**.

### Full: the room lit by the lamps

| Where | What | How many | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The long table** (about 6 m, the room's spine; drinks or the products) | Hemisphere in a line, rims 750 above the top, about 1 m apart | 6 | About 500 lux on the table under each (590 lm at the COB). The line of shades is the picture people take. |
| **High-top tables** (cocktail tables the room or the caterer supplies) | Core, one a table | 8 | The ball is the table's candle: it lights faces from below. |
| **The product plinths** (Mantle, Tropos) | a Core beside each | 2 | It ties the stations to the lamps, in the same tint. |
| **The door** | one Hemisphere and one Core in Smoke, **off**, on a plinth | 1 + 1 | The "off" look, so people see both states; the price card here. |

**Tints:** along the table in the speakers' order (Ember, Dust, Acid, Poison, Ice, Haze), then Fog and Smoke on the high
tops. Or all Ember, if the room is busy. Shades swap by hand in a minute, which is worth a demonstration at the door.

**The house lights:** ask the room to dim its track to about 20–30 % and keep two heads on the plinths. Hemisphere at
590 lm lights a table, not a gallery. The room reads right when our lamps are the brightest things in it.

### Middle: one strong table

Three Hemispheres over a 3 m table, still at about 1 m apart, plus one at the door. Six Cores: four high tops and the two
stations. It still reads as a line, but only just. Below three, the shades stop reading as a line.

### No extra lamps: a single island

Only the first articles (one Hemisphere in Smoke with a spare Fog shade, one Core, Mantle):
- **one island**, a 1.2 m table or a plinth in the middle of the room;
- Hemisphere hung over it from a single light stand with a boom arm, its rim 750 over the top;
- Core and Mantle under it, the room's track dimmed around the island;
- the **shade swap** shown by hand: Smoke to Fog and back;
- the rest of the room lit by the house as a gallery.

This costs only the stand and sandbag and is honest about where the company is. The pictures from it are close ones,
not the long line.

## Hanging, with no ceiling feed: the plug-in swag

A hired room gives no junction boxes, and most forbid drilling. **The plug-in swag is the only version that hangs here**:
the hardwired canopy needs a box and an electrician. This settles the debut whichever version the owner lists first.

**The rig (Full and Middle):**
- a **freestanding "goalpost"** over the long table:
  - two black wind-up light stands, about 3–3.5 m;
  - a 6 m (or 3 m) black aluminium tube or a box truss across them;
  - each stand's base on two 25 lb sandbags;
- each Hemisphere hangs from the tube by its **steel suspension cable** on a clamp hook, the cable carrying the weight
  (1.6 kg) and the cord carrying only current (`certification.md`);
- the cords are swagged along the tube, gathered, and dropped down one stand to the power strip at its foot.

| | Rent (a night) | Buy |
|---|---|---|
| Two stands and a 6 m truss or pipe, sandbags | about $150–400 [E] (a theatrical or AV rental house) | about $400–700 [E] |
| Clamp hooks or beam clamps, 6 | | about $60 [E] |
| Gaffer tape, cable ramp, black drape for the stand feet | | about $60–100 [E] |

**If the room has its own system** (a gallery picture rail, a ceiling track for hanging, or a pipe grid), hang from
that and skip the goalpost. Ask the room for its rigging rules, and whether a rigger must sign off on anything over
heads. Keep the line over the table, not over a walkway. Each pendant hangs on its steel cable, never on its cord.

**No extra lamps:** one stand with a boom arm (about $100–200 [E] to buy, less to rent) and a sandbag.

## Power

- **Hemisphere (plug-in):** each pendant's listed Class 2 adapter, about **5.5 W at the wall**, so **33 W for six**:
  - one power strip at the goalpost's foot, on one ordinary 15 A outlet;
  - each cord's in-line low-voltage dimmer sets the line by eye.
  - Run the one supply lead along the wall or under a cable ramp; nothing loose on the floor where 120 people walk.
- **Core:** cordless on the night. The event runs 6:30 to 10:00 pm (the brand lane's run-of-show), 3.5 hours, plus
  the afternoon's setting up:
  - about 4.7 h at full brightness (cells at the 90 % limit), about 5.8 h at the event setting (2 W);
  - so **charge full that afternoon and run at the event setting**;
  - keep **one spare set of cells per four lamps** charged behind the table (twelve spare cells and a four-bay LiFePO4
    charger, about **$60–80** [E]). A swap is four screws and about two minutes.
  - The two at the stations can run on their USB-C cables if an outlet is near.
- **Total draw** for the lamps: under 100 W including the chargers. Lighting asks nothing of the room's power beyond
  two outlets.

## "Lights" at 8:45 pm

The run-of-show (`r47-brand`, `brandbook/photo/RUN-OF-SHOW.md`, 93132fda) turns every house light off for ten minutes
at 8:45, so only the lamps light the room and the pair is lit from below. It's the picture of the night. For the
lamps:
- **Full:** the line of shades and the high-top Cores carry the room.
- **Middle:** the table carries it.
- **None:** the island alone, in the dark. It's intimate, not a room shot.

**Before 8:45:**
- every Core at the event setting with at least half its charge (they have run 2.25 h by then, of about 5.8);
- the pendants' in-line dimmers at full for those ten minutes;
- the room's technician knows which circuits are the house lights and which strip feeds the rig, so the rig stays on.
