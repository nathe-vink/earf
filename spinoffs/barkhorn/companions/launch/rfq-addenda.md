# RFQ addenda: stock colours for the prototypes (round 43, 2026-10-31)

One paragraph per quote request, plain text, to paste into the drafts already written (the coordinator places them; nothing
here has been sent). The picks are `stock-colours.md`'s: AC Plastics TruCast cell-cast sheet, Ember as **2423 Red**, Smoke as
**2064 Gray** (or **2074 Dark Gray** for Core if the chips show 2064 too pale at 3/8"). Imperial stock, so 3/8" (9.5 mm)
stands for our 10 mm and 1/2" for half of our 25 mm. The custom tints stay the production plan; these ask for a second
price alongside it.
